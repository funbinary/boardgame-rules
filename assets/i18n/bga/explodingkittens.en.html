These latest rules for example do not include the 5 card combo.

Overview

Be the last player left after all others are eliminated.

Turn

On your turn, you can play no cards or as many as you wish.

Then, draw 1 card. If this is an Exploding Kitten and you cannot counter it in any way, you are out!

Special Combos

If you Play two cards of the same name, you can take a random card from someone else.

If you Play three cards of the same name, you can ask for a particular card, but get nothing if that player does not have it.
