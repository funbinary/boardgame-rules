Overview

The game plays over a series of turns, until the game end is triggered in one of three ways.

  • If an HQ is captured by a Troop, the capturing player immediately wins.
  • If through region control a player gains a certain number of medals as defined by the map, that player immediately wins.
  • If a player cannot perform any more actions (e.g. they have no tiles to draw and cannot play any tiles), then the game ends. In this case, the player with the most medals wins. In the case of a tie, the player who triggered this end game condition loses.

Each player has 24 troops--8 types with 3 of each. During set up, 4 troops are randomly removed from each player's reserve without the players knowing which tiles are removed. The removed tiles are likely different between the two players, creating a mysterious asymmetry.

Your Turn

You can either:

Draw 2 Troops from your reserve and place them on your rack. Your rack can hold up to 8 Troops maximum

or

Play 1 troop from your rack onto a space on the battlefield. You can place it anywhere that fulfills the following conditions:

  • An empty base that is connected to your HQ (this means you must always follow a continuous path from your HQ (starting point) to the troop you are placing, passing only through bases you occupy)
  • A base occupied by any of your troops
  • A base occupied by an enemy troop with a lower strength than your troop
  • Your opponent's HQ. (You cannot place a troop on your own HQ)

Each troop has an effect that triggers after placement.

If the troop was placed on a special base, you first fully complete the troop's effect before trigger the effect of the special base.

If you control a region by occupying all the bases surrounding it, you claim any and all medal(s) currently in that area.

If you would draw a 9th troop, you simply do not draw the troop.
