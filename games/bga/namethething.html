    Name The Thing

    You're a Marketing Think Tank team member, and the Manager needs your team to name all the things!

    Get To it!

    Each Turn someone will be the Manager and pick the best pitch! The last person that's named something will be the first Manager.

    How to play a round

    1. Players draw up to 6 Word cards.
    2. The Manager reads a random Prompt card.
    3. Team Members build a 2-4 Word card Name and place them face down while the Manager looks away.
    4. The Manager reads the Names out loud and picks their favorite.
    5. The winner keeps the Name (Word cards) in their score zone.
    6. The person to the left of the Manager is the new Manager.

    How to win

    Be the first player with 16 words in their score zone!
