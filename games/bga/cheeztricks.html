    Cheez-Tricks

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Cheez-Tricks is a trick-taking game where you should avoid some missions (or try to get them all for more points).

    1. Deck composition: 2 players: 1-7 3 players: 1-9 4 and 5 players: 1-10 All the cards are dealt, except in 2 players where 20 cards are dealt and 8 are removed from the game.
    1. Playing the game: Regular rules for trick-taking game: must follow suit, trump (random on the first round, chose by the player who has the least score on all the others). Highest card of the trump takes the trick; in case of no trumps, highest card of the lead suit takes the trick.
    1. Scoring:
      • Number of tricks x 2 if you have not been captured by any cat.
      • Number of tricks x 1 if you have been captured by 1 cat.
      • Zero points if you have been captured by 2 cats.
      • Number of tricks x 3 if you have been captured by all cats.

    BGA deck by color / cheese:

    Orange = Parmesan

    Red = Swiss

    Green = Roquefort

    Blue = Brie

    After rounds = number of players, the player who has the most points is the winner.

    • The Cats:
      • Black Cat: Capture the player who wins a certain trick.
      • Siamese Cat: Capture the player who wins a certain card.
      • White Cat: Capture the first player who wins a certain amount of tricks
      • Gray Cat: Capture the player that wins the most of a card/suit/tricks by the end of the round.
      • Orange Cat: Capture the player that wins the least of a card/suit/tricks by the end of the round.
      • Tuxedo Cat: Capture the player that wins exactly a certain amount of tricks by the end of the round.
