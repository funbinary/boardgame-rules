    Fairytrails

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.1 · 排名 #6980 · 权重 1.19/5 · 1168人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Gameplay

    Players take turns to play a tile to the table. Whenever you complete a closed network of paths of your colour (pink/yellow), place a stone on every house of your colour that's connected to that network.

    First to place all of their stones wins.

    If all tiles have been placed and no one has placed all of their stones, the player who placed more stones wins.

    Readability tip

    If you try to complete a path, but when you lay down a tile, it doesn't complete, look for other exits for the path. Four-way crossroads that don't have an underpass will extend your roads.
