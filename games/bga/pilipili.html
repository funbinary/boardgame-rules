    Pili Pili Rules Summary

    Goal

    Pilis 🌶️ are points.

    Points are bad, you don't want points.

    The game is over when someone reaches six 🌶️.

    Bill of Materials

    • The shuffled play deck is formed of:
      • 55 numbered cards: 1 to 55, with one of each card. There are no suits.
      • one joker card, wild in rank
    • A shuffled mission deck is formed of 36 mission cards.

    Gameplay

    This is a once-around trick taking card game.

    1. Each hand will be determined by a mission card drawn before the cards are dealt.
    2. Each player receives the number of cards indicated on the mission card.
    3. In order each player bids, the final bidder can't make the bids even to the tricks available.
    4. Each trick is taken by the highest card played. There are no suits.
      1. a joker's value is declared when played; it can be any value 0-56.
    5. After the mission/hand/round is complete, each player receives 🌶️ for the difference between their bid and the number of tricks that took in the hand.
    6. When one player reaches 6 🌶️, the game ends and the player with the fewest 🌶️ wins.
