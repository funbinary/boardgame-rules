    Dragon Castle concise reference by hecose


    Setup:

    • Board – 1 Spirit card, 1 Dragon card, fill countdown tokens according to #players, extras stacked on leftmost space. • Player – 1 shrine.

    Concepts:

    • Available tile = at least one long side not blocked. • Adjacent = orthogonally, not diagonally. Different levels OK.

    Turn:

    • 1 Mandatory Action:

    a) Take 1 tile from top floor, then exact matching tile from any floor.

    b) Take 1 tile from top floor and 1 shrine.

    c) Take 1 tile from top floor, then discard facedown for 1VP.

    d) Summon dragon. Allowed when only floor 1 tiles left. Claim rightmost countdown token (worth 2VP). Once exclamation mark revealed, play until end of round.

    • Put claimed tiles on own board, on free space or facedown tile, never on faceup tile or shrine.

    • If any 4+ adjacent tiles are of same colour, must consolidate by flipping facedown and scoring.

    • When consolidating, may build shrine from own pool onto newly consolidated tiles. Faction(red/green/yellow) tiles allow 1 shrine, Special tiles(blue/black) allow 2, Dragon tile(Purple) also gives 1VP.

    • 1 Optional Action: discard faceup tiles or shrine to activate Spirit power.

    Game end scoring:

    • VP tokens collected
    • Countdown tokens
    • Dragon card objective
    • Shrines score 1/2/3VP at floors 1/2/3+
    • Tiebreaker: most facedown stacks, then shrines

    Other notes:

    • For Spirit cards and Dragon cards, refer pages 12 – 15.
