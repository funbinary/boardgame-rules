    Kingsinthecorner

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 5.3 · 排名 #30827 · 权重 1.13/5 · 629人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Draw a card and then play lower numbers onto opposite colors (reds on blacks and blacks on reds)

    If you have a King, you MAY play it on one of the four corner areas, which then becomes a new pile to play lower numbers of opposite colors onto.

    You may move an entire pile onto another pile if the base card of the moved card is lower and opposite to the top card of the destination pile

    If an orthogonal pile is empty, you may play any card onto it

    If you can't do any of the above, end the turn

    First player to get rid of all their cards wins the round

    Kings score -10 per each, other cards score -1 per each

    SOLO MODE winning conditions - Leave 5 or more cards in the deck to win
