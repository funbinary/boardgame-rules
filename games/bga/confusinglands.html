    Confusinglands

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Build up a landscape whilst making sure to have at least one private objective


    Turn

    You receive two double sided cards, pick one card to keep and choose which side you wish to play it as

    The chosen side must be played at any 90 degree orientation i.e. not at an angle

    Except for the first turn, the new card should cover at least 1 square of a previous card

    The new card CANNOT cover a scoring condition space

    Pass the unchosen card to your opponent and place the new card you each received

    Finally, draw 2 new cards, and repeat the turn


    Terminology

    Touching - Orthogonally adjacent

    Near - Orthogonally or Diagonally adjacent

    Connected - Orthogonally adjacent to other spaces of the same terrain

    Border - The very edge of your display

    Into - Goes from one edge of a space to an orthogonally adjacent edge, but does not have to appear on both spaces

    Past - Is orthogonally adjacent to another feature, but does not meet that feature directly

    X Spaces From - Count the distance Orthogonally


    Game End

    The game ends on the turn in which each player has 8 cards in their display, there will be 1 card left over

    As part of setup, a shared objective was played, this is first scored

    After which, each player scores private objectives created during the game

    Warning: If a player has no Private objectives, they CANNOT score the shared one

    Each private objective included in a player's landscape, regardless of whether it scored or not, lose that player 10 points each

    The player with the most points is the winner!

    If tied, the player with the single highest scoring objective wins!
