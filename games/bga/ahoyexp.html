    Ahoyexp

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    Amass the most Fame in the Caribbean. While the Bluefin Squadron and Mollusk Union battle for regional control, Smugglers deliver cargo to gain wealth and influence.

    The Factions

    • Bluefin Squadron: Military faction. Uses gunboats to patrol regions, build strongholds, and collect taxes.
    • Mollusk Union: Guerrilla faction. Uses hidden currents, spreads propaganda, and leads insurrections to reclaim the seas.
    • Smugglers (Blue/Yellow): Neutral merchants. Deliver cargo between islands to increase wealth and place bets on the war's outcome.

    Gameplay

    The game is played in rounds. On your turn, perform the following steps:

    1. Roll Dice: Roll your 4 action dice.
    2. Take Actions: Place your dice on your player board to activate abilities:
      • Sail: Move your ship across the map. Crossing borders may cost extra movement.
      • Cannons: Attack adjacent ships to damage them or force them to retreat.
      • Recruit: Hire Crew cards to gain permanent bonuses or new action slots.
      • Faction Specific: Sharks build strongholds, Mollusks spread propaganda, Smugglers pick up or deliver cargo.
    3. Explore: When moving off the edge of a tile, draw and place a new region tile.

    Combat & Damage

    • Attacker and defender compare combat strengths (Base + Card bonuses + Die roll).
    • Taking damage adds damage tiles to your board, blocking action slots until repaired.

    Game End & Scoring

    The game ends immediately when a player reaches 30 Fame. Final scoring includes:

    • Regional Control: Sharks and Mollusks score points based on the Wealth value of regions they control.
    • Influence: Smugglers score points based on their influence with factions from successful deliveries.
    • Cards & Rewards: Points from specific Crew cards or treasures.
    • The player with the highest total score wins. In case of a tie, the player with the most gold wins.
