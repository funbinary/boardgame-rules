    Pandaarei

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    The game is played over 2 rounds, each consisting of 11 turns

    Score the animals you collected at the end, but remember some animals score negatively


    Turn

    The player with the starting player token, plays a card, followed by the others

    The highest Unique card wins UNLESS

    There are duplicate cards. Of the players who played one of those duplicates, the one with the most pigs wins. If tied, the one that played the card first wins

    If there are different duplicate card sets, first the duplicate card there is the most of will be judged as above, then if still tied the duplicate group with the highest value will be judged as above

    Regardless of how you win, you gain the starting player token along with two animal cards played this turn of your choice. After which, in the new turn order, the other players pick from the remaining cards played to keep. This means whoever is in last turn order gets no card.

    For the first turn, BGA randomly allocates one of the players the starting player token

    2 Player

    There is a Dummy

    The Dummy cannot win a turn

    As such, the purpose of the Dummy is to provide the chance of a duplicate card for either player to win via that method

    The Dummy always plays last and plays a randomly selected card from its hand


    Game End

    After everyone has played their entire hand once, deal a new hand to everyone

    After everyone has played their second hand, the game ends.

    Rabbits - 1-5 score, 6+ lose 5pts

    Bear - only pairs score, solos score nothing

    Giraffes - each giraffe scores by itself

    Owl - An owl scores nothing, but if you can pair it with a giraffe it scores

    Cat - Cats are multiplied by each other, e.g. 3 cats is 3x3 for 9

    Fox - Foxes only lose points!

    Lions - having no or too few Lions lose points, have to have at least 2 to breakeven

    Pigs - have no score, but give tiebreaking when duplicates are played during a turn

    Pandas - most and least gain and lose points respectively

    Monkey - Wild for Rabbit, Bear, Giraffe, Owl, or Cat (during play its worth is one higher than the previous card played)


    The player with the highest score, wins!
