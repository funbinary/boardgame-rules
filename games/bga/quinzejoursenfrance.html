    Quinzejoursenfrance

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Goal

    Plays in 15 rounds (1 per day)

    Earn the most Victory Points by completing missions and collecting Location cards with smileys.


    Game Play

    Choose one of the following actions:

    1. Voyager – Take 1 Location card, pay steps, gain symbols/reward

    2. Rencontrer – Draw 2 Encounter cards. Discard 1 Encounter card from your hand. You do not have to discard 1 of the 2 you just drew.

    3. Récupérer – Gain 4 Step tokens


    Itinerary Change

    At the start of Days 4, 8, 12 — discard and refresh all 8 Location cards.


    Missions (Rencontre)

    - Score if condition met (e.g., most of a symbol, 3 smiley cards, 6 Nord cards)

    - Fail = 0 pts; Succeed = earn smileys listed


    End Game Scoring

    After Day 15

    1. Gain smileys from Encounter Cards (if the requirements have been met).

    2. Gain smileys from your Location Cards.

    3. +1 bonus per Encounter Card if ALL Encounter Cards are completed

    The player with the most points wins!

    Tiebreakers: most step tokens → shared win if still tied
