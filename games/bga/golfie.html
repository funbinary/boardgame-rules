    Golfie

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Try to gain the lowest score across two rounds


    Turn

    An action card is revealed and all players must perform it

    After which, everyone checks to see if they have a Combo or a Lost Ball


    Action Cards

    Hole - This means you pick a card in the upper row (faceup or facedown) and reveal it before scoring its depicted value. After which, it is discarded, the column it was taken from is shifted up, and a new facedown card is drawn to fill the empty space


    Zone to Play - These cards look like your playing grid with a ball in the space relevant e.g. middle left. After locating the space, each individual player has a choice, you can either flip the card faceup if it were facedown, or you can exchange it with a card orthogonally adjacent to it. You are only forced to exchange if the card in the space has already been revealed. You do not have to exchange like for like, e.g. a faceup or facedown card can be exchanged with either a faceup or facedown card.

    Double Zone - This is like Zone to Play, except there are two spaces which must be actioned

    Golf Cart - This is like Zone to Play, except you can choose a single card from anywhere in your grid

    Lost Ball - Mark that it has come up on your scorecard in the +1 box, then draw again


    Combos

    A Combo is three cards which share the same value and aligned in a straight orthogonal line

    All three cards are removed and scored by placing a mark in the -2 box, then other cards are moved up to replace them before the lower empty spaces are refilled


    Game End

    When the Final Hole card is revealed for the first time, mark your score in the first score box, then play another round

    When the Final Hole card is revealed for the second time, mark your score in the second score box, then add both scores together to make your final score

    The player with the LOWEST score, wins!
