    Kabal — How to Play

    Kabal is a 1-4 player conspiracy-themed deck-building and area-control game. Players lead secret factions competing for global influence through missions, political strength, and hidden allegiances. An AI opponent called the Nemesis adds tension to every game.

    Game Overview

    Each round, players take turns using Action Points (AP) to:

    • Deploy operatives to world locations
    • Complete missions for Victory Points (VP)
    • Buy asset cards to improve their deck
    • Battle other factions for territory
    • Activate psychic heroes (White) or launch covert operations (Black)

    The game ends when the Alpha or Omega track reaches 21, a Level 10 mission is completed, or after 15 rounds. The player with the most total VP wins.

    BASIC GAME

    Setup

    All players start as Black allegiance. On your very first turn (Round 1 only), you may flip to White for free — this also gives you a free End Score Card change. After Round 1, flipping costs $100bn M9.

    Each player receives:

    • A starting deck of 10 asset cards plus an additional 5 random cards
    • A faction with a unique HQ location
    • Starting operatives at their HQ and one world location
    • An End Score Card (secret VP bonus at game end)

    Your Turn

    Each turn follows this sequence:

    1. Turn Summary Popup

    At the start of your turn, you see a summary showing your VPs, allegiance, resources, and the option to Flip or Stay. You can reopen this anytime via the Turn Summary button.

    2. Draw Cards & Collect Income

    Your hand of 5 cards is drawn automatically. Each card generates income:

    • M0 (cash) — used to buy new cards from the Market
    • M9 (billions) — used to fund missions
    • Blue Power — psychic energy for hero activation and special actions
    • Green Power — political influence
    • Red Power — combat tokens for board moves
    • Yellow Power — additional board movement
    • White Power — required for certain missions
    • Wild — can substitute for any token type

    3. Spend Action Points

    You have a base of 1 AP from your HQ, plus bonuses from Antarctica political strength, crystals, artifacts, and blue conversion. Each action costs AP:

    Action AP Cost Description
    Board Action 1 Deploy/move operatives. First HQ deploy is FREE.
    Mission 1 Attempt to complete your active mission.
    Buy 1 Purchase a card from the Market using M0.
    Trash 1 Remove a card from your hand (slim your deck).
    Psychics 1 Gain blue power tokens.
    Battle/Heroes 1 Battle (Black) or activate Heroes (White).

    Free actions (no AP cost):

    • Groups — view and manage your operative groups at locations
    • Market — browse available cards for purchase

    4. End Turn

    Click the large End Turn button when done.

    Missions

    Missions are the primary way to earn VP. Each mission has:

    • Requirements — resources you must spend (Red, Yellow, Blue, White, M9) or conditions you must meet (control regions, own crystals)
    • Rewards — Victory Points + Alpha/Omega track advancement

    Basic missions are straightforward: spend the required resources, earn the VP.

    Level VP Typical Cost
    Level 1 2 VP Low resources
    Level 3 6 VP Medium resources
    Level 5 8 VP High resources
    Level 10 12 VP Very high (ends the game!)

    Board Actions & Combat

    Deploy operatives from your HQ or move between locations:

    • First HQ deploy each turn is FREE (no Red Power needed)
    • Additional moves cost 1 Red Power each
    • Yellow Power enables extra movement capacity

    Battles occur when your operatives share a location with another faction:

    • Combat uses dice + Combat Factor (Operatives=2, Mercenaries=1, Titans=10)
    • Winners gain Black VP from casualties
    • Win at a crystal/artifact location? Roll to capture it!

    Political Strength & Region Control

    The world map has 5 contestable regions: Europe, North America, South America, Middle East, Asia (Antarctica is your HQ base — not contestable).

    • Green Power increases your political strength in regions
    • The faction with the highest uncontested political strength in a region controls it
    • At end of each round: +1 VP per region you control
    • Region control also satisfies mission requirements (POLSTR)

    Allegiance & Victory Points

    VP comes in two types:

    • Black VP (BVP) — earned from Black missions, battles, covert ops
    • White VP (WVP) — earned from White missions, hero activation

    Your Total Score at game end = BVP + WVP + Team VP

    Team VP includes:

    • Track Bonus — your allegiance track value (Alpha for White, Omega for Black, max 50)
    • Polarity Bonus — +3 VP if your team's track is higher
    • 5D Bonus — Black only: max(0, 21 - 5D track position)
    • End Score Card — unique bonus card (0-20 VP based on achievement)

    The Nemesis (AI Opponent)

    The Nemesis is an automated opponent that:

    • Changes program each round (Guardian = White, Artemis = Black, Balance = either)
    • Draws cards and executes actions automatically
    • Completes missions, gains VP, and advances tracks
    • Has its own End Score Card worth +10 VP (Normal difficulty)

    The Nemesis panel on the left shows its current stats, program, and active mission progress.

    ADVANCED GAME (Addendum)

    The advanced game adds deeper mission mechanics and strategic items.

    Advanced Missions

    Instead of one-shot basic missions, advanced missions have multiple specific requirements that you work towards over several turns:

    • Consumed requirements (Red, Yellow, Blue, White, M9, Loki, Angel) — allocate tokens from your player mat by clicking them
    • Checked requirements (Control regions, own crystals/artifacts, 5D track level) — automatically verified; use the Claim button to refresh

    When ALL requirements are met (or enough to qualify), click Complete to earn VP:

    Level All Met 1 Missing 2 Missing Track Steps
    L1 6 VP 5 VP 4 VP +2
    L3 16 VP 12 VP 8 VP +2
    L5 24 VP 18 VP 12 VP +2
    L10 40 VP 30 VP 20 VP +3

    Stargates, Crystals & Artifacts

    Six Stargates connect world locations to crystal/artifact sites:

    • Crystals (6 types) — captured via battle victories; contribute to AP and mission requirements
    • Artifacts — powerful items captured from portal locations
    • Empowered items at stargates grant special abilities

    Heroes & 5D Track

    The 5D Track (0-21) determines hero activation power:

    • Higher 5D = more heroes drawn + easier activation rolls
    • White players activate heroes for WVP equal to hero level
    • Black players use the Battle action instead

    Nemesis Advanced Missions

    In advanced mode, the Nemesis also works on persistent missions:

    • Assigned an L1 mission at game start
    • Each turn, rolls 2d6 per unmet requirement (target: 9+)
    • Requirements met are saved — progress accumulates over rounds
    • On completion, earns VP and is assigned a higher-level mission
    • Its current mission and progress are visible on the Nemesis panel

    Quick Reference

    Resource Summary

    Resource Source Used For
    M0 (Cash) Card income Buy market cards
    M9 (Billions) Card income, missions Fund missions, flip allegiance ($100bn)
    Red Power Card actions Board moves (after free HQ deploy)
    Yellow Power Card actions Additional movement
    Blue Power Card income, Psychics action Hero activation, end card change (3 Blue), AP conversion
    Green Power Card income Place political strength in regions
    White Power Card income Mission requirements
    Wild Card income Substitute for any token
    Loki Battle rewards (Black) Black mission requirements
    Angel Hero activation (White) White mission requirements

    End-of-Round Events

    Each round has a random event affecting all players (shown at top of board).

    Game End Triggers

    • Alpha or Omega track reaches 21
    • Any player completes a Level 10 mission
    • Round 15 ends
