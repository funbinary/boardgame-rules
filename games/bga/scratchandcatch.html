    Scratchandcatch

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Get rid of your Fleas and collect cats


    Turn

    On your turn, play 2 cards

    First card does NOTHING

    Second card will have an effect based on how it's played


    Second Card Effects

    Normal or Scratch added to Stack - Take all cards on the immediate adjacent spaces

    Normal or Scratch added to Empty - Discard fleas equal to the number of connected adjacent cards in a sequence including itself

    Scratch (black border) bonus - Give each opponent fleas from the supply equal to the number of cards in the space regardless of whether they are Scratch or Normal. e.g. a Scratch added to an empty space means there is now one of that number so everyone else takes 1 Flea. A Scratch added to a pile of two before it, means there is now three in that pile and so everyone else takes 3 Fleas


    Game End

    When either a player cannot draw cards at end of turn or a player runs out of Fleas, the game ends

    Except for the Game Ender, everyone else takes a final turn

    Each collected Cat is a point

    Fleas gain you points if under 11

    Fleas lose you points if over 15

    Fleas neither gain nor lose if 11-15

    The player with the most points wins!

    If tied, the tied player with the least Fleas wins!
