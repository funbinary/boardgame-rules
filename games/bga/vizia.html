    VIZIA

    Objective

    Score the most points by forming hexagons of 6 tiles and claiming them with your markers.

    Scoring:

    • 6 different colors = 3 points
    • 2 alternating colors = 2 points
    • any other hexagon = 1 point

    Game end:

    • a player places all markers OR
    • all tiles have been played


    Game Overview

    Play proceeds clockwise.

    A turn consists of:

    1. Play tiles (at least 1)
    2. optionally play additional tiles
    3. optionally refill hand
    4. refill shared display

    A player may end their turn at any time after playing at least one tile.


    Turn Structure

    1. Play Tiles

    The active player must play at least 1 tile.

    Tiles may be played from:

    • personal hand
    • shared display
    • any combination

    Additional tiles may optionally be played.

    Placement rules:

    • first tile: placed freely
    • all further tiles: must be adjacent
    • colors must be neighbors on the color wheel


    Color Adjacency

    • Green ↔ Yellow / Blue
    • Blue ↔ Green / Purple
    • Purple ↔ Blue / Red
    • Red ↔ Purple / Orange
    • Orange ↔ Red / Yellow
    • Yellow ↔ Orange / Green


    2. Complete a Hexagon

    When a hexagon of 6 tiles is completed:

    • active player places their marker inside
    • marker remains until game end
    • multiple hexagons may be completed in one turn


    3. Continue Playing Tiles (Optional)

    The player may:

    • play additional tiles
    • complete multiple hexagons
    • voluntarily end the turn

    The turn ends when:

    • player stops OR
    • no legal placements remain


    4. Refill Hand (Optional)

    After placing tiles, the player may:

    • take tiles from the display
    • refill up to hand limit
    • or skip

    Then:

    • refill shared display


    5. No Possible Move

    If a player cannot place a tile:

    • reveal hand
    • turn is skipped
    • replace the entire display


    Player Actions

    During your turn:

    Required:

    • play at least 1 tile

    Optional:

    • play additional tiles
    • use tiles from display
    • complete multiple hexagons
    • refill hand
    • end turn early

    If blocked:

    • pass (after showing hand)


    Placement Rules (Summary)

    • tiles must be adjacent
    • colors must be neighbors
    • any number of tiles per turn
    • hexagon = exactly 6 tiles
    • completing player claims hexagon


    Game End

    The game ends immediately when:

    • a player places all markers

    OR

    • no tiles remain

    Then:

    • count points
    • highest score wins
    • tie: most hexagons wins


    Scoring Summary

    • 6 colors = 3 points
    • 2 alternating colors = 2 points
    • otherwise = 1 point


    Turn Summary

    1. play 1+ tiles
    2. claim completed hexagon(s)
    3. optionally play more
    4. optionally refill hand
    5. refill display
    6. next player
