    Aiye

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 7.0 · 排名 #7781 · 权重 2.41/5 · 265人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    End Game will trigger if either is met:

    • 4 masks played by one player (including council)
    • 18 cards played by one player (including council)

    Final Scoring:

    Characters - points on lower left of cards (including council)

    Masks - points by fulfilling Mask conditions (including council)

    Council cards - 1 point per Council card

    Columns - points for number of columns

    • 4 columns = -5 points
    • 5 columns = -3 points
    • 6 columns = 0 points
    • 7 columns = 3 points
    • 8 columns = 5 points
    • 9+ columns = 8 points
