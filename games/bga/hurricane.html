    Hurricane

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    GAME SETUP

    For the game you need a classic 52 card deck and then remove the JQK to remain 40 cards (2 to 9 and A per suit). The A is played as 1. The game is for two players only.

    After the deck is shuffeled, every player received six cards and four cards are revealed to the table.


    GAME PLAY

    One player starts. In your turn you have two options. 1. Either play one of your cards to the table. 2. Pick a card from your hand and arbitrarily many cards from the table which ranks are in sum the rank of your card.

    When both players don't have cards on their hands, every player draws six new cards. The player who played last gets to start and therefore plays twice.

    If the deck is empty and both players have played their last card, points are given and the game starts with a new round. The player that played the last card gets to start the new round. If a player has more than 15 points, the game ends.


    POINTS

    If you get to remove all cards from the table, you score a hurricane. The next player can only play a card and can not pick cards. For every hurricane you receive a point at the end of the round.

    Additionally, at the end you receive one point for: 

    - Taking more cards than your opponent
- Taking more hearts than your opponent
- Taking the 7 of hearts
