    Yro

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    OBJECTIVE

    Recruit adventurers for your guild, optimize your formation, and collect the most Victory Points (VP) through combat, technology, magic, and fulfilling quests.

    SETUP

    • Each player receives a player board and 4 markers (Combat, VP, Technology, Magic).
    • Place the shared Combat and Victory board in the center.
    • Each player starts with 3 Money and a starting hand of 5 cards.
    • Display the Quest cards face-up.

    GAMEPLAY

    Each round consists of 6 phases, played simultaneously:

    1. Discard & Draw Phase:

    • Discard any number of hand cards and draw back up to 5 cards.

    2. Recruit Phase:

    • You may play 0 /1 / 2 cards from your hand by paying their cost (top left) in gold. Each card not played earns 1 gold.
    • Place it in your 3x3 grid, orthogonally adjacent to an existing card.
    • Complete the recruit effects and link bonuses for the cards that were played.

    3. Combat Phase:

    • Compare your Combat Power with all other players.
    • The stronger player(s) receives VP based on the player count.
    • Example: In a 5-player game, if two players are tied for first place, they each receive 5 VP. Any second place players receive 1 VP instead of 3. Players in third, fourth, and fifth place will receive no VP

    4. Production Phase:

    • Every Adventurer with the Production symbol activates that line of text now. Most Production involves adding Magic and Technology.

    5. Income Phase:

    • Gain 3 Money. Then look for any of your recruited Adventurers with the Income icon ; you gain extra Money as specified on their cards.

    6. Victory Point Phase:

    • Look for any of your recruited Adventurers with the VP icon , and gain that many VPs.

    KEY CONCEPTS

    • Factions: There are 6 different factions (e.g., Floating City, Mining Company) essential for line bonuses.
    • Professions: Warrior, Wizard, Merchant, etc., also determine synergies within the grid.
    • Tech & Magic: These tracks grant additional victory points at the end of the game or via card effects.
    • Resources: Symbols on cards that provide permanent cost reductions for recruiting.

    END OF GAME

    The game ends at the end the round where a player has 9 cards in their grid or a player reaches 40 VPs.

    • Every 3 remaining money converts to 1 VP.
    • The player with the most VP wins.
