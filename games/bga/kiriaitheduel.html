    Kiriaitheduel

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Play cards to move your Samurai and try to deal 2 damage to your opponent, whilst switching between the Heaven and Earth stances to your advantage


    Turn

    First, each player secretly picks a 1st and 2nd card

    Then reveal both 1st cards and resolve them, before revealing both 2nd cards and resolving them

    There are two possible stances: Heaven and Earth

    Movement cards played during Heaven Stance are resolved first

    Movement cards when in Earth Stance are resolved second

    A Movement card cannot move one Samurai beyond another, it can still be played but it will at best move the Samurai so they are adjacent instead

    Attack and Special cards are resolved last

    If two successful attacks are played, they cancel each other out

    If only one successful attack is played, it deals damage to the opponent who flips their card to the damaged side

    After all cards are resolved, card 1 is returned to the hand, whilst card 2 is put aside. In a following round, the same will happen, but the set aside card will return to the hand when the new card 2 is set aside


    Game End

    The game ends when a player takes their 2nd damage, they are defeated and the opponent wins!


    Cards

    Movement
    Approach Move 1 space closer
    Retreat Move 1 space away
    Charge Move 2 spaces closer
    Change Stance Change from Heaven to Earth or Earth to Heaven
    Attack
    High Strike Only succeeds in Heaven and if the opponent is at 2 spaces away
    Low Strike Only succeeds in Earth and if the opponent is at 1 space away
    Balanced Strike Only succeeds if the opponent is at the same location
    Special
    Kesa Strike Only succeeds in Heaven and if the opponent is at 0-1 spaces away, then causes you to switch to Earth
    Zan Tetzu Strike Only succeeds in Earth and if the opponent is at 2-3 spaces away, then causes you to switch to Heaven
    Counterattack If the opponent lands a successful attack or special attack, deal them back 1 damage
