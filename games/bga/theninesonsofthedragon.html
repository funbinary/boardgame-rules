    Theninesonsofthedragon

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    The game starts with the first player taking their turn, followed by alternating turns between players. On their turn, a player must choose one of the following actions to perform:

    1. Place a piece: Move one of their off-board pieces onto an empty space on the board.
    2. Move a piece: Move an existing piece on the board according to the game's movement rules.

    Dice Movement Rules:

    1. Select Your Top Die: Choose one stack where your die is on top.
    2. Lift the Stack: Pick up all dice from that stack while maintaining their current order.
    3. Determine Movement: The number of dice in hand equals the exact number of spaces you must move.
    4. Execute Movement:
      • Players may only move to empty spaces or spaces holding 1-2 dice
      • At each new space, place the bottommost die from your hand. But you can't do it when that die is smaller than the top die in the space (specially, 6 is smaller than 1 here)
      • Continue until all dice are placed

    Key Points:

    • You must move the exact number of spaces equal to the dice you picked up
    • Dice are always placed from the bottom of your hand upward
    • Each move must land on a new, unvisited space during your turn


    Obtaining Dragon Son Cards:

    • One of the card's spaces must contain a stack of 3 dice
    • Important: Yellow cards require fulfilling additional conditions specified on the card itself to be obtained
    • Important:Acquiring red cards may trigger negative side effects


    Game End Conditions:

    The game ends when either of the following conditions is met:

    1. There are only 4 cards left on the board.
    2. A player reaches 7 points first.
