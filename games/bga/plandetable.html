    Plan de Table

    Objective

    Help the newlyweds create the perfect seating plan by placing guests at tables according to their personal wishes and the table objectives. Score points by fulfilling as many wishes and objectives as possible. The player with the most points at the end of the game wins.

    Gameplay

    • On your turn, you must choose ONE of the following actions:
      • Place a guest card from your hand onto a free seat (put your meeple on it), then draw back up to 3 guest cards.
      • Move one of your own guests already seated to another available seat.
      • Discard one or more guest cards from your hand and draw the same number to refill your hand.
    • If you place a guest on a seat marked with an "E", immediately draw and resolve an Event card.
    • After your action, refill your hand to 3 cards if needed.
    • Play continues clockwise.

    End of game

    The game ends when one of these conditions is met:

    • There are no more guest cards in the deck
    • All seats are filled
    • A player has placed all their meeples (guests)

    Players finish the current round so everyone has played the same number of turns. Scoring:

    • Each guest scores points for fulfilled personal wishes (yellow cubes = 3 pts), for matching table objectives (green cubes = 1 pt), and loses points for penalties (red cubes = -1 pt).
    • The player with the most points is the winner.

    Variant

    Strategic Draw Variant: Instead of drawing blindly, three guest cards are placed face up next to the draw deck. On your turn, you may choose to draw from these face-up guests or from the top of the deck. Actually the variant is not implemented on bga (maybe for the next release)
