    Dobro

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.2 · 排名 #9332 · 权重 1.04/5 · 464人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Have the most points after 3 rounds


    Turn

    You can play a single card with a value equal or greater than the current played value

    You can play two cards of the same value if the sum of both cards are equal or greater than the current played value

    If you play a card or cards equal to the current played value, the new current played value will be doubled.

    You can play a special card

    You can take all played cards and add them to your score pile

    At end of turn, draw back to 6.


    Round End

    After the draw pile is emptied, the next player to empty their hand ends the round

    Any hand and play cards are discarded

    Gain points depending on how many score cards you have compared to other players (more cards = less points)


    Game End

    The game ends after 3 rounds, the player with the most points wins!
