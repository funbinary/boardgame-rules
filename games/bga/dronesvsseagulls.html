    Dronesvsseagulls

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.4 · 排名 #11585 · 212人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Play cards to the table to attempt to control all outposts by gaining control and switching them to your color.

    Turn

    Play 1 card to your side of an Outpost

    If the outpost is currently of opposite colour & the total strength of your cards is greater than your opponent's take control of the outpost

    If you take control of an outpost, apply the outposts effect

    Round End

    If a player controls all outposts simultaneously, they win immediately

    If a player plays their last card without triggering the end of the round, one outpost is selected that cannot be flipped and their opponent takes one additional turn. The player with the most outputs then wins

    Game End

    First player to win 2 rounds wins the game
