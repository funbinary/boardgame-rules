    TomaToss

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 5.5 · 排名 #26927 · 权重 1.33/5 · 61人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    TomaToss is a light card game about collecting tomatoes and tossing them to complete festival targets.

    Gameplay

    On your turn, perform 3 actions. For each action, you may:

    • Pick up 1 tomato card from the shared market, or
    • Toss tomato cards at 1 target card.

    There are 2 kinds of toss:

    • Toss: play tomato cards from your hand to meet the target condition.
    • Quick Toss: play tomato cards from your hand, then reveal 1 extra tomato card from the deck and include it in the attempt.

    If the toss succeeds:

    1. You claim the target card.
    2. Score the normal value for a Toss, or the quick value for a Quick Toss.
    3. Refill the target if possible.

    After your 3 actions, check your action pattern for a bonus:

    • 3 actions in the same slot: draw a bonus tomato card.
    • 2 actions in one slot and 1 in another:
      • if your basket is full, empty it and draw a bonus tomato card;
      • if your basket is empty, fill it.
    • 3 different slots: no bonus.

    If you have more than 8 tomato cards at the end of your three actions, discard down to 8.

    Game End

    When the last target is claimed and no new target can replace it, finish the turn and end the game.

    The player with the most points wins.

    If tied, the tied player with the highest total value of tomato cards in hand wins. If there is still a tie, the tied players share the victory.
