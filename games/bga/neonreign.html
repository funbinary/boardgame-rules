    Neonreign

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    OBJECTIVE

    Defeat your opponent in this arcade-style fighting game by reducing their health to 0.

    SETUP

    • Each player chooses a Hero and their corresponding Action deck.
    • Starting health is set to 25.
    • Each player draws 7 cards for their starting hand.
    • The player who says "Ready, Fight!" first goes first.

    GAMEPLAY

    Players take turns performing actions. A turn consists of three phases:

    1. Draw Phase:

    • Draw 2 cards from your Action deck. (There is no hand limit).

    2. Action Phase:

    • You may play any number of cards to perform Attacks or activate effects.
    • Joysticks: Cards representing movement or positioning.
    • Buttons: Cards representing punches, kicks, or special moves.
    • Combos: Combine cards according to the symbols on your Hero card to trigger extra effects or damage.

    3. End Phase:

    • Effects that last "until the end of the turn" expire.
    • Play passes to the opponent.

    COMBAT & DEFENSE

    • Attacking: The active player plays attack cards. Total damage is calculated.
    • Defending: The defender can play Block cards (to reduce damage from Buttons) or Counter cards (to react to specific maneuvers).
    • Star Power: You gain Star tokens through attacks and effects. Once you reach 5 tokens, your powerful Star Power Ability triggers automatically.
    • Shields: Shield tokens can be spent to block incoming damage on a one-for-one basis.

    END OF GAME

    • The game ends immediately when a player's health reaches 0. The surviving player is the winner.
