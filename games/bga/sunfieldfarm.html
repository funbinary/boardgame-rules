    Sunfieldfarm

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Each turn you may either:

    A) Add a Field Tile to your field

    • Take a Field Tile from the display, the top of the column for free or lower by placing seeds on higher tiles

    • Place the tile in your plot, adjacent to an existing tile. Take the vegetables shown on the tile and place them on fields that are either adjacent to or the same color as the placed tile.

    • Harvest - If any tile is full, put the vegetables on it into your wheelbarrow, flip it to its pasture side, and place the pictured animals on it.

    • Deliver animals - If you have one animal of each type (cow, sheep, pig), return them to the supply and take a 3-value medal.

    -or-

    B) Deliver to a Customer

    • Take the vegetables pictured on one face-up customer card from your wheelbarrow and return them to the supply. Take the card and the medal of the highest value below the customer.

    • If the customer cards you have acquired fulfill the requirements of any reputation tile (either in your hand or from the board) collect it and flip it to show its point value.

    At any time, you may use seeds to exchange vegetables:

    • 3 seeds to exchange a vegetable from your wheelbarrow with one from the supply

    • 5 seeds to take any vegetable from the supply and add it to your wheelbarrow

    At the end of the game you gain points for:

    • Customer Cards

    • Medals

    • Reputation Tiles

    • 3 pts for three customers of the same color

    • 3 pts for three customers of the same value

    • 1 pt for any three vegetables remaining in your wheelbarrow.
