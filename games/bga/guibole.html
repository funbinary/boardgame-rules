    Guibole

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    👥 2–6 人 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    The goal of the Guibole is to lose the least amount of points during a game.

    Playing the game

    Number of players : 2-6 players.

    Guibole is played with a standard 52-card deck. The cards points values are as follow:

    • A = 1
    • 2 = 2
    • ...
    • 10, J, Q, K = 10

    Cards are dealt one at a time clockwise until all cards have been dealt. And another card is discarded.

    When it's your turn to play, you have two possibilities:

    • show your cards, if you have 10 points or less in your hand to end the round.
    • discard a card (or multiple of same value) and take the first card from the pile or the last discarded card before yours.

    Note: showing your cards by having more than 10 points makes you loose the round.

    End of the game

    The game will continue until a player loses all their points.

    Points

    Each player starts with a certain amount of points (depending of the game length).

    If you showed your cards and: - nobody have equals or less points than you, you don't lose any points. Other players lose the points in their hand.

    Example: Bob announce 6 points, Alice have 8 points in hand and Peter 12. Bob don't lose points. Alice loose 8 and Peter 12 points.

    - Anybody have equals or less points than you, you lose the points in your hand * 2 + 25 points. The players who countered don't lose points. Other players lose the points in their hand.

    Example: Bob announce 6 points, Alice have 6 points in hand and Peter 12. Bob loose 6 * 2 + 25 = 39 points. Alice don't lose points and Peter lose 12 points.

    - You had more than 10 points in your hand, you lose 10 * 2 + 25 points. Other players doesn't lose any points.
