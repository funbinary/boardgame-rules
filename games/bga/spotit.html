    Spotit

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Introduction

    Spot It (or Dobble) is a collection of 5 mini games. They are all players with 55 cards. Each card has 8 symbols from the 50 possible. However, there is one and only one matching symbol on any 2 cards.

    Towering Inferno

    • Each player gets a card and the rest forms a face-up stack.
    • All players try to find the matching symbol on their card and the stack’s top card. The first to find it gets the top card on the stack.
    • The game ends when the stack is depleted. The player with the most cards wins.

    Well

    • Place a card in the middle stack and the rest are divided among players.
    • All players try to find the matching symbol on their card and the stack’s top card. The first to find it place their card to the top of the stack.
    • The first to get rid of their cards wins and the last to do so loses.

    Hot Potato

    • All players get a card each round.
    • All players try to find the matching symbol on the top card of their stack and an opponent’s stack. If you find it, put your stack on top of that opponent’s.
    • The only player with a stack at the end of the round collects the stack.
    • The game ends when all cards are used. The player with the least cards wins.

    Poisoned Gift

    • Each player gets a card and the rest forms a face-up stack.
    • All players try to find the matching symbol on the top card of their stack and the middle stack. The first to find it gives the top card of the middle stack to an opponent.
    • The game ends when the middle stack is depleted. The player with the least cards wins.

    Triplets

    • Draw 9 cards and place them in the middle. The rest forms a stack.
    • All players try to find a matching symbol between 3 cards in the middle. The first to do so collects these 3 cards and refills the middle.
    • The game ends when there are no more matches left. The player with the most cards wins.
