    Tallyup

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    Be the first player to reach 2500 points at the end of a round.

    Overview

    There are 6 Tally dice and 1 Up die. Before each roll, players decide if they stay IN and risk it, our go OUT and bank the running total to their points. The active player chooses 3 of the 6 Tally dice to roll -- the choice they make depends on their risk tolerance (see Tally Dice). The points on the dice are added to the running total. If 2 stars are rolled, then the running total resets to 0. If 3 stars are rolled, it is a Tally Up which awards bonus points.

    Tally Dice

    Each die has its own mix of stars, circles, and points. Use the chart below and the Dice Roll Cards during the game to plan your strategy. Parentheses indicate a circle around a number on the die.

    Die Risk Faces
    Red High ★ ★ ★ 100 50 (50)


    Orange High ★ ★ ★ 70 (70) 50
    Yellow Medium ★ ★ 70 (40) 20 20
    Green Medium ★ ★ 40 40 (40) 30
    Blue Low ★ 50 20 10 (10) (10)
    Purple Low ★ 20 20 20 (20) (20)

    Playing a Round

    1. The active player chooses three Tally Dice to roll. These dice cannot be swapped out for other Tally Dice during the round.

    2. They roll the chosen dice. This roll is called the Main Roll.

    3. The roll is resolved (see Resolve The Roll).

    4. Each player chooses to stay IN or go OUT.

    5. For all players who go OUT the current running total is added to their score.

    6. Repeat steps 2-5 until:

    • A. Everyone has gone OUT, or...
    • B. Two stars cause a bust!
    • C. NOTE: The active player continues to roll even if they have gone OUT for the round.

    7. Once there is a bust or everyone has gone OUT, the Running Total resets to zero, and all IN / OUT tokens are flipped back to IN. The dice moves to the next player. This player will become the new active player. The new active player chooses any three Tally Dice to roll (go back to Step 1).

    Resolve the Roll

    0 or 1 Star showing

    • Add the numbers shown on the dice to the Running Total for this round.
    • If one or more circled numbers appear on the Main Roll, the Up Die will be rolled (see The Up Die).


    2 Stars = Bust!

    • The round ends immediately.
    • The Running Total resets to zero.
    • Everyone still IN scores no points.
    • The Tally Dice passes to the next player.


    3 Stars = Tally Up!

    • Anytime the original three dice in a Main Roll show three stars, a Tally Up! occurs.
    • The first player to click on "Tally Up!" scores a 200-point bonus, even if they have gone OUT for the round.
    • This bonus is added to their score.
    • All other players still IN get 100 points added to their score.
    • You cannot score a Tally Up! Bonus from an Up Die Bonus Roll.

    The Up Die

    If one or more circled numbers appear on the Main Roll, the Up Die will be rolled. Before the Up Die is rolled, all IN players decide whether to stay IN or go OUT. Then the active player rolls the Up Die! The roll is resolved using the chart below. NOTE: The Up Die is only rolled once, even if multiple numbers are circled.

    Face Effect Description
    +1 Bonus Roll Roll one extra Tally Die
    +2 Bonus Roll Roll two extra Tally Dice
    +3 Bonus Roll Roll all three remaining Tally Dice
    -100 Penalty Subtracts 100 points from the Running Total
    +100 Bonus Adds 100 points to the Running Total
    x2 Bonus Doubles the Running Total

    After resolving the Up die, play returns to step 4 in Playing a Round.

    Winning

    The player who scores 2500 or more points at the end of a round wins! If multiple players have more than 2500 points, the highest score wins. If there is a tie, another round is played.
