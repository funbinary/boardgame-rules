    Xxung

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Be the first to play 5 in a row OR put your opponent in a position where they cannot play


    Turn

    Place a piece horizontally or vertically to the previously placed piece, as far as you like, but as long as no other pieces are between the two


    Game End

    The game ends when a player makes 5 in a row either vertically, horizontally OR diagonally

    Alternatively, a player loses when they can't make a legal play
