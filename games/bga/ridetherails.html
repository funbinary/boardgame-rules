    Ridetherails

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    Invest in various railroad companies and strategically expand their rail networks to earn the most money through dividends across several rounds (generations).

    Card Reference

    • Share Tiles: Each color represents a railroad company. Owning a share allows you to build tracks for that company and entitles you to dividends.
    • Game Board: Displays cities with different values. The goal is to connect these cities using the rail networks.

    Gameplay

    The game is played over a fixed number of rounds (depending on player count). Each round consists of three phases:

    1. Take a Share:
      • In turn order, each player chooses exactly one share of an available company from the supply and places it face-up in front of them.
      • Each player takes only one share per round.
    2. Build Track:
      • If you own at least one share of a company, you may expand its network.
      • The number of track pieces you can place is limited (usually 1 to 3, depending on the company).
      • Tracks must be placed to extend the existing network of that color.
      • Building over mountain terrain costs additional track points.
    3. Ride the Rails (Dividends):
      • Select a city where at least one of your companies has a presence.
      • Move an imaginary train to another city. Each city reached on the route generates points.
      • All players holding shares in the companies whose tracks were used for this route receive money (dividends).

    Game End

    The game ends after the final round (generation).

    • All money collected during the game constitutes your final score.
    • there is no additional end-of-game scoring for remaining shares.
    • The player with the most money wins.
