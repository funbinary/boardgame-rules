    Stackfive

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Disclaimer: Official rules are not currently available online. This summary is compiled from official product descriptions and observed gameplay behavior on BGA, so some details may differ from the official rules. Feel free to edit this summary.

    Goal

    First to 3 points wins.

    Placement Rules

    Manage up to 3 columns (max 5 cards each). You can only place cards to form these 5-card combinations:

    • 5 different numbers: 1 point
    • 5 identical numbers: 2 points
    • 5 same color: 2 points
    • 5 different numbers of the same color: 3 points (Instant Win)

    If no legal spot exists, discard an entire column to make room.

    Your Turn

    Draw two cards to start and then you can start drawing cards one by one; stopping whenever you want.

    • Stop: Keep and place all drawn cards.
    • Bust (Duplicate Number Drawn): Lose all cards drawn this turn; choose an opponent to receive the cards. Note that this may be triggered immediately if the first two cards you draw are of the same number.

    Special Cards (Resolve Immediately)

    • Draw 3 More: Draw 3 cards. (Bust rules apply).
    • Number Bomb: Draw 1 card. All players discard matching numbers.
    • Color Bomb: Draw 1 card. All players discard matching colors.
    • Steal: Take the top card of an opponent’s column.
    • Joker: Counts as any number and color.
