    Saddle

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 4.3 · 排名 #0 · 5人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    OBJECTIVE Saddle is a two player card game, the objective of which is to get rid of all of your cards or be the last to take a turn.

    THE DECK A standard 52-card deck, ranked from ace (1) to King.

    SETUP Each player starts with 12 cards dealt face down. Two cards are placed separately face up to begin two discard piles. The remainder of the cards become the draw pile.

    PLAY Players take turns matching a card from their hand by rank or suit with the top card in one of the discard piles, but not both. The card placed is called the saddle. Based upon it, one or more additional cards can be added before the turn ends. The added cards must either be of the same rank as the saddle, or a sequence up or down from it. The added cards may be of different suits. At no time do players pick up any new cards. A player wins if he gets rid of all his cards or his opponent is unable to take a turn.

    SCORING After each game the winning player gets one point for each card remaining in his opponents hand, and when one player reaches 13 points he wins the match.
