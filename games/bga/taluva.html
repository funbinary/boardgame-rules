    Overview

    • Players take turns to place volcano tiles and settlements on an island emerging in the South Pacific.
    • The first player to build all their buildings of two types wins.

    Tile: 3 hexagonal spaces; one ​ and two other terrain types from: ​, ​, ​, ​, ​.

    Buildings: temples ​, towers ​, and huts ​

    Settlement: a group of one or more buildings of the same colour on adjacent spaces.

    Setup

    • Each player receives 3, 2, and 20.
    • 12 tiles per player are shuffled face-down into a draw pile.

    Game play

    Tile placement

    • A tile is randomly selected.
    • Select the space and orientation of ​.

    Expand the landscape

    Place the tile touching at least one edge of an existing 1st-level tile.

    Volcanic eruption

    • Place the ​ on top of an existing ​.
    • Must have ​ in a different direction.
    • Must have spaces under both its terrain spaces.
    • Any ​ covered are destroyed and remain out of play.
    • Cannot destroy ​, ​ or an entire settlement.

    Building placement

    Place buildings on empty terrain space(s) of any one terrain type (not ​).

    Build a Hut

    • Place 1 on a 1st-level space not adjacent to any of your existing settlements.

    Build a Temple

    • Place 1 adjacent to one of your existing settlements that occupies at least 3 spaces.
    • Existing settlement must not already have a ​.

    Build a Tower

    • Place 1 adjacent to one of your existing settlements and on a 3rd-level (or higher) space.
    • Existing settlement must not already have a ​.

    Expand a settlement

    • Place ​ adjacent to one of your existing settlements on all spaces of that terrain type.
    • 1st-level ⇨ 1, 2nd-level ⇨ 2, 3rd-level ⇨ 3 etc.
    • Cannot build more ​ than available.
