    Carnuta

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 7.2 · 排名 #2700 · 权重 1.65/5 · 1361人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Game Play

    Perform two actions out of four possible actions. You can do the same action twice or take two different actions:

    1. ・Take a card into your hand. The hand limit is 3 but the limit is only checked at the end of your turn.
    2. ・Play a card from your hand; pay (discard) or flip the corresponding runes listed on the top of the card. A swoosh and an arrow means you will flip the rune to its other side. “X” means you will discard the rune to the center of the table. Runes are discarded without changing their faces.
    3. ・Take runes from the reserve; you take as many runes with the same symbol as are available from the reserve, up to your limit.
    4. ・Flip your runes so that they are either all suns or all moons.

    Game Board

    ・Placing your 4th card into your tableau will unlock one extra slot for runes. Placing your 8th card will unlock another extra slot for runes.

    ・Placing your 6th and 10th card will allow you to pick a token from the reserve. A token adds to your count of that symbol for end game scoring purposes.

    Other

    ・As a free action on your turn, you can discard one of your sickle tokens to refresh one of the two rows of cards.

    ・Salt cards are wild and must be played onto cards already in your tableau

    Game End

    The end of the game begins when one player adds an 11th card to their tableau; finish the round so that everyone has an equal number of turns and then play one last round.

    Scores are determined by the scoring conditions of each card. The same cards/icons can be counted towards multiple scoring conditions. The player with the highest score wins the game!
