    Assigning Dice

    Goal Cards

    Place your die on the star(*) on the Main Game Board to draw 2 goal cards, keep 1.

    Pantry & Marketplace

    Place a die or dice on the desired ingredient. (see chart below)

    ● Gain ingredients equal to the current Pantry Card value.
    ● To buy additional ingredients, click on the die to pay coins equal to the die value for each additional ingredient.
    ● Marketplace is limited to 6 per ingredient.
    ● Assign ingredients to recipes(permanent) prior to die placement and/or purchase to ensure adequate space.
    Ingredient Die Placement Info
    Flours
    Leavening Agents
    Sugar & Salt
    Vegetables & Herbs
    Fruits & Jams
    Milk & Dairy
    Oils & Fats
    		 Place a die matching one of the options displayed. Additional ingredients must be purchased based on the die value you used.

    You may not place a second die under the same ingredient.

    Eggs
    		 Place a die on the star(*).

    Must be less than 4 (1,2 or 3 only).

    		Additional ingredients must be purchased based on the die value you used.
    Seeds & Spices
    		 Place a die on the star(*).

    Must be greater than 3 (4,5,6 only).

    		Additional ingredients must be purchased based on the die value you used.
    Water
    		 Place a die of any value on the star(*). Additional ingredients must be purchased based on the die value you used.
    Chili & Hot Sauce
    		 Place a die on each star(*).

    The left die must be lower value than the right die. Dice cannot be equal.

    		Dice must be placed during the same turn.

    Additional ingredients must be purchased based on the right/HIGHER die value you used.

    Chocolate & Cocoa
    		 Place a die on each star(*).

    The dice must be equal.

    		Dice must be placed during the same turn.

    Additional ingredients must be purchased based on the die value you used.

    Time Track

    Place a die of any value on the leftmost available star(*).

    ● Gain the amount of time indicated.
    ● Dice must be placed during the same turn to achieve the higher value time rewards.

    Savings

    Place a die of any value on the leftmost available star(*).

    ● Gain the amount of coins indicated.
    ● Dice must be placed during the same turn to achieve the higher value coin rewards.

    End Game Scoring

    ● VP from completed recipes.
    ● VP from successful goal cards.
    ● 2 VP per used upgrade (brown).
    ● 3 VP per unused upgrade (brown).
    ● 1 VP per refreshment (blue).
    ● 1 VP per 3 leftovers (coins + unused ingredients).
    ● -5 VP per recipe in the trash.
