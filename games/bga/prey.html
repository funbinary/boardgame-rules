    Prey

    Overview

    Prey is a trick-taking game where players alternate between being powerful predators and vulnerable prey.

    Each round contains:

    • 6 Predator tricks
    • 6 Prey tricks

    To score:

    • Win a number of tricks matching one of your Goal Numbers

    The first player to score 2 points wins the game.

    Goal

    Play 6 tricks as predators...

    ...and 6 tricks as the prey that survive.

    Components

    • 48 Animal cards
      • 4 suits
      • 12 cards per suit
    • 8 Dice
      • 2 per player
    • 4 Score cards

    Setup

    1. Each player takes:
      • 1 Score card
      • 2 matching dice
    1. Place Score cards with the 0 side face-up.
    1. Each player rolls both dice.

    Important:

    • If you roll doubles:
      • Reroll until the numbers are different
    1. Place dice on your Score card.
    1. Shuffle the Animal cards.
    1. Deal each player 12 cards.

    Players should hold cards with:

    • Predator numbers at the top

    3-player game:

    • Deal a 4th hand of 12
    • Set it aside unused

    First Lead Player

    The player with:

    • The lowest total on their dice

    leads the first trick.

    Tie breaker:

    1. Lowest individual die
    2. Random choice

    Goal Numbers

    Your 2 dice create your Goal Numbers.

    To score 1 point for the round:

    • Win tricks equal to either Goal Number

    Example:

    • Goal Numbers = 2 and 5
    • Score by winning exactly:
      • 2 tricks

    OR

      • 5 tricks

    Special Rule: Goal Number 6

    A Goal Number of 6 is special.

    If you rolled a 6:

    You may score by winning:

    • 6 tricks
    • 0 tricks
    • Your other Goal Number

    Example:

    • Goal Numbers = 2 and 6

    You score if you win:

    • 0 tricks
    • 2 tricks
    • 6 tricks

    Predator & Prey Tricks

    Each round contains:

    Trick Numbers Mode
    1–6 Predator
    7–12 Prey

    Predator Tricks

    During Predator tricks:

    • All cards use their darker Predator numbers

    Prey Tricks

    After the 6th trick:

    • All players rotate cards in hand to the Prey side

    During Prey tricks:

    • All cards use their Prey numbers

    Playing a Trick

    The lead player:

    • Plays any 1 card

    The suit played becomes:

    • The Lead Suit

    Then, clockwise:

    • Each player plays 1 card

    Following Suit

    If possible:

    • You must play the Lead Suit

    If you do not have the Lead Suit:

    • You may play any card

    Winning a Trick

    Predator Trick

    The winner is:

    • The highest Predator number in the Lead Suit

    Prey Trick

    The winner is:

    • The highest Prey number in the Lead Suit

    After Winning

    The winner:

    1. Takes all cards in the trick
    2. Places them face-down in front of themselves
    3. Leads the next trick

    Players keep trick piles separated to clearly show:

    • Number of tricks won

    Round End

    After the 12th trick:

    • Each player counts tricks won

    If your total matches one of your Goal Numbers:

    • Score 1 point

    Scoring Points

    When a player scores their first point:

    • Flip their Score card from 0 to 1

    When a player scores their second point:

    • The game ends immediately
    • That player wins

    If multiple players score their second point simultaneously:

    • Victory is shared

    New Round

    If no player has won the game:

    1. Players reroll Goal Numbers
    2. Shuffle and redeal cards
    3. Play a new round

    The player who won the final trick:

    • Leads the next round

    Strategy Tips

    • Winning too many tricks can be just as dangerous as winning too few.
    • Goal Number 6 creates strong “avoid everything” strategies.
    • Saving off-suit cards for the Prey phase can create huge swings.
    • Watch opponents’ trick piles carefully.

    Clarifications

    • Predator tricks always use Predator values only.
    • Prey tricks always use Prey values only.
    • You only need to match ONE Goal Number to score.
    • Players may score simultaneously.
