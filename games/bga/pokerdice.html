    Pokerdice

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 5.4 · 排名 #0 · 14人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Gameplay

    • Each player has 5 dices with 6 sides (one each of A, K, Q, J, 10, 9).
    • In each round, each player rolls all 5 dice. Then, they may reroll any number of those dice up to twice (click on the dice that you want to reroll).
    • After all rerolls (or when the player stops), compare all hands from highest to lowest.
    • Tied hands are resolved by the dice used in the combination, then by those unused. Note that A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9.
    • Lose lives according to the variant chosen. By default, rank-based life loss is used.
    • Game ends when a player loses all lives. The player with the most remaining lives wins.

    Poker Dice Hands

    In the following table, 1 is the best combination while 8 is the worst. Note that there are no suits in this game.

    Name Example Rank (Standard) Rank (Probability-based)
    Five of a Kind A-A-A-A-A 1 1
    Four of a Kind A-A-A-A-K 2 2
    Full House A-A-A-K-K 3 4
    Straight A-K-Q-10-J 4 3
    Three of a Kind A-A-A-K-Q 5 6
    Two Pair A-A-K-K-Q 6 7
    One Pair A-A-K-Q-J 7 8
    No Combination A-K-Q-J-9 8 5

    Variants

    • Number of Lives: 3 or 5
    • Life Loss: Rank-based life loss, All players except winner lose 1 life or Lowest rank players lose 1 life (Detailed below)
    • Hank Rank: Standard or Probability-based (Described above)
    • Simultaneous Mode: All players play simultaneously. Rolled dice are revealed at the end of the round.
    • Elimination Mode: Off or On (Detailed below)

    Life Loss

    • Rank-based life loss: The number of lives is multiplied by the number of players - 1. All players lose lives equal to the round rank - 1. If all players tie, all players lose 1 life.
    • All players except winner lose 1 life: All players except the round winner lose 1 life. If all players tie, all players lose 1 life.
    • Lowest rank players lose 1 life: The lowest rank players of the round lose 1 life. If all players tie, all players lose 1 life.

    Elimination Mode

    • Off: When a player loses all lives, the game ends. All remaining players are ranked by the number of remaining lives.
    • On: When a player loses all lives, the player is eliminated and the game continues. Game ends when only one player is left.
