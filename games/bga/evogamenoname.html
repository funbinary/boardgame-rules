    Evogamenoname

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    The aim is to capture an opponents castle by moving onto it (The castle itself cannot move)


    Turn

    On your turn You have 3 options:

    1) Rotate a piece

    2) Move a piece

    3) Move then Rotate a piece


    Pieces

    All pieces (except the castle) are essentially identical, the icon which is currently the right way round (aka at the top from your perspective) is the active type, these are split into:

    Warrior - Moves 1 square Left/Right, Forward, or Diagonal Left/Right forward (5 options)

    Blade - Moves up to 2 squares forward

    Catapult - Moves up to 3 squares diagonally forward

    Shield - The only type which can move backwards, 2 squares straight back or 1 square diagonal back
