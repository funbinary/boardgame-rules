    Mod Ten

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    👥 6 人 📄 BGA 官方文档

    INTRODUCTION

    Mod Ten is a shedding card game for 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6 players.

    DECK

    Either a single, or double stripped deck is used.

    In a single deck, the following cards are removed

    • All games: 5s, 10s, and face cards.
    • 3/5/6 player games, additionally: The Ace and 9 of Spades.

    In a double deck (BGA default) deck, the following cards are removed

    • All games: 10s and face cards.
    • 3/5/6 players games, additionally: The Aces and 9s of Spades, Clubs and Diamonds

    BGA DECKS

    N players, deck

    2 - single w/o 5s,10s, face cards.

    3 - single w/o 5s, 10s, faces; Ace, 9 of Spades.

    4 - double w/o 10s, faces.

    5, 6 - double w/o 10s, faces; Ace, 9 of S, C, D.

    RANKS

    High to low: 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, Ace. Suits are irrelevant.

    PLAY

    The player to the dealers left leads by placing any card from their hand face up on the table. Continuing clockwise, players must follow suit if possible. If not, their turn is skipped. If your played card brings the sum of table cards to a multiple of 10, you collect all the table cards, place them face down on the discard pile, and lead another card of any suit.

    OBJECT OF THE GAME

    The first player to play their last card wins the game.

    Dealer Selection

    The dealer of the first hand of the game is selected at random, in subsequent hands it will be the player to the right of the last player to shed a card in the previous hand.
