    Piles

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.5 · 排名 #0 · 24人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    The purpose of this game is to sort laundry into pules that all have identical styles but different colors. Working at the same time as other players, you sort your laundry to try to make 6 complete sets of 4 using the pile of laundry at the top. When a pile is complete, it will turn white and you can't access it anymore. When you have all 6 piles done first, you win the game. An example pile is like 4 colors of short socks.

    Have fun sorting laundry!
