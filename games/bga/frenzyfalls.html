    Frenzy Falls

    Overview

    Frenzy Falls is a tactical card game of influence, timing, and cascading spillovers.

    Players play Waterfall cards into shared Pools in order to raise water levels and win valuable Score cards.

    Each card contributes:

    • Water Value — helps Pools spill over
    • Influence Icons — determines control

    When a Pool reaches a total water value of 10 or more, it spills over. The player with the most influence in that Pool wins the Score card.

    The player with the most points at the end of the game wins.

    contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

    Components

    • 90 Waterfall cards
    • 32 Score cards
    • 1 First Player card

    Card Types

    Waterfall Cards

    Each Waterfall card contains:

    • Water Value
    • Influence Icons

    Some cards also have special actions:

    • PROXY
    • BUMP
    • PULL

    ---

    Score Cards

    Each Score card contains:

    • Starting Water Value
    • End Game Points

    ---

    Pools

    A Pool consists of:

    • 1 Score card
    • All Waterfall cards played to its right
    contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

    Setup

    1. Shuffle the Score cards.
    2. Create a Score deck based on player count:
    Players Score Cards
    2 10
    3 12
    4 14
    5 16
    6 20
    1. Reveal Score cards:
      • 1 per player
      • 3 cards in a 2-player game
    1. Arrange them vertically to create the starting Pools.
    1. Each player:
      • Takes one deck of 15 matching Waterfall cards
      • Shuffles their deck
      • Draws 5 cards
    1. Give the First Player card to whoever went swimming most recently.

    Play proceeds clockwise.

    contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

    Gameplay

    Frenzy Falls is played in rounds.

    Each round has two phases:

    • Playing Cards
    • Revealing Cards

    Phase 1: Playing Cards

    Starting with the first player and moving clockwise:

    • Play 1 Waterfall card face-down to the end of any Pool.

    Repeat until:

    • Each player has played 2 cards

    2-player game:

    • Each player instead plays 3 cards

    Cards may be played into any Pool.

    contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

    Phase 2: Revealing Cards

    After all cards have been played:

    • Start with the top-most Pool
    • Reveal cards from left to right

    When an action card is revealed:

    • Resolve its action immediately

    This phase consists of:

    1. Reveal and resolve cards
    2. Check for spillovers
    3. Proceed to next Pool
    contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

    Card Actions

    PROXY

    Discard this card and replace it with a card from your hand face-up.

    • If the replacement card has an action:
      • Resolve it immediately

    BUMP

    Move the card immediately to the left or right of this card into the Pool below.

    • Choose left or right
    • Move it to the end of the next Pool

    If this is the bottom Pool:

    • The card loops to the top Pool

    If no neighboring card exists:

    • Nothing happens

    PULL

    Move any one Waterfall card from another Pool into this Pool.

    • Choose any other Pool
    • Move the chosen card to the end of this Pool

    If no other cards exist:

    • Nothing happens
    contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

    Spillovers

    After all cards in a Pool are revealed:

    Calculate:

    Score Card Water Value + all Waterfall card values

    If the total is 10 or higher:

    👉 The Pool spills over.

    Resolve the spillover:

    1. Determine Influence

    Count influence icons for each player.

    • Most influence wins the Score card.

    Tie breaker:

    • Earliest card played (left-most) wins.

    Winner takes the Score card face-down.

    Replace it immediately with a new Score card.

    ---

    2. Winner Discards

    The winning player discards all of their cards from that Pool.

    ---

    3. Other Players Move Cards

    Starting with the earliest played card:

    Each other player:

    • Chooses one of their cards
    • Moves it to the Pool below

    Bottom Pool:

    • Cards loop to the top Pool

    ---

    4. Discard Remaining Cards

    All remaining cards are discarded.

    Players must reveal all cards before checking for spillovers.

    contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

    Proceed to the Next Pool

    After resolving a Pool:

    • Move to the next Pool below

    After the bottom Pool:

    • Return to the top Pool if needed

    Continue until:

    • No face-down cards remain
    • Every Pool has water value below 10

    Cards may cascade multiple times.

    contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

    Round End

    After all Pools are resolved:

    Each player:

    • May discard any remaining hand cards
    • Draws until they have exactly 5 cards

    If your deck runs out:

    • Shuffle your discard pile
    • Create a new draw deck

    Pass the First Player card clockwise.

    contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

    End of the Game

    If a Score card cannot be replaced:

    • Finish the current round
    • The game ends

    Players reveal their Score cards.

    Add up all points.

    Highest score wins.

    Tie breaker:

    1. Most Score cards
    2. Shared victory
    contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

    Strategy Tips

    • High-value Pools may be worth more points—but are easier to spill.
    • PROXY cards become stronger later in resolution.
    • BUMP and PULL can trigger chain reactions.
    • Sometimes losing a Pool is worth setting up the next one.
