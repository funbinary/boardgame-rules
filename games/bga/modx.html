    Modx

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Place X's to form patterns either with your own or combined with the Wild X's, but be careful as your score is placed directly on the playing board and if someone plays a pattern over it they steal the score from you!


    Turn

    Place a single X in any empty space

    If you make one of the three patterns ( X , + , - ) with 5 pieces of either all your own or your own mixed with Wild, you score (Note: the line shape can be vertical, horizonal, or diagonal)


    Patterns with all your own pieces

    Replace all the X's with score cubes

    Positions with Score cubes are NOT blocked, and so can have subsequent X's of any player placed on them

    If a player completes a pattern over a Score cube, the cube is replaced with that of the player who made the most recent pattern


    Patterns with Wilds

    This works more or less the same as above with a few exceptions

    You always have to use at least one of your X's in the pattern

    Only remove and place score cubes on your X's

    The Wild X's used are then each either left where they are or moved to an unoccupied space as long as said space would not create an instant scoring pattern for anyone's personal X's

    However, if you move the Wilds so as you form a pattern with exclusively Wilds, this is an instant win condition


    Combined Patterns

    If you place in such a way to complete two or more patterns in one go, you can claim both of them


    Game End

    The game ends in an instant win if a player completes a pattern with only wild pieces

    Otherwise, the game ends when a player meets or exceeds 15/12/10 points based on player count

    The game also ends when a player runs out of X pieces to place, in which case the player with the most points wins!


    Team rules

    A team's score is combined, and a team wins when they reach 12 points

    A team's pieces are NOT combined, so each member has to create their own patterns
