    Vivo

    Overview

    Vivo is a trick-taking card game of harmony, timing, and precise suit play.

    The game is played over 2 rounds, each consisting of 12 tricks.

    In each trick:

    • Players must collectively play the exact number of suits shown on the current Harmony card, if possible.
    • At the end of the trick:
      • The player who played the highest rank scores
      • The player who played the lowest rank scores

    After 2 rounds:

    • The player with the most points wins.

    Harmonize with other players to hit the right notes and play a perfect puppy performance!

    Components

    • 48 Playing cards
      • 4 suits
      • Ranks 1–12
    • 15 Harmony cards

    Card Types

    Playing Cards

    Each card has:

    • Suit
    • Rank

    Ranks range from:

    • 1–12

    ---

    Harmony Cards

    Harmony cards show how many suits must appear during the current trick.

    Possible harmonies:

    • Solo = 1 suit
    • Duet = 2 suits
    • Trio = 3 suits
    • Quartet = 4 suits

    Setup

    1. Grab a pen and paper for scoring.
    1. Remove cards based on player count.
    Players Remove
    3 Remove any 1 suit
    4 Use all 4 suits

    Harmony card adjustments:

    Players Remove Harmony Cards
    3 Remove 1 Solo, 1 Duet, 1 Trio
    4 Remove all 3 Quartets
    1. Shuffle the Harmony cards.
    1. Reveal 1 Harmony card face-up in the center.
    1. Place remaining Harmony cards in a face-down pile.
    1. Reveal the next Harmony card face-up beside the pile.
    1. Shuffle the Playing cards.
    1. Deal each player 12 cards.
    1. The player who most recently sang a song leads the first trick.

    Playing a Trick

    The lead player:

    • Plays any card from their hand.

    Then, moving clockwise:

    • Each player plays 1 card.

    Players must follow the harmony if possible.

    Following the Harmony

    On your turn:

    Count how many different suits have already been played this trick.

    === If the required number of suits has already been played:

    You must play:

    • One of the suits already played

    === If the required number of suits has NOT yet been played:

    You may play:

    • Any suit

    Provided the required number of suits can still be completed by the end of the trick.

    Forced New Suit

    If the harmony can only be completed by introducing a new suit:

    • You must play a new suit.

    Impossible Harmony

    If the required harmony cannot be completed regardless of what you play:

    • You must still play a new suit.

    Playing Off-Harmony

    If you have no cards that follow the harmony:

    • You must play off-harmony

    To play off-harmony:

    • Choose any card
    • Play it face-up as normal

    Important:

    • Off-harmony cards are ignored during scoring
    • Off-harmony players cannot score points

    Scoring a Trick

    After everyone has played:

    Ignore all off-harmony cards.

    Then resolve scoring:

    Score Highest

    The player who played the highest rank:

    • Flips their card face-down
    • Adds it to their scoring row

    Each facedown card will be worth:

    • 2 points

    Score Lowest

    The player who played the lowest rank:

    • Keeps their card face-up
    • Adds it to their scoring row

    Each face-up card is worth:

    • Its printed rank

    Discard

    All other played cards:

    • Are discarded face-down

    Each player keeps their scored cards in a scoring row.

    Tie Breakers

    If players tie for:

    • Highest rank

    OR

    • Lowest rank

    The player who played later in the trick wins the tie.

    All Cards Tie

    If all players tie:

    • The tie winner scores as highest rank
    • No player scores lowest rank

    Next Trick

    After scoring:

    1. Discard the current Harmony card.
    2. Replace it with the next Harmony card.
    3. Reveal the following Harmony card, if available.

    Next lead player:

    • The player who scored lowest rank

    If no player scored lowest:

    • The player who scored highest rank leads instead.

    Round End

    After 12 tricks:

    Each player totals their scoring row.

    Scoring:

    • Each facedown card = 2 points
    • Each face-up card = printed rank

    Record scores.

    If only 1 round has been played:

    • Set up and play a second round.

    First player of Round 2:

    • The player who scored lowest rank in the final trick of Round 1.

    Game End

    After Round 2:

    • Add both round scores.

    The player with the highest score wins.

    Tie:

    • Victory is shared.

    Clarifications

    • Players may NOT look at:
      • The discard pile
      • Opponents' facedown scored cards
    • In a Solo:
      • All players must play the same suit as the lead.
    • In a Trio with 3 players:
      • Every player must play a different suit.
    • In a Quartet with 4 players:
      • Every player must play a different suit.
    • If harmony becomes impossible:
      • You must still attempt to introduce a new suit.

    Strategy Tips

    • Going late in a trick gives tie-breaker advantage.
    • Sometimes playing low safely is better than chasing the high card.
    • Watch which suits have already appeared before committing.
    • Off-harmony hurts—protect your suit flexibility.
