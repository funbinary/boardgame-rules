    SUPER

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.1 · 排名 #19443 · 143人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    SUPER is a competitive card game where players manage superhero agencies, racing to reach 50 million followers by recruiting Supers and completing missions.

    Game Goal

    Be the first to reach or exceed 50 million followers by successfully completing missions with your recruited Supers.

    Components

    • 4 Agency boards (6 slots each for Super cards)
    • 56 Super cards (4 factions: blue, purple, red, green)
    • 42 Mission cards (5 types: blue, purple, red, green, gold)
    • Score dials, player aids, shock tokens, first player token

    Setup

    1. Each player gets an Agency board, player aid, and score dial (set to 00)
    2. Place 4 Super cards face-up in recruitment line
    3. Place 4 Mission cards face-up in mission line
    4. Last person to read a comic book goes first

    Turn Structure

    Each turn has two phases:

    Actions Phase

    A. Play a Super (Mandatory)

    • Choose one of the 4 face-up Supers from recruitment line
    • Place it on any slot in your Agency (can replace existing Supers)
    • Apply any immediate powers
    • First turn only: Each player must play 2 Supers

    B. Go on Mission (Optional)

    • Can be done multiple times per turn
    • Requirements:
      • - Strength: Combined strength of sent Supers must equal/exceed mission requirement
      • - Leader: At least one Super must match the mission's color (your choice for Rescue missions)
    • If you send 2+ Supers of the same faction, you gain an additional Partner Bonus
    • Used Supers are discarded after mission

    End of Turn

    • Refill empty slots with new Super and Mission cards
    • If Super deck is empty, shuffle discard pile

    Special Mechanics

    Bad Buzz

    Once per turn, before playing a Super, pay 2 million followers to discard all 4 face-up Supers and reveal 4 new ones.

    Shock Tokens

    Can disable opponent Supers' powers (they keep faction but lose strength/abilities).

    Mission Types

    Mission Type Effect
    Villain Attack Gain immediate followers
    Chemical Hazard Apply shock effects
    Destruction Threat Gain followers based on completed Destruction missions
    Alien Invasion Recruitment effects and ongoing faction bonuses
    Rescue Mission Gain followers based on variety of completed mission types

    Super Powers

    Immediate Powers (♦)

    • Popular: Gain X followers immediately
    • Recruitment: Draw and place a Super from deck immediately

    Permanent Powers (◆)

    • Veteran: Can return to bottom row instead of being discarded after missions
    • Shapeshifter: Counts as all factions simultaneously
    • Strategist: 1 discount on all mission types
    • Partner: Triggers partner bonus even without same-faction allies
    • Power Up: Gains +X strength for each specified faction Super in your agency
    • Influential: Gain followers when specified faction Supers are placed
    • Ready to Go: Gain followers when specified faction Supers go on missions

    Support Powers

    • Faction Support: Makes another Super count as additional faction
    • Strength Support: Gives another Super +X strength

    Game End

    The game ends when:

    1. A player reaches 50+ million followers (finish the round), OR
    2. Mission line can't be fully refilled

    Winner: Player with most followers (ties are shared victories)

    Key Strategy Elements

    • Balance immediate vs. long-term gains
    • Consider faction synergies and partner bonuses
    • Manage Super placement strategically
    • Time mission completion for maximum efficiency
    • Use shock tokens to disrupt opponents

    FAQ

    Q: Can I go on a Mission with just one Super?
    A: Yes, as long as its color matches the Mission and its power is equal to or greater than the Mission requirement.

    Q: Do powers that affect a faction also apply to the Super with that power if it matches the faction?
    A: Yes.

    Q: Can I apply effects on a card with a SHOCK token?
    A: Yes. The SUPER card loses its SHOCK token and regains its power.

    Notes

    The game combines tactical card play with resource management, requiring players to build efficient superhero teams while adapting to changing recruitment and mission opportunities.
