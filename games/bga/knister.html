    Overview

    In every round, all players write down the sum of both dice (2-12) on their own score sheet in the blue 5x5 grid.

    As you play the sum of a pair of dice in a row / column / diagonal row, score them.

    You get double points for whatever you score on your two diagonals.

    After 25 rounds, all 5x5 grids are finished. Sum up the score. The player with highest score wins.

    Finish a row / column / diagonal row

    Score them with the combination on the left of the sheet.

    The numbers can appear in any order in the row / column / diagonal. For example, "a straight without a 7" can be be 52346 or 10,8,12,9,11.

    Diagonal rows are worth double points

    2er (pair) = 1 point

    3er (Three of a kind) = 3 points

    4er (Four of a kind) = 6 points

    5er (Five of a kind) = 10 points

    2er + 2er (Two pairs) = 3 points

    Full House (2 of one number and 3 of a different dice total, for example 69969) = 8 points

    Str 7 (Straight with 7, for example 45678) = 8 points

    Str 7 (Straight without 7 for example ) = 12 points

    Solo results

    The publisher lists these tiers for solo play:

    50-79: Good

    80-99: Very good

    100+: Amazing

    It appears that anything over 50 is a win in BGA's opinion
