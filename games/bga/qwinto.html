    Qwinto

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Game Play

    On your turn, roll 1–3 dice matching the row colors. Everyone may use the total to fill a shape in a row that matches one of the dice colors, following two rules:

    1. Numbers must increase from left to right in a row.
    2. No number can repeat in a vertical column.

    You may skip placing a number, but the active player (the roller) must take a penalty if they do.

    Game End

    The game ends when someone fills two rows or marks four misthrows.

    Scoring

    Scoring is based on:

    • Completed rows (score the rightmost number),
    • Completed columns (score the pentagon),
    • Partial rows (1 point per number),
    • Misthrows (-5 points each).

    Highest score wins!
