    Jackpot: Set The Reel

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    👥 1–4 人 ⭐ BGG 6.2 · 排名 #13347 · 178人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Jackpot: Set The Reel is a 'slot building' game for 1-4 players. Through card manipulation and purchases, you will improve your hand of cards throughout the game to score more and more Jackpots.

    Objective

    Your goal is to score as many points as possible by collecting ⭐

    ⭐are gained from:

    • Jackpot cards (scored immediately)
    • Bonus tokens (scored immediately, and also worth 1VP at the end of the game if unspent)
    • 7 cards (from the Market, scored at the end of the game)
    • Upgraded cards (scored at the end of the game)

    Gameplay

    Your Turn

    Play 3 adjacent cards from your hand. Played cards are then assessed from left to right. For matching symbols (matching the leftmost card), gain the following:

    • 1 symbol = 1 coin
    • 2 symbols = Bonus token
    • 3 symbols = Jackpot card

    On your turn you may also upgrade or buy cards with coins.

    Pass 10th Card

    Once you have taken 3 turns, you will pass your 10th card 'clockwise'.

    Gain Income

    Then, gain income from 1 column of cards.

    Reassemble Hand

    Finally, reassemble your hand by taking the columns into your hand one at a time.

    Game End

    The game ends when one player reaches 30VP, then any additional VP (from gold stars) is added.

    Coins

    Coins can be spent to upgrade cards (flip them to the other side) or buy new cards from the market.

    Upgrading Cards

    Cards can be upgraded by spending coins, which flips them to their other side.

    The reverse side of each card is shown on the left and right side of each card. On Board Game Arena, you can hover over the card to see the reverse side.

    Buying cards

    Cards in the market are limited - one of each type per player. For example, in a 2 player game, there are only 2 Bells, 2 Cherries, 2 Grapes, 2 Watermelons, and 2 '7' cards in the market.

    Bonus tokens

    Bonus tokens are gained when your leftmost and middle cards match, or when you gain the reward from the last Jackpot card of each type. They have special effects which you can use on your turn:

    Effect
    Bonus token effect
    		Gain 2 Coins Gain 4 Coins
    		Gain 2 ⭐ Gain 4 ⭐
    		Move a card in your hand to another position in your hand Move 2 cards in your hand to other positions in your hand
    		Treat 1 of your played cards as WILD Treat 2 of your played cards as WILD
    		Upgrade a card in your hand with a discount of (up to) 3 Coins Upgrade 2 cards in your hand, each with a discount of (up to) 3 Coins

    Jackpot cards

    Jackpot cards are gained when you play 3 cards of the same type. The final card, which shows a 'do not take' symbol is never taken so is available for all players to score.

    Jackpot cards descend in value depending on player count:

    • 1/2 players: 5⭐, 3⭐, 1⭐ and a bonus token
    • 3 players: 5⭐, 4⭐, 3⭐, 3⭐, 1⭐ and a bonus token
    • 4 players: 5⭐, 4⭐, 4⭐, 3⭐, 3⭐, 1⭐ and a bonus token

    Pass 10th Card

    After you have played 3 turns (9 cards) you will pass your remaining card clockwise, and receive another card in turn from your other neighbor. You will add this card to one of your columns.

    Income

    Gain Income from 1 column of cards, from the coins showing at the top of the cards

    Reassemble Hand

    Reassemble your hand by taking the columns into your hand one at a time.
