    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    Players and stones

    Connect 6 is a two player game. Each player plays with stones, as in Go and Gomoku.

    Game board

    Connect6 is typically played on a go board.

    Moves

    Black plays first, putting one black stone on one intersection, then White and Black take turns to place two stones on unoccupied intersections each turn.

    Winner

    The player who is the first to make a line of at least six stones (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins!
