    Abrachadabra

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 5.8 · 排名 #19233 · 236人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Winning condition

    The game ends when a player has 22 points or more. The player with the most points wins.

    Gameplay

    The game starts in the day. When an exam ends, the phase switches from day to night, or vice versa.

    Each player follows these two steps in turn, until no one is left to play for this exam.

    Then, all players who do not lose their exam score points accordingly.

    On Your Turn

    Draw a single card from the 4 piles of magic cards and place them face-up.

    Instead of doing so, a player can use a vision potion to look at 2 cards and choose 1 to draw.

    Validate a Diploma

    If you have the exact number of rabbits corresponding to the value of one of the available diploma, you may take it. Doing so ends the exam.

    A diploma is worth as many points as its value.

    During the day, diploma of values 4 to 8 are available. During the night, diploma of values 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11 are available.

    Effects

    Rabbit

    You can obtain a diploma if you have exactly as many rabbits as the value of that diploma.

    If you have more rabbits than the value of the biggest diploma, you will lose your exam.

    Black Cat

    Each black cat is worth 1 point at the end of the exam.

    If you have 3 or more black cats, you will lose your exam.

    Wool Ball

    The wool ball takes up one cat, so you can have more and keep its bonus points.

    Vision Potion

    Secretly look at two cards from the top of a stack of magic cards. Keep one and put the other in its original place.

    Grimoire

    When you draw the grimoire, take the feather. If someone else has the feather, steal it from them.

    Feather

    The feather is the only card that can be stolen from you.

    It is worth 3 points at the end of the exam.

    Pile Distribution

    Each pile has 12 cards.

    Green: 1 Rabbit (x7), 1 Black Cat (x3), Grimoire (x2)

    Yellow: 2 Rabbits (x5), 1 Black Cat (x4), 1 Black Cat + Grimoire (x1), Grimoire (x1), Vision Potion (x1)

    Red: 3 Rabbits (x3), 1 Black Cat (x4), 1 Black Cat + Grimoire (x2), Vision Potion (x3)

    Blue: 1 Rabbit (x1), 2 Rabbits (x1), 3 Rabbits (x1), 4 Rabbits (x1), 2 Black Cats (x1), 1 Black Cat (x3), 1 Black Cat + Grimoire (x2), Wool Ball (x2)
