    Girafferaffe

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 5.7 · 排名 #23404 · 权重 1.00/5 · 153人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Play tricks to Trees, or play Apples to skip your turn, but be careful to not put yourself in a position where you cannot play cards or an Apple


    Turn

    Either play one or more cards to a tree as long as your play is higher than what is already there and gain a reward

    Or you can play an apple and skip your turn


    Game End

    If someone cannot take their turn, they lose!
