    Sinoda

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 8.6 · 排名 #12920 · 92人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Upgrade and Downgrade pieces to enable you to move longer and shorter distances, but be warned that the higher the value of a piece the more it is worth to your opponents!


    Turn

    On your turn, either Move or Rotate

    If you Rotate, change the value of one of your pieces 1 stage up or down, but there is no wrap-around i.e. a 4 cannot become a 1

    If you Move, pick one of your pieces and move it a number of non-occupied spaces equal to its value exactly i.e. if you have a 3, it has to move 3 spaces (You may not move through the same space multiple times in one turn)

    If a Move ends you on an occupied enemy space, you capture this piece, there is no relevance between your piece's value and your opponent's piece's value


    Game End

    There are three ways to end the game:

    All players only have 1 piece remaining

    One player has no pieces remaining

    One player has 6 pieces captured

    Regardless of how the end game was triggered, each player adds up the value of all the opponent pieces they have captured, the player with the highest value wins!

    If tied, the player with the most pieces still on the board wins!
