    Personanongrata

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    The Game Round

    - All players play simultaneously.

    - Each turn (day), players select one Information card and one Action card and place them face down. After revealing, they execute their actions:

    • Download: Reveal and place the Information card in your play area.
    • Encrypt: Place the Information card face down in your play area.
    • Send (Left or Right): Reveal and place the Information card in the player’s play area to your left or right, respectively.

    - Players pass remaining Information cards to the next player (clockwise/counterclockwise based on the week).

    - The week ends after all Action cards are used. Last card in hand is discarded.

    Archiving Information

    - Reveal all Encrypted cards.

    - Compare relevance points of Information cards for each corporation.

    - First-place player archives the top Corporation card; second-place player takes the Key card.

    - If a tie occurs, the player with the Key decides the winner.

    End of the Game

    - The game ends after three weeks.

    - All remaining Information cards are archived.

    - Players gather archived cards, activating corporations with an Activator (any Corporation card or Worker).

    - Score based on activated cards and Key cards (1 point each).

    - The player with the highest reputation points wins. Tiebreakers are the number of activated corporations, then Key cards, and finally sharing the victory.
