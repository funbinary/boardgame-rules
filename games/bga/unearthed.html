    Unearthed

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Unearthed Overview

    In Unearthed, players lead archaeological expeditions, carefully uncovering artifacts while trying to avoid worthless rubble. Each player builds a grid of excavation sites and gradually reveals what lies beneath.

    Rare discoveries reduce your score, but careless digging can leave you with piles of debris. Timing when to reveal your final site is crucial — end the expedition at the right moment to avoid a costly mistake.

    The archaeologist with the lowest total score wins.

    Setup

    Shuffle all cards to form the excavation deck.

    Deal 12 cards to each player.

    Each player arranges their cards face-down in a 4 × 3 grid.

    Each player flips two random cards face-up in their grid.

    Flip the top card of the deck to start the discard pile.

    The player whose two face-up cards have the highest total value takes the first turn.

    Card Values

    The deck contains:

    5 cards of value –2

    10 cards of value –1

    15 cards of value 0

    10 cards each of values 1–12

    Negative cards represent valuable discoveries, which reduce your score.

    Turn Structure

    On your turn, perform the following steps:

    1. Draw a Card

    Choose one:

    Draw the top card from the draw pile (you may look at it before deciding what to do).

    Take the top card from the discard pile.

    2. Choose One Action Keep the Card

    Place the drawn card into your grid by replacing any card (face-up or face-down).

    The replaced card is placed face-up on the discard pile.

    Discard the Card

    Place the drawn card onto the discard pile, then:

    Flip one face-down card in your grid face-up.

    Ending the Round

    A round ends when a player reveals their final face-down card.

    After this happens, each player takes one final turn, including the player who revealed the last card.

    Once these turns are complete, the round ends.

    Scoring

    At the end of the round:

    All remaining face-down cards are revealed.

    Each player adds together the values of all cards in their grid.

    Negative values reduce your total score.

    Expedition Bonus

    The player who ended the round receives a bonus:

    If they have the lowest score of the round, their score becomes 0.

    If they do not have the lowest score, their score is doubled (unless the score is 0 or less).

    End of the Game

    Play multiple rounds until a player reaches 100 points or more.

    At that point, the game ends and the player with the lowest total score wins.

    Strategy Tips

    Revealing cards early helps control your grid.

    Valuable discoveries (negative cards) are rare — protect them.

    Ending the round at the right moment can avoid dangerous high-value cards.
