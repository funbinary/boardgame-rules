    Valeofeternity

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Goal

    Score 60 points at the end of a round or have the the most points at the end of round 10 by summoning creatures from your hand

    Core Concepts

    Cards

    There is no hand limit. The maximum number of cards a player can have in their summoning area is equal to the current round. Additional spots are added every round, cards may also be removed from the summoning area by spending magic stones. A card has a family, which determines how much it can be sold for and other effects. Card images can be seen at higher resolution at https://valeofeternity.wiki.gg/wiki/All_Cards .

    Each card has one of 3 types of effect:

    • Instant (lightning bolt ⚡︎): Triggers only when the card is being summoned.
    • Permanent (infinity ∞): Activates always after the card has been summoned.
    • Active (hourglass ⌛︎): Activates once per round during the Resolution Phase.

    Magic Stones

    Magic Stones are the currency used to summon and remove cards. A Red Stone is worth 1, a Blue Stone is worth 3, a Purple Stone is worth 6.

    Magic stones are limited to a quantity of 4 regardless of their value (e.g. 4 red 1s or 4 purple 6s). If a player exceeds this limit, they must keep 4 magic stones and discard the rest.    This decision must be made before taking any other actions.

    Magic Stones cannot be freely traded to make room for others, e.g. if you have 4 red 1s, you cannot trade 3 of them for 1 blue 3. If a player overspends their magic stones, they cannot make change e.g. using a purple 6 for something that costs 4 does not grant you 2 red 1s back.

    Points

    Due to various effects, players can gain or lose points throughout the game, although minimum score at any time is 0 (i.e. no negatives). The Game ends when a player has 60 points at the end of a round or at the end of the 10th round.

    Hunting Phase

    To start the round, each player drafts two cards

    First card is drafted clockwise from start player, second card is drafted anticlockwise from last player (this means the last player drafts both their cards at once essentially)

    Turn

    On your turn, you take as many of the actions as many times as desired

    After all players have done this, the action phase ends

    The actions are: Sell, Tame, Summon, Remove

    Sell - Pick a card you chose during the Hunting Phase and discard it, receive Magic Stones based on its family (this is NOT its cost). Red Fire gives 3 Red Stones, Blue Water gives 1 Blue Stones, Green Earth gives 4 Red Stones, Pink Wind gives 1 Red & 1 Blue stone, Purple Dragon gives 1 Purple Stone

    Tame - Pick a card you chose during the Hunting Phase and put it into your hand

    Summon - If you have open areas, you may pay the cost of a card and place it into your playing area, this cost may be reduced by the effect of cards already in your playing area. After which, resolve any instant effects if it has one.

    Remove - You may remove a card from your own playing area at the cost of the current round, e.g. in round 6 the cost is 6

    Resolution Phase

    In turn order, each player can activate each active effect on their cards in their playing area once each, in any order. Card effects must be used if player meets the card criteria.

    Recover is a keyword that puts a summoned card back in the owner’s hand. If no specific target is described, the card recovers itself.

    Game End

    The game ends if at end of round a player reaches 60 points or it is the 10th round, if neither of these happen then start a new round with a new start player

    The player with the most points wins!

    If tied, the player with the most summoned cards wins!
