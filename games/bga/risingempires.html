    Risingempires

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview:

    The game is played with 5 kingdom cards in the middle of the table and players on each side of the table. Players add cards to either their army's side or their opponent's side. The victory goes to the biggest army for each kingdom. Each kingdom has requirements it needs in order to be played on.
