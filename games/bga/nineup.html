    Nineup

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Shuffle the deck. Deal 9 cards to each player and 1 card face up as a discard pile. Arrange cards in a 3x3 grid. Randomly choose a starting player. On your turn choose one action and proceed clockwise.

    FLIP one of your face down cards face up, triggering any card abilities.

    EXCHANGE one face down card with another players face down card. You may peek at the acquired card but it must remain face down.

    DRAW a card from the deck or discard pile and exchange it (face up) with one of your face down cards, discarding it face up. *Note this action does not trigger card abilities.

    When any player has all 9 cards face up the round ends immediately. Any remaining face down cards are flipped (with no effect). Score all cards. The card in the center is worth double. Shuffle the deck, deal new cards and begin a new round starting with the player with the lowest score. The player with the most points after 3 rounds is the winner. Tied players share the victory.

    SCORING

    All cards count as single value except the card in the middle which is worth double. No points are scored if the card is adjacent to a zero card in the direction of the arrow.

    SPECIAL ABILITY CARDS

    1 point - Exchange another of your face up cards with another player's face up or face down card. Peek if it is a face down card (four cards).

    3 points - Discard any face up card. Deal that player a new card face down (four cards).

    9 points - Draw a card, reveal it and exchange it with another player's face down card (four cards).

    10 points - Exchange this card with another player's face down card. Peek at this card (four cards).

    CARDS WITH ZERO POINTS

    Zero, and zero for a card below - one card.

    Zero, and zero for a card to the left - one card.

    Zero, and zero for a card to the right - one card.

    Zero, and zero for a card above - one card.

    CARDS WITH POINTS

    -5 points - four cards.

    -10 points - two cards.

    2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10 points - four cards each.

    Number of cards are for the standard deck of 54 cards. Two decks are used for games of 5 to 8 players.
