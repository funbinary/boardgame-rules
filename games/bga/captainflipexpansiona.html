    Captainflipexpansiona

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    How to Play:

    Players take turns recruiting crew members to their adventure board to earn coins.

    On your turn:

    1. Draw 1 Character tile from the bag, looking at only one side.

    2. Choose to keep it as is or flip it to the other side (if you flip, you must keep the new Character).

    3. Place the tile on the lowest empty space of any column on your board.

    4. Resolve effects:

    Apply the Character’s immediate effect, if it has one.

    If the tile is placed at the top of a column, also apply any immediate bonus shown on that space.

    Players continue clockwise, starting with the player who has the board with the pirate flag.

    End of the Game:

    • When a player completes four columns, finish the round. Then:
    • Apply all endgame effects of Characters.
    • Count coins (scoring one column at a time).
    • The player with the most coins wins.

    In case of a tie, the player with the Treasure Map wins; otherwise, the victory is shared.
