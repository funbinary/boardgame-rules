    Cross

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 7.0 · 排名 #0 · 权重 2.00/5 · 25人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    About

    Embark on an exhilarating journey with this captivating 2-player board game, where strategic thinking and cunning tactics hold the key to victory.

    Your ultimate objective is to outwit your opponent and successfully navigate your way to the opposite side of the board before they can claim the same prize.

    Goal

    Cross the board before your opponent does!

    Rules

    Players have to play connections to make a path connecting their two sides of the board.

    One of the players has to connect the top with the bottom, while the other player has to connect left to right.

    Each turn, a player places one connection. These connections are made between two dots of the players color. A connection can not cross an already existing connection.

    The first player to make a path between their sides wins the game!

    Random initialisation variant

    This option allows for a few random moves to be made randomly before starting the game, introducing a bit of randomness to the game.

    Bridges variant

    This variant gives a slight chance for the players to receive a bridge. Players can choose to play this bridge or to change it for a regular connection. A bridge can be played over an existing connection of your opponent.
