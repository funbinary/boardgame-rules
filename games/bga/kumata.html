    Kumata

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Game Overview

    In Kumata, different animal clans compete for dominance in the jungle. By tactically stacking dominoes, players try to keep their clan symbols visible on the highest levels. It is a vertical tile-placement game where height translates directly into points.

    Goal of the Game

    Score the most points through your visible clan symbols and your placed totem marker at the end of the game.

    Setup Details

    • 3-4 Players: Use 10 dominoes per clan (Monkey, Snake, Toucan, or Tiger), split into two face-up stacks of 5.
    • 2 Players: Use the Hippo and Crocodile clans (15 dominoes each, three stacks of 5). Flip board tiles to the 2-player side.

    Gameplay

    On your turn, you must perform exactly one of these two actions:

    1. Place a Domino

    Take the top domino from one of your stacks and place it.

    • Level 1: Must be placed on two empty board squares.
    • Higher Levels (Stability Rule): A domino can never perfectly cover a single domino below it. It must always bridge across or touch at least two different dominoes/squares underneath.
    • Matching values: Each half of the domino must cover a matching value. It doesn't matter what color it is (including your own).

    2. Place the Totem (only once per game)

    Instead of placing a domino, place your totem on an empty half of one of your dominoes already on the board.

    • Protection: A space with a totem can no longer be built over.

    Scoring Specifics

    Element Points at Game End
    Visible Domino Halfs You score the visible points from your clan’s halves
    Empty Halves Domino halves without a symbol score 0 points, regardless of their level.
    The Totem Scores points equal to the level it sits on +1 (e.g., Level 3 = 4 pts).
    Penalty If the totem is still in your reserve at the end, subtract 1 point.

    Game End

    The game ends immediately when the active player cannot make a legal move. No other players take further turns.

    Variants

    • Maze Mode: Arrange the 9 board tiles freely (connected by at least one side) instead of a 3x3 grid.
    • Alliance Mode (4 Players): Play 2 vs 2. Teammates sit opposite each other and combine their final scores.
