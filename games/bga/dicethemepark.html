    Dicethemepark

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    🎯 Goal

    Score the most stars ⭐ by efficiently operating attractions in your theme park.

    Guests are dice: you move, modify, and exhaust them to run attractions and score points.

    🔄 Round Overview

    The game is played over 4 rounds (or 5 in the full game).

    Each round has 6 phases:

    1️⃣ Plan (simultaneous)

    • Play 2 staff cards.
    • These determine:
      • Turn order (lower dice total = earlier)
      • Income (money icons)
      • Staff abilities you can use this round

    2️⃣ Welcome (turn order)

    • Choose a monorail.
    • Take its 3 guest dice and place them at your Park Entrance.
    • Some staff can boost arriving dice.

    3️⃣ Expand (turn order, optional)

    You may:

    • Build 1 attraction (pay cost, place adjacent)
    • Recruit 1 mascot (£5)
      • Full game: also gain a skill card

    Matching amenities across your park can give instant bonuses.

    4️⃣ Improve (simultaneous, optional)

    You may:

    • Buy 1 map (£3)
    • Install up to 2 upgrades:
      • 💰 Merch kiosk → +£2 per operation
      • ⭐ Special effect → +2⭐ per operation
      • ⚙ Generator → operate attraction twice per round

    5️⃣ Operate (simultaneous – main phase)

    Run your park in any order.

    To operate an attraction, always do these steps:

    1. Place an operate cube
    2. Assemble a party of dice that meet the attraction’s requirement
    3. Pip down all used dice (–1 each; dice at 1 are discarded)
    4. Move one used die to an adjacent attraction

    Helpful tools:

    • Maps move dice between attractions
    • Mascots adjust die value or colour (or use skills)
    • Generators allow extra operations

    👉 You don’t score immediately — everything is counted later.

    6️⃣ Upkeep (simultaneous)

    • Score stars and money from operated attractions
    • Apply upgrade bonuses
    • Check the round objective (mid/late game)
    • Discard all value-1 dice
    • Reset staff, maps, mascots, and refill monorails

    🏁 End of Game

    • Gain 1⭐ per £3 remaining
    • Highest ⭐ wins

    Ties:

    1️⃣ Most guest dice remaining

    2️⃣ Most money

    3️⃣ Shared victory

    🧠 Key Things to Remember

    • Dice wear out as you use them
    • Moving dice is mandatory when operating
    • Efficient chaining of attractions is the key to high scores
    • Staff planning matters as much as park layout
