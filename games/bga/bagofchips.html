    Bagofchips

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Be the first to receive four rewards


    Turn

    Each round has 4 phases followed by scoring, players are aiming to keep cards which have objectives they feel are obtainable


    Phase 1

    FIVE chips are added to the first zone

    Each player discards TWO objectives in hand, these will NOT be scored


    Phase 2

    FOUR chips are added to the second zone

    Each player discards ONE objective in hand, this will NOT be scored


    Phase 3

    THREE chips are added to the third zone

    Each player places TWO objectives facedown on the RIGHT of their discard pile and ONE objective facedown on the LEFT of their discard pile

    Once everyone have placed, these cards are flipped faceup

    Any objectives which are impossible to complete are discarded immediately


    Phase 4

    TWO chips are added to the fourth zone

    Any objectives which are now impossible to complete are discarded


    Scoring

    Objectives which remain on the RIGHT of your discard pile give you POSITIVE points

    Objectives which remain on the LEFT of your discard pile give you NEGATIVE points

    The difference between your positive and negative is your score

    The player with the highest score gains 2 Rewards (if tied, 2 Rewards each and no 2nd place)

    The player with the 2nd highest gains 1 Reward (if tied, 1 Reward each)

    If someone now has 4 Rewards, end game is triggered

    Otherwise, reset for another round

    If end game was triggered, the player with the most rewards at the end of that round wins! (it's possible to have 5 Rewards)

    If tied, the player who won the end round wins!
