    Toeshambo

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 4.8 · 排名 #30855 · 权重 1.17/5 · 230人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    A player wins the game when one of the following occurs:

    • The player gets three of their cards with the same symbol in a row or a column or diagonal;
    • The other player cannot make a move on their turn, or;
    • Both players have played all of their cards, and that player has more visible cards on the board.

    Composition of each deck

    • 8 scissors
    • 8 papers
    • 8 rocks
    • 1 bomb
    • 2 undos

    Gameplay

    • Each player takes a deck, shuffles, and draws 3 cards.
    • The players play 'rock, paper, scissors' to decide who goes first.
    • On your turn, play a card to the 3x3 grid, and then draw a card.
    • You can play a card on top of another card if your card beats the other card. Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper and paper beats rock.
    • An undo card removes the most recently played card from the game.
    • A bomb card removes all the cards under it, and disappears the next time a card is played.
    • Play until a player completes an objective. That player wins.
