    Lovelettermf

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Card Reference (Quick Guide)

    Each card has a value (1–9) and a unique effect triggered when played:

    • 9 – Princess: If you discard this card (by choice or force), you are immediately out of the round.
    • 8 – Countess: You must play this card if you also have the King (7) or Prince (5) in your hand.
    • 7 – King: Trade your hand card with another player of your choice.
    • 6 – Chancellor: Draw two cards. Keep one of the three cards in your hand (the two drawn + your original) and put the other two back on the bottom of the deck.
    • 5 – Prince: Choose any player (including yourself) to discard their hand and draw a new one.
    • 4 – Handmaid: You are protected from all effects of other players' cards until your next turn.
    • 3 – Baron: Secretly compare hands with another player. The player with the lower value is out of the round.
    • 2 – Priest: Secretly look at another player's hand.
    • 1 – Guard: Name a non-Guard card and choose a player. If they have that card, they are out of the round.
    • 0 – Spy: If you are the only player still in the round who played or discarded a Spy, you gain 1 bonus point (token) at the end of the round.

    Objective

    The goal is to deliver your love letter to the Princess. You win a round by having the highest-value card at the end or being the last player remaining.

    Gameplay

    The game is played in quick turns clockwise:

    1. Draw: Start your turn by drawing one card from the deck (you now have two in hand).
    2. Play: Choose one of your two cards and play it face-up in front of you.
    3. Resolve: Apply the card's effect immediately (see the list above).
      • If all opponents are protected by a Handmaid, your card effect fails.
      • If an effect forces you to choose a target and everyone else is protected, you must choose yourself if possible (e.g., Prince).

    Round End & Scoring

    A round ends immediately if:

    1. The deck is empty: All remaining players compare their hand cards. The highest value wins.
    2. Only one player remains: If everyone else is knocked out, the last person standing wins.

    The winner receives a Token of Affection (point). The first player to reach the required number of tokens (based on player count) wins the game.
