    Toepen

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 5.7 · 排名 #24095 · 权重 1.63/5 · 95人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Game Overview

    In the lively taverns of the Netherlands, players gather for Toepen, a fast-paced trick-taking game for 3 to 8 people. Thematically, it is about outsmarting opponents through strategic bluffing and timely knocking to raise the stakes and force others to fold. Mechanically, players must manage 4 hand cards effectively to win the crucial final trick and avoid losing their precious lives.

    Goal of the Game

    The goal is to win the 4th (last) trick of a round. The player who wins this trick is safe, while all other players lose lives according to the current stake.

    Gameplay

    Dealing and Preparation

    Each player is dealt 4 cards from a 32-card deck. The ranking is unique (high to low): 10, 9, 8, 7, Ace, King, Queen, Jack.

    Exchanging Cards

    Players holding only Face cards or Aces may discard them face down and draw new ones. Opponents may challenge this; if a player is caught lying (holding a 7 through 10), they lose 1 life immediately.

    Trick-Taking

    The eldest hand leads the first trick. Players must follow suit if possible. If unable to follow suit, any card may be played. The winner of a trick leads the next one. The winner of the 4th trick wins the round.

    Special Actions (Knocking)

    Action Description Effect
    Knocking (Toep!) A player knocks on the table. Increases the stake by 1 point.
    Stay An opponent accepts the knock. They continue playing for the higher penalty.
    Fold An opponent rejects the knock. They leave the round immediately and lose the previous (lower) stake.

    Game End & Scoring

    • Players typically start with 10 to 15 lives.
    • A player is eliminated when they reach 0 lives.
    • The last remaining player wins the game.
    • Poverty: A player with only 1 life remaining cannot knock but can still be knocked upon.
