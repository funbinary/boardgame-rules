    Boomerangusa

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.8 · 排名 #4353 · 840人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Gameplay

    • The game consists of 4 rounds. At the start of each round, deal 7 cards to each player. The others are not used for the round.
    • On your turn, pick a card you want, then pass the rest to the left. All cards drafted are not revealed.
    • After 7 turns, all hands are depleted and drafted cards are scored. Afterwards, shuffle all 28 cards and start a new round.

    Scoring

    • Throw and Catch: The first card drafted in a round is the throw, and the last card drafted is the catch. If the catch is equal to or higher than the throw, score points equal to the throw.
    • Tourist Sites: Cross out all sights visited each round. At the end of the game, each sight is worth 1 point. If two visited towns have a connecting path, draw it. The first to connect from east coast to west coast scores 7, then 3, then 1. Besides, the first player to visit all towns in a region scores 3 points.
    • Americana: Total all scores on icons. If they are higher than the score last round, score them. Otherwise they are worth 0.
    • Animals: For each pair of animals scores the points marked.
    • Activities: Choose an unscored activity to score the number of icons (0/2/4/7/10/15).

    End of the Game

    The game ends after 4 rounds. The player with the highest score wins. If there is a tie, the player with the highest throw and catch wins.

    Variants

    • Direction Variant: Alternate passing to the left and to the right each round.
