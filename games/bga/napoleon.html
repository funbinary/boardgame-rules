    🃏 Napoleon - Basic Rule Guide

    Napoleon is a highly strategic trick-taking game for 5 players, originating from Japan. Players are divided into the Napoleon's Army (2 players) and the Allied Forces (3 players).

    🏆 Game Overview

    • Deck: 52 standard cards + 1 Joker = 53 cards in total.
    • Honor Cards: A, K, Q, J, 10 (20 cards in total). The goal is to collect these cards.
    • Victory Conditions:
      • Napoleon's Army: Collect at least the number of Honor Cards declared during the bidding.
      • Allied Forces: Prevent Napoleon's Army from reaching their declared target.

    👑 Game Flow

    1. Bidding

    Players take turns declaring how many Honor Cards they can collect if they become "Napoleon."

    • Bid Range: 10 to 20 cards.
    • Bid Content: Number of cards and the desired Trump suit (e.g., "13, Spades").
    • Bidding continues until someone makes the highest bid or everyone else passes.
    • All Pass Scenario: If everyone passes, the dealer (first bidder) is forced to become Napoleon with a bid of "10, Clubs".

    2. Card Exchange

    The winner of the bid (Napoleon) takes the 3 face-down cards from the center (the "Widow") and discards 3 cards from their hand. These 3 discarded cards are revealed to all players.

    • Important: If any Honor Cards are included in the discarded 3 cards, they are awarded to the winner of the first trick.

    3. Adjudant Nomination

    Napoleon nominates an "Adjudant Card" to choose their secret partner.

    • Example: "The Ace of Spades" or "The Queen of Hearts (Yoromeki)."
    • The player holding that card becomes the secret partner, the "Adjudant."
    • Napoleon must fight alone (Solo Napoleon) if they hold the nominated card themselves.
    • If the nominated card is among the 3 cards in the Widow or among the cards Napoleon discarded, Napoleon also becomes a Solo Napoleon.

    4. Trick-Taking

    Starting from the player to the left of Napoleon, players play one card clockwise.

    • Must Follow: You must play a card of the same suit as the lead card if you have one.
    • If you are void in the lead suit, you may play any card (including Trump cards or other suits).
    • Leading with Joker: If a player leads with the Joker, they specify a suit that others must follow for that trick.
    • The strongest card wins the 5 cards in that trick and leads the next trick.

    🔥 Card Strength (from Strongest)

    1. 🂱 Mighty: ♠A. Always the strongest card in the game.
    2. 🃏 Joker: The second strongest card.
    3. 🂻 Right Jack: The Jack of the Trump suit.
    4. 🂻 Left Jack: The Jack of the same color as the Trump suit.
      • If Trump is ♠ (Spades), ♣J is the Left Jack.
      • If Trump is ♣ (Clubs), ♠J is the Left Jack.
      • If Trump is ♥ (Hearts), ♦J is the Left Jack.
      • If Trump is ♦ (Diamonds), ♥J is the Left Jack.
    5. Trump Cards: A, K, Q, J, 10, 9...
    6. Lead Suit Cards: A, K, Q, J, 10, 9... of the suit matching the first card played in the trick.

    ⚠️ Special Rules

    💃 The Queen of Hearts (Yoromeki)

    • Card: ♥Q.
    • Effect: Activates only if the Mighty (♠A) is played in the same trick.
    • Result: The ♥Q defeats the Mighty and becomes the strongest card. In this scenario, the Mighty becomes the weakest card in the trick.

    ♣️3 Joker Request

    • Condition: If the ♣3 is played as the lead card of a trick.
    • Effect: The player holding the Joker must play it in this trick.

    🦈 Same-Two

    • Card: The "2" of the lead suit.
    • Effect: Activates only after the first trick and only if all players followed the lead suit (no Joker/Mighty/Right Jack/Left Jack or other suits played). The "2" of the lead suit defeats all other cards in the trick.

    📊 Scoring

    After 10 tricks, victory points (VP) are distributed based on the outcome.

    Result Napoleon Adjudant Allied Forces (each)
    Napoleon Army Wins (Normal) +4 +2 0
    Napoleon Army Wins (Solo) +6 - 0
    Allied Forces Win (Down) 0 0 +2
