    Eighteenthirtyfive

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    This is an adaption of an already existing game for Board Game Arena. Therefore some of the UX is completely unusual for BGA games. Like drag'n'drop. For a swifter transition, I (the developer) will leave it as is in order to keep my effort to a minimum. Please be patient, I'll change the UI in the future. Thanks for your understanding.

    When it is your turn, keep in mind to make all actions you want to do before hitting "Send Turn".

    As a player in a stock round you can:

    • buy: drag a share (or a package in the starting round) to your player's info on the right side
    • sell: drag a share from your player's info onto the bank pool in the stock exchange
    • nationalize: drag a share from the player's info you want to get it from to your player's info

    As a director of an operating company in the operation round you can:

    • lay a track: click (and hold) on the hex you want to lay it then drag the pointer to the turning symbol to rotate to select your track tile
    • place a station: click on the according city
    • run a train: click on the according train in your company to select it, then drag your route on the board
    • buy a train: drag a train from the stock exchange to your company

    Laying a track, placing a station and running a train need your confirmation on the green checkmark before proceeding.
