    Aero

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.1 · 排名 #0 · 权重 2.00/5 · 20人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    Arrest all 6 criminals with the highest score and win the game.

    Dice

    • 2 blue dice (eye, eye, 2, 3, 4, 5)
    • 2 black dice (eye, 2, 3, 4, 5, 5)
    • 4 bandit dice (6 different bandits)

    Game Play

    Each player rolls 8 dice. 4 of the dice have the 6 criminals on them and the other 4 have number values from 1 (snake eye) to 6.

    You must split minimum of 1 die after each roll and re-roll the rest. That split die will not be rerolled again.

    At the end of your roll, you pick one of the criminals from your dice roll and arrest them.

    Scores are determined by the number of dice you have of that one criminal multiplied by the LOWEST number value.

    For example, if you rolled Clown, Clown, Clown, Fly, 2, 2, 4, 5. You will score 3 (clowns) x 2 (lowest numeric value) = 6. Or, if you choose Fly, you will score 1 (Fly) x 2 (lowest value) = 2.

    Twist: Snake Eye has value of 1 UNLESS you rolled 4 snake eyes, then it has a value of 7.

    If, at the end of your roll, you do not have a criminal dice face matching any of your unarrested criminals, you will have to choose any of your unarrested criminals, flip them over, and score 0 for that turn.

    Game ends after 6 turns.
