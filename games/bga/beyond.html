    Beyond

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 5.5 · 排名 #27387 · 权重 1.20/5 · 102人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    Win by scoring the most energy points or by enchanting all 6 Nian Shou cards for an instant victory.

    Game Turn (3 Phases)

    Phase 1: Reveal and Collect

    Reveal cards one by one from the draw pile.

    If all are different Spirits: keep going or stop and take all.

    If a duplicate is revealed: stop and take only the last card.

    If first 2 cards are duplicates: gain a Nian Shou card.

    Use Lotus tokens to take extra revealed cards (1 token = 1 card).

    Phase 2: Place Collected Cards

    Choose how to place each collected card:

    • Spirit side up: goes into your play area for patterns.

    • Capture side up (Yin/Yang): goes on your Stock card (max 4).

    Phase 3: Enchant / Capture / Compensate

    Enchant a Spirit by completing a valid 4-card pattern.

    Capture an opponent’s Spirit using: • Yin card → captures Yang-protected Spirit • Yang card → captures Yin-protected Spirit • Yin + Yang → captures Nian Shou card

    Captured card goes to your play area; used capture cards are discarded.

    Opponent gains 1 token from the supply per card lost.

    End of Game

    Game ends when:

    A player enchants 3 Spirits

    OR the second draw pile runs out

    OR someone enchants all 6 Nian Shou cards (instant win)

    Scoring

    Points from Enchanted Spirit patterns (Completed spirit)

    Turtle snake (Blue): +6

    Vermilion bird (Orange): +6

    Double Azure Dragon (Green): +9

    Double Metal Tiger (Purple): +9

    Yellow Unicorn (Yellow): +12


    +1 per Celestial Spirit Face card outside of the complete patterns

    +1 per Nian Shou card owned

    +1 per Capture Face card (card that use Yin/Yang capture Yang/Yin protected spirit card etc)

    +1 per token in their entire own play area

    Tiebreaker

    Player with lesser Spirit face-up cards wins (But BGA set more will be winning in tie break)

    Advanced Mode

    Protection Tokens

    Place token on Yin/Yang symbol of a Spirit card.

    Protects that card from being captured (requires Yin + Yang to take).

    Token returns to owner if the card is captured.

    Tokens on enchanted patterns return to common supply.

    Spirit Summoning (Once per Enchanted Spirit)

    Place a Lotus token on the center of an Enchanted Spirit to activate its effect:

    Turtle Snake: Choose 1 of the top 4 cards → add to play area

    Vermilion Bird: Take a capture card from discard pile

    Double Azure Dragon: Pick 1 of top 2 cards → add to play area

    Double Metal Tiger: Take a Nian Shou card (if any left)

    Yellow Unicorn: Reclaim a Lotus from another Spirit pattern
