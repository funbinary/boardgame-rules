    End game Scoring:

    - Victory Point Chits collected during the game - Point values of buildings - Point values of all bonuses on buildings - 2 Victory Points for each Gold, Livestock, and Copper Resource. (Wood, Food and Steel are worth nothing.)

    Debt chits can be paid off for 5 silver. All unpaid debts are negative points, consult the chart. -1 VP for first, -2 VP for second, -3 VP for third, etc.

    In case of a tie, the winner is the tied player with the most silver.
