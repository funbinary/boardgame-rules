    JOURNEY CARDS: These

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.8 · 排名 #7473 · 权重 2.68/5 · 397人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    JOURNEY: HORIZON

    Draft cards and assemble them in a photo album to showcase your many and farflung travels, earning points with every addition. Organize the cards to benefit from multipliers as you collect sets of colors and symbols over the course of 4 rounds.


    COMPONENT DETAILS

    JOURNEY CARDS: These are the photos you will place in your album.

    ~ They come in 5 colors (green, purple, blue, orange and yellow), sometimes a single color and sometimes a combination of two, as seen on the colored tape at the top of the card.

    ~ They may also bear symbols at the top of the card. These symbols are added to your collection.

    ~ At the bottom, you may find a multiplier ("Number/") on the right and a symbol on the left. Earn points on every turn by pairing symbols and multipliers on adjacent cards


    GOAL CARDS: These are common objectives that provide bonuses.

    ~ 3 of 12 possible objectives are randomly selected at the start of the game.

    ~ Depending on which objective you complete, you may be able to score extra victory points OR place a symbol or multiplier on any card in your tableau and score that card again.

    ~ Each objective may only be completed once.


    GAMEPLAY

    Everyone's tableau begins with a random start card showing a multiplier and symbol at the bottom but no colored tape or symbols at the top.


    DRAFTING PHASE:

    All steps in the drafting phase take place simultaneously.

    1. Everyone receives 3 random journey cards.

    2. Everyone chooses 2 of their cards to give to the player on their left.

    3. Everyone receives the 2 cards from their neighbor and chooses 1 of the 3 cards in their hand to again pass to the player on their left.

    4. Everyone receives the 1 card from their neighbor and chooses 1 of their 3 cards to discard.


    PLACEMENT AND SCORING PHASE:

    You will now place your 2 remaining cards, one-by-one, into your tableau. This adds card colors and symbols to your collection AND triggers scoring.

    The player with the lowest score begins this phase. Then play progresses clockwise.

    1. Select one card in your hand.

    2. Place the card at the END of the tableau, to the left or the right.

    3. Score points for the multiplier and symbol combination your just created!

    4. Check to see if you have fulfilled an objective card. If so, take the bonus immediately.

    5. Repeat steps 1-5 for your 2nd card.


    BONUS DETAILS

    There are 3 types of bonuses.

    ~ Victory Points ranging from 1-8 points.

    ~ Multipliers: three x1, one x2 and one x3. Immediately place it on any card in your tableau. Immediately re-score the card IF it has already been combined with a symbol/tape.

    ~ Symbols and Colored Tape, which are immediately added to a card in your tableau. That card is immediately re-scored IF it has already been combined with a multiplier.


    GAME END

    At the end of round 4, the player with the highest score wins.

    ~ Tied? Share the victory!
