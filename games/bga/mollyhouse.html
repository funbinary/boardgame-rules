    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Festivity Possibilities ( In rank Order )

    1. Surprise Ball: Four desire cards of the same suit in sequential order.
    2. Christening: Queen and 3 desire cards of the same value.
    3. Dance: Jack and 3 desire cards in sequential order (ignore suit).

    If none of the above ranks are made, a Quiet Gathering occurs. The four lowest desires are selected as the winning set, ignoring rogues. If there were fewer than four desires played, score only those.
