    Quibbles

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    👥 2–5 人 ⭐ BGG 5.9 · 排名 #16781 · 权重 1.27/5 · 411人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Quibbles

    2-5 players, 8+, 5 minutes per player

    Components

    • 144 cards, numbered 1 to 6: 22×1, 22×2, 22×3, 24×4, 26×5, 28×6

    Setup

    1. Shuffle the cards thoroughly.
    2. Draw 6 cards and put them in a face up row in the middle of the table.
    3. Deal every player a hand of 6 cards.
    4. Form a face down draw pile from the remaining cards and put it in the middle of the table.
    5. Make room for a discard pile.
    6. Every player makes room in front of them for a collection of 6 cards.

    Goal of the game

    To be the first player to collect cards with a total value of 21 points or more.

    Gameplay

    Play in turns. When it is your turn, you first choose to either SPLIT/COMBINE or DRAW.

    SPLIT/COMBINE

    Play 1 to 6 cards from your hand with a total value of 6 or less. Put them on the discard pile. Now take cards from the central row on the table in your hands.

    SPLIT

    • If you played a single card, you may take all sets of 2 or more cards with the same total value as the card you played.

    Example: you play a 6 from your hand and now take back a pair of 3s, and a 2+4 set from the table. You leave a 1+5 set on the table, even though you were allowed to take it.

    Note: every card in the row on the table can only be part of 1 set.

    COMBINE

    • If you played 2 or more cards, you may take all single cards with the same value as the total value of the cards you played.

    Example: play a 1, a 2, and a 3 and take all three 6s from the table.

    DRAW

    Add 1 card from your hand face up to the row on the table, draw 2 cards from the draw pile, and put them in your hand.

    NOTE: There is no hand limit.

    ADDING TO COLLECTION

    You may optionally move 1 card from your hand to your collection, for scoring. To do this, you must take all cards of the same value from your hand, with a minimum amount equal to the value of the card. Add one of them to your collection and discard the other cards.

    In other words: to add a 5 to your collection, you need to have at least five 5s. But for a 2 you only need two 2s.

    Example: you take all four 4s from your hand, put one 4 in your collection and discard the remaining 4s. You now have no more 4s in your hand.

    NOTE: you can have only 6 cards in your collection. If you still want to add a card to your collection, you will have to remove one card from your collection first, and put it on the discard pile.

    FINALLY

    • Refill the central row to 6 face up cards with cards from the draw pile.

    • When there are 10 cards in the central row, discard all cards and put 6 new face up cards on the table.

    • If your hand is empty, all players have to discard the cards they hold in their hands. Deal every player a hand of 6 new cards.

    Note: if the draw pile is empty, shuffle the discard pile and form a new pile.

    Now it is the next player’s turn.

    End of the game

    You win as soon as you have a collection of six cards with a total value of at least 21 (for instance, a 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6). The game ends immediately.


    Expansions

    The Magnificent Sevens

    Add the seven 7 cards to the base game. All basic rules apply, with the following adjustment:

    When COMBINING, you may play up to 7 cards from your hand, with a total value of 7 or less.

    The Zero Heroes

    Add the four 0 cards to the base game. All basic rules apply, with the following adjustments:

    You cannot SPLIT a 0. You cannot STASH a 0 (that is, you cannot add it to your collection). You may use a 0 when COMBINING.

    The Magnificent Sevens + The Zero Heroes

    If you use both expansions, add all 23 expansion cards to the base game:

    4× 0 2× 1, 2× 2, 2× 3, 2× 4, 2× 5, 2× 6 7× 7

    All basic rules apply, with the following adjustments:

    When COMBINING, you may play up to 7 cards from your hand, with a total value of 7 or less. You cannot SPLIT or STASH a 0 (that is, you cannot add it to your collection). You may use a 0 when COMBINING.
