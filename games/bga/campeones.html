    Campeones

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    1v1 deck builder; ~25min ; 8y+ ; 2/5 complexity; Football(soccer) game based on Captain Tsubasa.

    Round

    1. Draw to 4 cards.
    2. Defending player drafts card from 4 available or deck. Then attacking player drafts.
    3. Attacking player plays card or passes. Cards have 1-2 optional abilities (draw, play, add tokens, delete, etc), which can combo.
      1. You cannot use abilities from cards from previous turns (except re-roll dice and goalkeepers, which activate in shoot/pass phase).
      2. During this step, you can Motivate (improve) some cards, and Recruit new cards into the play area. Check bench for their token costs.
      3. Repeat this step(3) for defending player. Repeat until 9 cards in area or both players pass.
    4. Attacking player shoots or passes. Attacking player can add tokens to increase shot/pass, then defending player can add defence.
    5. If goal, increase goals by 1. If pass or corner (from shot), keep possession, otherwise change attacking player.

    Extra rules

    • When the last card is taken in each half, Injury Time begins. Play continues until attacker loses the ball or scores a goal.
    • After the First Half, the match goes to half-time, hand&decks are re-shuffled.
    • After the Second Half, match ends, unless there is a tie, which starts Extra time.
    • Extra time - 2 halves with 2 rounds each (passing still works). Reshuffle at the start of each half. Card limit is 7. If tied, ends anyway.
