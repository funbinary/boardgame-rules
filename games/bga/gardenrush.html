    Gardenrush

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.5 · 排名 #8500 · 权重 1.50/5 · 407人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Skillfully plant Veggies in your garden and harvest them in specific patterns. Be the fastest to harvest your Veggies to win!

    [English Tutorial]

    Gameplay

    On your turn, you must either: Place 1 Veggie token or Harvest 1 Veggie bed.

    When either the Trampoline cannot be refilled, or any player has reach at least 40 points, the end game triggered. If this happens at the end of the First player's turn, the other player takes one final turn. The player with the most points wins the game.

    Place 1 Veggie token

    Choose 1 of the 5 Veggie tokens from the Hedge and place it onto an empty space in your Garden.

    If it is placed onto the same column as it was in the Hedge, the double Veggie token side is placed face up. Else, the single Veggie token side is placed face up.

    Refill the Hedge by pushing a token from the Trampoline, and refill the Trampoline from the bag.

    If the token is placed on a Tool space, you may immediately use the matching bonus action (see below).

    Harvest 1 Veggie bed

    To harvest a Veggie bed in your garden, the bed must match one of the patterns indicated for that Veggie (no matter the orientation).

    Each token in the pattern scores 1 point. Discard the single Veggie tokens and flip the double Veggie to their single side.

    If your Gnome finishes its movement on a Tool space, you may apply the bonus action of any Tool space Visible in your garden.

    Tool space Bonus Actions

    Gloves: Do a Place 1 token action.

    Basket: Do a Harvest 1 Veggie bed action.

    Wheelbarrow: Move 1 Veggie token in your garden onto another space. If you move a Veggie token onto a Tool space, you may apply its bonus action. (You cannot choose the token that you just placed onto this Tool space. )

    Watering Can: Flip 1 Veggie token in either player's Garden.

    Bonus Tools Variant

    8 additional Tool tokens are added to the token bag with Veggie tokens.

    The Place 1 token action allows you to choose 1 Tool token from the Hedge and place it on any empty space in your garden except the Tool space. Unlike Veggie tokens, it doesn't matter which column you place it in. After the Hedge is refilled, apply the bonus action of the Tool token like you covered a Tool space.

    Alternatively, you can flip the Tool token on the Hedge to its Mole side and place it onto any empty space in your opponent's Garden, except a Tool space. Moles do nothing except take up space.

    Tool/Mole tokens can NEVER be flipped for the rest of the game. But you can use Wheelbarrow to move them to another empty space except the tool space.

    When your Gnome ends on a Tool space, you may apply the bonus action of any visible Tool token or Tool space in your garden, OR you can move a Mole token from your garden into your opponent's garden (still, except the Tool space).

    Tool Token Bonus Action

    Golden Shovel: After resupplying the Hedge, swap 2 adjacent tokens on the Hedge, then place 1 of those tokens using Place 1 token action.

    Poison: Discard ALL Veggies of a single type from the Hedge (not the Trampoline) and score 1 point per discarded token.

    Hose: Flip ALL the Veggie tokens from any column in both players’ Gardens. The token on the Hedge of the chosen column is unaffected.

    Toolbox: Apply the bonus action of any visible Tool space or Tool token from your opponent’s Garden to your own Garden.
