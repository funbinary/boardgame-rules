    Quatrix

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.7 · 排名 #0 · 权重 2.00/5 · 3人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Game Overview

    Quatrix combines line-building and area control strategy with a forcing mechanic. Every move restricts the opponent's freedom of action.

    Goal of the Game (Instant Win)

    You win the game immediately if:

    • You form a continuous line of 4 of your color (horizontal, vertical, diagonal).

    OR

    • You occupy (capture) 4 squares of the same color (e.g., 4x Blue) with your pieces.

    Gameplay

    Place a piece on an empty cell. The symbol on your piece dictates the opponent's mandatory next move:

    • X Symbol: The opponent must place their next piece on a cell in a diagonal line from your piece.
    • Square Symbol (□): The opponent must place their next piece on a cell in an orthogonal line (horizontal/vertical).
    • Free Choice: If all cells in the required directions are occupied, the opponent may place their piece anywhere on an empty cell.


    Pro Tips

    Be careful to not let your opponent capture quickly 3 same colors !

    Keep a balance between your X and □ pieces but force your opponent to spend one of the kind.
