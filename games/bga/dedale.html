    Dedale

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.0 · 排名 #14197 · 权重 1.14/5 · 271人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    In this cooperative game, you play as the assistants of the architect Daedalus. Your goal is to succeed in locking the Minotaur in a labyrinth by completing increasingly difficult Labors.

    The game has 10 levels originally, additional levels are publishing each season on the official website.

    Zone of Tiles

    Levels 3, 4, 7, 9 & 10

    Group of Tiles connected to each other and not separated by any walls or the Minotaur (the Tiles must be in one contiguous sequence).

    Ariadne & Theseus

    When you have a Labyrinth card with one of these characters in your hand, you can place it wherever you want without considering the type of Tile on top of your drawing pile!
