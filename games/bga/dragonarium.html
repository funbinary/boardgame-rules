    Dragonarium

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.8 · 排名 #10262 · 188人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    Take on the role of an imperial hatchery official and build the most magnificent Dragonarium. Collect territory tiles, strategically place them outwards and upwards, and use the elements of fire, water, and air to hatch dragons. The player with the most valuable domains and dragons after 12 rounds wins.

    Card & Tile Reference

    • Territory Tiles: Each tile consists of two squares of the types fire (red), water (blue), air (green), or royal (gold).
    • Dragon Eggs: Depicted on tiles. They require element stones to be placed on them to hatch.
    • Dragons: Once hatched, a dragon nests on a square. That square now counts as having two types: the original square type AND the dragon's type.
    • Pillar Tiles: Used as supports to place territory tiles flat on higher levels.

    Gameplay

    The game lasts 12 rounds. Each round consists of player turns and replenishing the planning site. A turn includes three steps:

    1. Draft and Place a Tile:
      • Choose one tile from the public display.
      • Place it on level 1 or higher. Tiles on higher levels must lay flat (use pillars as support if necessary).
      • Rules: Tiles cannot be placed on top of dragons or eggs that have not yet hatched.
    2. Hatch Dragons:
      • If a placed tile or pillar covers an egg that has all its required element stones, the dragon hatches.
      • Take the corresponding dragon and place it on any empty square in your Dragonarium.
      • The square with a Dragon now counts as also having the Territory type of the placed Dragon.
    3. Move the Assistant:
      • Choose one element (fire, water, or air).
      • Count the number of squares in your largest domain of that type and move your assistant exactly that many steps clockwise.
      • At each step, place one stone of the chosen element onto a matching empty egg space in that row or column.

    End of Game & Scoring

    The game ends when the supply of territory tiles is exhausted.

    • Domain Points: For each of the four types (fire, water, air, royal), score your largest continuous domain:
      • Formula: Number of connected squares x Height of the domain (highest level).
    • Royal Dragons: Each hatched gold dragon grants an additional 3 victory points.
    • The player with the most victory points wins. In case of a tie, the player with the most dragons wins.
