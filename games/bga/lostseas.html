    Lostseas

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Gameplay

    Place tiles in a 4x4 grid according to the goals per column and row. At the start, choose between the front and back side of each goal card. The potential points are shown in the flag at the top left corner.

    Symbols

    There are 6 different symbol types:

    • Kraken (Red Squid)
    • Leviathan (Green Sea Monster)
    • Maelstrom (Lightning Bolt)
    • Rock
    • Sandbank (Palm Tree)
    • Shipwreck

    Notes

    Read the goals carefully. Many require an exact number of a particular symbol.

    The last player in the previous round is the first player in the next round. (So in a 2-person game, after the first round, you will be playing 2 turns in a row -- be careful what you discard!)

    If there is a tie, the winner is the player who has achieved the most goals. If there is still a tie, the winner is the player who fulfilled the goal tile worth the most points. If there is still a tie, the players share the victory.
