    Yinshrules

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    Standard: First to remove 3 rings wins.

    Blitz: First to remove 1 ring wins.

    Setup

    • Each player gets 5 rings (black or white).
    • Players alternate placing their rings on empty intersections.

    Turns (after setup)

    • Place a marker of your color inside one of your rings.
    • Move that ring in a straight line (any distance) to an empty space.
      • If you jump over a series of markers (any color), the ring must land in the first empty space of that line.
      • All jumped-over markers are flipped to the opposite color.
      • You may not cross over other rings regardless of their color.

    Scoring

    • If you create a row of 5 markers of your color:
      • Remove those 5 markers from the board.
      • Remove one of your rings from the board.
    • If the pool of 51 markers runs out:
      • The player with the most rings removed wins.
      • If both players have removed the same number of rings, the game ends in a draw.
