    Diggingfordinos

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.7 · 排名 #11956 · 154人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Goal

    Collect fossil tiles and complete dinosaur skeletons to score the most points. The player with the highest score at the end of the game wins.

    Turn Overview

    1. Players take turns clockwise. On your turn:
    2. Move one of your paleontologists
    3. Flip that paleontologist
    4. Collect the fossil tile you moved from
    5. Replace the tile with a new one from the current era

    Movement

    Move a Paleontologist

    Choose one of your two paleontologists and move it in the direction shown on the token.

    Each token has two sides:

    • Orthogonal side – move up, down, left, or right
    • Diagonal side – move diagonally

    Movement Rules

    • You must move in one of the directions shown on the token.
    • You cannot land on a space occupied by another paleontologist.
    • You may jump over any number of tokens to reach the next open tile in that direction.
    • You cannot move off the board.


    If no legal move is available, move one of your tokens to any open tile, ignoring movement rules.

    Flip the Paleontologist

    After moving, flip the paleontologist token to its other side.

    This causes the paleontologist to alternate between:

    • Orthogonal movement
    • Diagonal movement

    Collecting Fossils

    After moving:

    • Take the fossil tile you moved from and place it in front of you.
    • Replace that tile with a new fossil tile from the current era stack.

    If the current era stack is empty, begin drawing tiles from the next era.

    Completing Dinosaurs

    Fossil tiles belong to dinosaur sets.

    Each tile shows:

    • The dinosaur species
    • Its position in the skeleton
    • Two scoring values:
    • - Left number – points if the dinosaur is incomplete
    • - Right number – points if the dinosaur is completed

    To complete a dinosaur, collect all tiles in its set.

    Special Ability

    Completing either of the following dinosaurs triggers an ability:

    • Velociraptor
    • Deinosuchus

    When one of these dinosaurs is completed, the player may immediately flip one of their paleontologist tokens.

    End Game

    The game ends when either condition occurs:

    • 1 - The final Era 3 fossil tile is placed on the board. or
    • 2 - A player completes their fourth dinosaur.

    All players finish the current round so everyone has taken the same number of turns. At the end of the round, players will collect the fossil tiles that their paleontologist tokens are currently on.

    Scoring

    Players score their fossil tiles as follows:

    • Incomplete dinosaurs: score the left value on each fossil tile
    • Completed dinosaurs: score the right value once for the entire set

    Tiles in completed dinosaurs do not score individually.

    Winning

    The player with the most points wins.


    Tiebreaker

    If players are tied:

    1. Compare the largest scoring completed dinosaur
    2. If still tied, compare the next largest
    3. Continue until the tie is broken
