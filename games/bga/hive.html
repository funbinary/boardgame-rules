    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    Overview

    Objective
    Surround the opponent's queen bee.
    ​
    ​
    ​
    ​
    ​
    ​
    ​
    ​
    • 2 player abstract.
    • Players compete to gain control of a hive by surrounding their opponent's queen bee.
    • One player plays the black pieces, the other player plays the white pieces.
    • Play area starts empty.

    Player Turn

    • Must make a move if possible, otherwise their turn is skipped.

    Each turn choose to either:

    1. Place a new piece.
    2. Move a piece.

    Place a piece

    • Must be on the edge of the hive.
    • Queen bee must be placed in your first 4 turns.

    After each player has placed their first piece:

    • Must touch a piece of the same color.
    • Cannot touch a piece of the opposite color.

    Note: Stacks are the color of the top piece.

    Move a piece

    • You cannot move pieces until your queen bee is placed.
    • Imaginary hexagonal grid.
    • Adjacent pieces always touch a complete edge.
    • All pieces must form one contiguous group at all times (One Hive rule, see below).
    • A piece cannot move through a gap that is too narrow (Freedom to Move rule, see below).

    Pillbug
    Slides to an adjacent space or moves an adjacent piece to another space adjacent to the pillbug.
    • Cannot move a piece moved in the previous turn.
    • Moved piece cannot move in the next turn.
    • Cannot move a piece in a stack.

    Game end

    Draw

    • By agreement / repetition.
    • Both queen bees are surrounded on all sides in the same move.

    Win

    • Only the opponent's queen bee is surrounded on all sides.

    Variants

    Expansion pieces:

    • Ladybug
    • Mosquito
    • Pillbug

    Opening:

    • Standard: no restriction
    • Tournament: The queen bee may not be placed in the first turn.
