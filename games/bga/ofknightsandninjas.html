    Ofknightsandninjas

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Hold a certain number of gems to win


    Turn

    On your turn, either Fortify, Attack, or take a Special Action

    If you Fortify, play as many Fortify cards as you wish, these act as defenses against opponent attacks in various ways

    If you Attack, pick a single opponent and play as many Attack cards as you wish until gems have been stolen or you stop playing attack cards

    If you take a Special action, you can play a single Special card to resolve, these cannot be responded to

    If you choose not to or are unable to play any card, you must discard a card instead


    Outside your turn, if you are Attacked, you can choose to play Respond cards in the hopes of repelling the attack

    Having no gems grants NO negative effects, if anything it will let you rebuild whilst your opponents are busy attacking one another


    Game End

    To start, when a player gains their 10th Gem, they win!

    However, as the deck depletes, and gets reshuffled, this requirement is reduced by 1 for each reshuffle, i.e. on the first reshuffle the requirement becomes 9, on the 2nd it becomes 8 and so forth

    There are no passive wins, if after a shuffle you have the required gems, you MUST still gain 1 gem, or lose 1 gem and gain one back, in order to win!
