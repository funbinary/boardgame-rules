    Gofish

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 3.7 · 排名 #31251 · 权重 1.05/5 · 2151人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    The aim is to collect sets of 4 matching cards

    Turn

    If you have a card, you can ask another player for cards of the same rank.

    If they have those cards, they must give you all copies.

    If they don't, you draw a card from the draw pile.

    If the rank of the drawn card is what you asked last or the card completes a 4 of a kind, you get another turn.

    Scoring

    Each time you hold 4 of the same card, you declare this and place them out to show as a "Book"

    When either the deck runs out or someone empties their hand, the player with the most Books wins
