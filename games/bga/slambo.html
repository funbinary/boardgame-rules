    SLAMBO!

    Overview

    SLAMBO! is a fast-paced card game played in rounds called bouts. Players take turns adjusting a shared balance total—but push it too far and your opponent will shout SLAMBO!

    Setup

    Players Cards in Deck Starting Hand
    2 17 7
    3 26 6
    4 26 5
    1. Place the start card in the middle with the 0–10 side up
    2. Place 1 shame card next to the start card
    3. Set remaining shame cards aside as a supply
    4. Give each player a player aid
    5. Shuffle the number cards (only add special cards with 3+ players)
    6. Deal each player a starting hand (see table above)
    7. Set leftover cards facedown
    8. Choose a random player to go first

    Gameplay

    Turn

    • Players take turns playing 1 card face up onto the central pile
    • Only the top card of the pile should be visible
    • The starting balance total is 5

    Each time you play a card:

    • Adjust the balance total by the number on the card
      • Blue cards raise the total
      • Red cards lower the total
    • Shout the new total

    SLAMBO!

    If the balance total goes:*Above 10

    • Below 0

    👉 Your opponent shouts SLAMBO! and you lose the bout

    • The losing player takes all shame cards from the middle

    Break

    If both players run out of cards and no one is SLAMBO’d:

    • The referee calls BREAK!
    • No one takes shame cards
    • Add 1 shame card next to the start card

    Important: The next bout will be worth more shame

    Again

    If no player has 3 shame cards:

    • Play another bout
    • The loser of the last bout goes first

    If the last bout ended in a break:*The player who went second goes first

    Win Condition

    If any player has 3 or more shame cards at the end of a bout:*That player loses

    • The remaining player wins

    3–4 Player Rules

    • Shuffle the 6 special cards into the deck
    • The game ends immediately when any player has 3 or more shame
    • That player loses
    • All other players win

    Special Cards

    • After adjusting the balance total, reverse the turn order
    • If a player has no cards left, skip their turn

    Special effects:

    • Choose whether a card’s number is positive or negative
    • Copy the last card played**If the last card copied something, copy that result

    Expert Match (1–9)

    For a more suspenseful game:

    • Flip the start card to the 1–9 side

    Now SLAMBO occurs if the balance total goes:

    • Above 9
    • Below 1

    ==Example==*Player 1 plays a blue 4 → “9!”

    • Player 2 plays red -7 → “2!”

    Later in the bout:

    • Balance is 1
    • Player 1 plays blue 2 → “3!”
    • Player 2 plays red -5 → “-2!”
    • Player 1 shouts: SLAMBO!

    Components

    • 1 Start Card (Hakkeyoi, Nokotta!)
    • 9 Shame Cards (Haji!)
    • 17 Number Cards (9 blue, 8 red)
    • 6 Special Cards
    • 4 Player Aids

    Theme Note

    Hakkeyoi! (“come on!”) and Nokotta! (“remaining!”) are traditional sumo referee calls used during matches.
