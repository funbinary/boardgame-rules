    Hats

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.7 · 排名 #3708 · 权重 2.03/5 · 1281人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Setup

    • Use only 4 colours with 2 players, otherwise 6.
    • Fill table with 5 cards with 2 players, otherwise 6.
    • Each player draw 9 cards.

    Gameplay

    • On your turn, you must take an action from exchange and create.
    • If you exchange, play a card from your hand face up and exchange it with one from the table, playing it face up.
    • The card played must have the same colour or a higher number than the card on the table.
    • If you create, play a card face down from your hand to create a black hat.
    • You may also discard a card from your hand and draw.
    • If there are 4 players, you may exchange a card with your partner.

    End of the Game

    • The game ends when each player has played 8 cards. The last card is revealed as their favourite hat.
    • If there are 2 or more same typed cards on the table, turn all them face down except the leftmost.
    • Each card in your collection scores a number of points equal to their colour matching position on the table.
    • Each black hat scores 1 point.
    • Each card played by you that matches your favourite hat score the number of points on it. The favourite hat scores negatively.
    • The player with the most number of different types (black as a type) gets the cookie and scores 5 points.
    • If there are 4 players, players add their points total to their partner. The player with the most points wins.
    • If there is a tie, the player with more black hats wins. If there is still a tie, the player with the cookie wins.
