    Battlegammon

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Game Rule

    Starting Setup:

    Each player randomly chooses either black or white pawns. Then, place your pawns on the board in their designated starting positions as indicated by the corresponding numbers.

    The black player always moves first.

    Your Turn:

    1. Rolling the Dice

    On their turn, a player rolls two dice and moves two pawns forward based on the numbers rolled. Players cannot move the same pawn twice or skip their turn. That means each pawn must move separately.

    Example: If a player rolls a 3 and a 5, one pawn moves three spaces, and the other moves five spaces.

    2. Movement

    - Direction:

    Both players move their pawns toward the opponent's home area, which serves as the goal. Black pawns move toward the white home area, and white pawns move toward the black home area.

    - Landing on an Occupied Space:

    If a pawn lands on a space already occupied by another pawn (either their own or the opponent's), the pawns stack together. A maximum of two pawns can stack in this way.

    - Stacking Rules:
    • Home area doesn’t have stack limit.
    • Each space’s stack limit is 2.
    • The pawn on the bottom of a stack cannot move until the top pawn moves away.
    • No other pawns can land on a space that already contains a stack.
    - Entering the Opponent's Home Area:

    To enter the opponent's home area, a pawn must move the exact number of spaces required. If the exact roll is not possible, the player must move a different pawn.

    Example: If a pawn is three spaces away from the home area, the player must roll a 3 to enter.

    - Limited Movement Options:

    Based on the dice roll and game rules, a player may sometimes only be able to move one pawn—or no pawns at all.

    - Finish Movement:

    After completing your move, it becomes your opponent’s turn.

    Victory Conditions:

    A player wins by achieving either of the following:

    • Moving three pawns into the opponent's home area.
    • Occupying eight spaces of the opponent's color with their pawns.

    遊戲規則（繁中）

    遊戲設置

    兩位玩家隨機持黑白棋。

    將己方五顆棋子放在起點，另外五顆棋子按照遊戲盤上標示的 黑點/白點 數量，放到棋格上。

    黑棋先行。

    遊戲進行

    1. 擲骰

    輪到自己時擲兩個骰子，依照骰子點數分別移動兩個棋子。

    棋子能移動就必須移動，不能合併點數走同一個棋子。

    2.移動棋子

    方向

    黑棋只能往終點(白棋起點)的方向移動，白棋反之。

    堆疊
    • 棋子在同一格可重疊，一格內最多重疊兩個棋子。
    • 被疊住的棋子不能移動。
    • 棋子不能走入有堆疊棋子的棋格。
    • 起點沒有堆疊數量限制
    進入終點

    棋子必須以剛好的點數走進終點。

    略過

    如果所有棋子都不符合移動條件，則玩家略過一回合。

    遊戲目標

    達成以下條件之一獲勝

    • 己方三顆棋子走到終點(對方的起點)
    • 己方八顆棋子走到對方區域(對方顏色的格子
