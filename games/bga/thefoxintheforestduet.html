    Thefoxintheforestduet

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    This is a co-operative version of the also two player Fox in the Forest (competitive)

    Gather all the gems before time runs out


    Turn

    A round consists of 11 turns

    Each turn, one player will lead the trick and the other will follow

    The player who won the most recent trick, leads the current trick

    The leader plays any card from their hand

    The follower must play a card matching the suit of the leader's, or play any card if they do not hold that suit

    The Decree card, which was revealed in setup and can be changed during the game, shows the Trump suit i.e. the suit that beats the lead suit

    If you played a card with an Odd number, enact its ability when appropriate

    The winner of the trick is either the player who played the highest Trump, or if none were played then the winner is the player who played the highest lead suit

    After the winner is determined, you will find paw prints on the two played cards, these combined determine how far the movement piece moves towards the trick winner e.g. a 2 paws and 3 paws would cause 5 movement in the winner's direction

    If when moving the team tracker it would move off the board on either end, move it back to the central space and cover the last visible space on the end which was moved off from with a forest marker e.g. if the movement made the piece leave the board on the right, the first available space from the right edge of the board would receive forest, this therefore permanently shortens the path for the rest of the game. If the Forest marker would go on an unclaimed gem space, move the gem closer along into the next accessible space on the path.

    If after moving step, the team tracker is on a space connected to Gems without a reset, you may claim a single gem from that pile regardless of how many may be stacked there (even if the tracker did not move)

    After a Trick is resolved, place the played cards nearby so it is easily accessible for reference during future tricks, then start a new trick with the winner of the current one


    Round End

    The round ends after 11 tricks, at which point perform the following:

    Add a gem to each space which contains a +

    Add a Forest token to one end of the path, the players may choose the end except when the team tracker is on the last space of one end then they have to choose the other. If there are no Forest tokens left to place, continue the game without placing a token.

    As with during the game, if placing the new Forest token covers an occupied gem space, move the gems one space inwards

    Shuffle all 30 cards and deal out 11 each in addition to revealing a single card for the start Decree

    The Team Tracker remains in the space it ended the round it, it does NOT reset to the central space


    Game End

    If the players have to place a Forest token when there are no tokens left, they LOSE!

    If the players have taken 3 rounds and still haven't succeeded, they LOSE!

    If the players collect all the gems before the end of the 3rd round, they Win!

    IF the players Win, they may score as follows:

    Score 10, 20, or 30 points depending on whether you chose a Level 1 game, a Level 2 game, or a Level 3 game (higher the level, higher the difficulty)

    Score 1 point for each remaining hand card

    Score 10 points if you won in round 2

    Score 3 points for each unplaced Forest token
