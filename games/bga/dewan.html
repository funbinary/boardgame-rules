    Scoring Summary

    Scoring Element Points
    Completed Story Tiles Sum of tile points
    Fires Sum of fires on completed story tiles and tucked cards
    Final camp placed 1 additional fire (and corresponding point)
    Most fires (all sources) 4 points (in case of tie, all tied players get 4 points)
    Connected-camp groups 4 points for each group of at least 2 connected camps (camps can reside on different terrains)
    Berry tokens 2 points for each berry collected from the board and on tucked cards
