    Impasse

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 4.6 · 排名 #29797 · 权重 1.60/5 · 61人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    STRATEGY 1 The ideal impasse strategy is to transpose your pieces at the beginning of the game and move them later. Blocking your opponents from reaching the other side is a highly useful technique known to those few that know how to play the game competitively, It may be used to it's highest extent at the end game where moves are a limited and valuable resource; and should be treated likewise.

    by RONIC_LIVES, How to play Impasse for the absolute failure.
