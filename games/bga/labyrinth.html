    Labyrinth

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.4 · 排名 #2127 · 1.6万人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    Navigate the labyrinth to collect all treasure shown on your cards.

    Taking a Turn

    On your turn perform the following 2 actions in order:

    Shift the Maze:

    1. Take the tile outside the maze and place it at the end of any row or column marked with an arrow.
    2. Rotate the tile to desired position.
    3. Push the tile into the board, forcing the tile on the other end to be ejected from the maze and becoming the next tile played.
      • Note: You cannot place the tile in the same row or column that was shifted in the previous turn.
    4. If player(s) were on the ejected tile, place the player(s) on the tile that is on the opposite end of where they exited

    Move Your Piece

    1. Move your piece to any desired tile along any open, continuous path. You may also choose to remain in place.
    2. If you end your turn on the same tile as the treasure card in front of you (opponents cannot see your treasure cards), Reveal the treasure card and look at the new treasure card on the top of your stack.

    Game End

    The person who collects all their treasure and returns to their starting point first wins!
