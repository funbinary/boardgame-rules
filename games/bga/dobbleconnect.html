    Dobbleconnect

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    👥 4 人 ⭐ BGG 6.2 · 排名 #9431 · 权重 1.14/5 · 438人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Dobble Connect is played in real time and simultaneously.

    Look for a symbol on the table that matches a symbol on the hex in your hand, and click the symbol on the table. You can then place your hex onto the table, adjacent to the hex that you clicked.

    First to get four of their hexes in a straight line row wins a point and ends the round, clearing the table.

    When you win a point, everyone else gives you one of their hex tiles. This gets shuffled into your hand and may come up later in the game. When you play it, it will count as belonging to whoever's colour it is!

    First to 5/4/3 points (for 2/3/4 players) wins.
