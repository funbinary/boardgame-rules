    Fivepairs

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 5.8 · 排名 #24395 · 71人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Game Play

    Make pairs by matching a card from your hand with a card from the common card area by their numbers or color.

    If you can't or don't want to make a pair, you draw two cards then discard one to the common card area.

    You get to take a second turn if you match a pair of 3s.

    Scoring

    You score the numbers on the pair of cards.

    If you match both color AND number, you get double the points.

    The first player who made their fourth pair take the 5/15 token with the 5 side up.

    If a player possesses the 5/15 token when they made their fifth pair, they get to flip it to the 15 sides.

    These scores are added to their total.

    Round and Game End

    Round ends when a player made their fifth pair. If that player is holding the 5/15 token when the fifth pair is made, it is flipped to the 15pt side and then all other players get one last turn. If the player making a fifth pair does not have the 5/15 token, it is stolen from the player who does and left on the 5 point side. The round then ends immediately.

    All players add points from made pairs (same number and color count as double points) and the 5/15 token if owned.

    All players will draw back up to two cards. If no player is at 200 points, the next round begins.

    Game ends when one or more players have more than 200 points at the end of a round.

    The player with the highest points is the winner.
