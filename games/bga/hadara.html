    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 7.4 · 排名 #584 · 权重 2.21/5 · 8282人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Game End Scoring

    Colonies

    Score points equal to the values on the bottom right of your colonies.

    Statues

    Score points for your carved statues. If you have any bonus tiles with points showing (flipped), score those points as well.

    Silver Medals

    Score points for your silver medals based on the bonus tiles on them. Score half the corresponding track values (rounded up).

    Gold Medals

    Score 7 points per set of 5 differently-colored cards for each gold medal.

    Cards

    Score points equal to the values on each of your cards.

    Remaining Coins

    Score 1 point for every 5 coins remaining (drop any remainder).

    Ties

    In the event of a tie, the tied player with the most leftover coins wins. If there is still a tie, the tied players share the victory.
