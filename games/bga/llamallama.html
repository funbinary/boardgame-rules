    Llama Llama

    Overview

    In this game, players try to predict which scoring goals will appear while building the best possible hand.

    Each round:

    • Players secretly contribute Goal cards
    • Goal cards are revealed one at a time
    • Players pass cards based on the revealed goals
    • At the end of the round, all 4 goals score

    The game lasts:

    • 4 rounds

    OR

    • Until a player reaches 100 points

    The player with the highest total score wins.

    Goal

    Try to guess the goal cards players have chosen and make the best scoring hand!

    Components

    • 4 Display cards
    • 32 Llama cards
    • 4 Player aids
    • 1 Scorepad

    Setup

    1. Line up the 4 Display cards in the middle.
    2. Use the side matching your player count.
    1. Shuffle the Llama cards.
    1. Deal 4 hands of 8 facedown cards.

    Each player:

    • Takes 1 hand

    Unused hands become:

    • Dummy hands

    Goal Selection

    Each player:

    1. Chooses 1 card from their hand
    2. Places it facedown as their Goal card

    If dummy hands exist:

    • Add 1 random facedown card from each dummy hand

    There should always be:

    • 4 total Goal cards

    Goal Placement

    1. Shuffle all 4 Goal cards.
    2. Deal 1 facedown Goal card onto each Display card.

    Leave the icons at the top visible.

    = Dummy Hands

    For 2–3 players:

    • Place dummy hands between players

    With 3 players:

    • The dummy begins between 2 random players

    Gameplay

    The game is played over rounds.

    Each round consists of 4 reveals.

    During each reveal:

    1. Reveal a Goal
    2. Pass cards

    Step 1: Reveal Goal

    Reveal:

    • The leftmost facedown Goal card

    This Goal now affects scoring.

    Step 2: Pass Cards

    Each player:

    • Passes cards to the player on their left

    The exact number of cards passed is shown above the newly revealed Goal.

    Players may choose any cards from their hand.

    After passing:

    • Add received cards to your hand

    End of Round

    After all 4 Goal cards are revealed:

    • Players score their hands

    Each player scores according to:

    • ALL 4 revealed Goal cards

    Record:

    • Round score
    • New total score

    New total = previous total + current round

    Scoring Rules

    Each Goal card contains:

    • A top scoring rule
    • A bottom scoring rule

    Top Rule

    Awards points for:

    • Each matching individual letter

    Bottom Rule

    Awards points for:

    • Each exactly matching combination of letters

    Important Scoring Rules

    • Letters may score:
      • Individually
      • As part of combinations
      • In multiple different combinations

    Example:

    • AABB may score:
      • AABB = 10
      • AA = 7

    at the same time.

    Duplicate Combination Rule

    An individual letter cannot be reused in the SAME combination multiple times.

    Example:

    • DDFF may score:
      • DF twice

    But:

    • DFF may only score:
      • DF once

    Duplicate Goal Cards

    If multiple matching Goal cards appear:

    • Score each one independently

    Example:

    If:

    • A = 1

    appears on 2 Goal cards:

    • Each A scores 2 total points

    New Round

    After scoring:

    1. Shuffle all cards
    2. Set up a new round

    Each player again contributes:

    • 1 new Goal card

    Goal Placement Order

    Players place their Goal cards based on total score standings:

    Standing Display Card
    Highest Score 1st
    2nd Highest 2nd
    3rd Highest 3rd
    Lowest Score 4th

    Ties:

    • Shuffle tied Goal cards randomly

    Game End

    The game ends when:

    • A player reaches 100+ points

    OR

    • 4 rounds have been played

    Highest total score wins.

    Tie Breaker

    If tied:

    • The tied player with the highest final round score wins

    If still tied:

    • Victory is shared

    2–3 Player Rules

    Always:

    • Deal 4 hands

    Unused hands become:

    • Dummy hands

    Each dummy adds:

    • 1 random Goal card each round

    After Round 1:

    • Place dummy goals on Display cards marked with the dummy icon

    Passing with Dummy Hands

    If a dummy hand is to your left:

    1. Shuffle passed cards into the dummy hand
    2. The next player draws that many random cards from the dummy

    3-Player Dummy Movement

    With 3 players:

    • The dummy starts left of a random player

    At the start of later rounds:

    • Move the dummy to the right of the player with the lowest total score

    With 2 players:

    • Dummies never move

    Strategy Tips

    • Watch how many cards opponents are forced to pass.
    • Flexible letter combinations are powerful.
    • Sometimes hiding your scoring intentions matters more than maximizing one combo.
    • Duplicate Goal cards can create huge scoring swings.
