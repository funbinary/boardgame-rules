    Soda Jerk

    Overview

    Soda Jerk is a quick card game of flavor manipulation and hidden information.

    Each round:

    • Players place Flavor cards face-down below the 5 taps
    • Players later flip cards to reveal their effects
    • Matching flavors increase a tap’s value
    • Non-matching flavors decrease it

    At the end of each round:

    • Players score the cards remaining in their hand based on each flavor’s final value

    After 3 rounds:

    • The player with the highest total score wins.

    Goal

    Mix matching flavors to make the cards in your hand worth more...

    ...or sour the batch for everyone else.

    Components

    • 5 Tap cards (double-sided)
    • 45 Flavor cards
      • 5 flavors
      • 9 cards each
    • 4 Player aids
    • 1 Scorepad
    • 2 Switcheroo cards (2-player only)

    Setup

    1. Give each player a Player Aid.
    2. Lay out the 5 Tap cards in a row with the A side up.
    3. Shuffle all Flavor cards.

    Deal starting hands:

    Players Starting Hand
    2 13*
    3 13
    4 10

    2-player games:

    • See 2-Player Variant
    1. Set aside leftover cards face-down.
    2. The player who most recently sipped a drink is first player.

    Gameplay

    Players take turns clockwise.

    On your turn, choose ONE action:

    • Place a Flavor card
    • Flip a Flavor card

    Important First Turn Rule

    During each player's first turn of the round:

    • They must place a card below a tap that has no cards yet

    Action 1: Place a Flavor

    Choose any Flavor card from your hand.

    • Place it face-down below any tap

    Cards remain hidden until flipped.

    Flavor Values

    At each tap:

    • Matching flavor cards increase the value
    • Non-matching flavor cards reduce the value

    Only the player who placed the card knows its effect until revealed.

    Action 2: Flip a Flavor

    Choose any face-down card below any tap.

    • Flip it face-up

    Once flipped:

    • It remains face-up for the rest of the round

    All players now know its effect.

    End of Round

    The round ends immediately when ANY of these conditions are met:

    A. Low Hand Size

    Any player has:

    • 3 cards left in hand

    at the start of their turn.

    ---

    B. Consecutive Flips

    All players choose to:

    • Flip a card

    on consecutive turns.

    ---

    C. All Cards Revealed

    • All cards at all taps are face-up.

    Round Scoring

    When the round ends:

    1. Reveal all remaining face-down cards.
    1. Determine each tap’s final value:
    • Add matching flavor cards
    • Subtract non-matching flavor cards
    1. Each player reveals their hand.

    Each card in your hand is worth:

    • The final value of its matching tap

    Important:

    • The number on the card does NOT matter for scoring
    • Scores may be negative
    1. Record scores.

    Starting Next Round

    If fewer than 3 rounds have been played:

    1. Shuffle all Flavor cards
    2. Deal new starting hands

    First player:

    • Player with the lowest total score

    Tie:

    • Decide randomly

    Game End

    After 3 rounds:

    • Add all round scores

    The player with the highest total wins.

    Tie:

    • Victory is shared

    2-Player Variant

    Before setup:

    1. Remove any 1 Tap card
    2. Remove all 9 matching Flavor cards

    At the start of each round:

    • Each player adds 1 Switcheroo card to their hand

    Switcheroo

    When played at a tap:

    • Positive becomes negative
    • Negative becomes positive

    Example:

    • +2 becomes -2
    • -4 becomes +4

    Two Switcheroos at the same tap:

    • Cancel each other

    If kept in hand:

    • Worth 0 points

    Advanced Play (B Sides)

    Advanced play adds special tap powers.

    All taps begin on the A side as normal.

    When a tap flips to the B side:

    • Its special effect remains active for the rest of the game

    Setup

    Before Round 1:

    • Flip 1 random tap to the B side

    Start of Round 2:

    • Flip 2 more random taps

    Start of Round 3:

    • Flip all remaining taps

    Strategy Tips

    • Sometimes hurting a flavor is stronger than helping your own.
    • Hidden cards create bluffing opportunities.
    • Flipping a card may reveal useful information—or end the round early.
    • Keeping valuable flavors in hand can create huge swings.
