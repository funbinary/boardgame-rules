    Sunrisesunset

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Play units to three lanes in order to have the most power and thus gain control of the lane, control a lane twice or more in a row to deal damage to your opponent until they die


    Turn

    At the start of a round, draw 5 cards then you may discard a card to draw a replacement once

    Put your personal god into your hand, so you now have 6 cards

    After which, take alternating turns to play all 6 cards

    Upon playing a card, pick which lane you wish to assign it to, and generally speaking it will stay there for the rest of the round

    Each lane can only hold 2 of your cards

    If the card played has a Placement Ability, resolve it and apply its effects to whichever player you wish

    After all cards are placed, resolve each lane from lowest base value to highest base value

    Turn over each Stealth (facedown) card when its lane is being evaluated

    Resolve all Combat abilities of the lane simultaneously

    Whoever has the most power wins the lane, if it is not theirs then it becomes theirs, whilst if it was theirs already then the loser is dealt 1 damage (ties result in nothing happening)


    Round End

    Once all lanes are resolved, flip all the lane cards so the control remains the same

    All playing cards are discarded, and the gods are returned to their owners


    Cards

    The number at the central top of a card shows its power, this is sometimes a flat number but sometimes may be modified by the lane it is played in depicted by the matching symbol

    On the lower section of the card is an ability, these can either be a when played ability or a during combat ability

    On the upper sides of some cards, there may also appear a stealth symbol, these cards will be placed facedown when played


    Game End

    The game ends as soon as one player has 3 or more damage, as such all lanes may not be resolved that round

    The player who is still alive, wins!
