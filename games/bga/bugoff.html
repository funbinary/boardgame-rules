    Bugoff

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    ABOUT THE GAME

    A whimsical bug themed game of press your luck. Can you score your bugs without your opponent cashing in too? Can you get your bugs to the safety of the score pile before the next BUG OFF?

    TAKING YOUR TURN

    Draw 1 card.

    If you draw a Bug Off card, immediately trigger a BUG OFF!

    Otherwise, choose one of the following actions:

    1. PLACE A CARD:
      • Place 1 of your cards face up in your display area.
    2. SCORE A CARD:
      • Place 1 of your cards face up in the Central Scoring Area.
      • All players move any bugs from their display area that match the scoring bug, to their Personal Scoring Area. These cards will score at end game.

    BUG OFF!

    1. Immediately place the Bug Off card on the top of the BUG OFF stack, ensuring all prior cards remain visible.
    2. All players move any bugs from their display area that match the top Bug Off card, to the discard pile. These cards are out of the game.
    3. All players move any bugs from their display area that match any of the previous Bug Off cards, to the discard pile. These cards are out of the game.
    4. If this was the 6th Bug Off card, go directly to SCORING. Otherwise, the current player draws a card to begin their turn again.

    SCORING & END GAME

    The game ends when the 6th BUG OFF has been completed.

    Each player counts the cards in their Personal Scoring Area. All bugs are worth 1 point.

    The player with the most points wins!

    Tie breaker:

    The player with the most bugs matching the oldest Bug Off card.
