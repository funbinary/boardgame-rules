    Herd

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 8.8 · 排名 #0 · 权重 2.33/5 · 28人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    The aim is to occupy the most spaces at the end of the game when all spaces are occupied

    Turn

    Roll two dice

    From the lower value, remove that many opponents' stones

    From the higher value, place that many of your own stones

    If placing a stone completely surrounds your opponent's stone with your own, it is removed, and you get +1 to the number of stones you can place this turn

    You cannot place in an empty space completely surrounded by opponent stones

    If you cannot place all stones allowed, your turn ends early and none are carried over
