    Summary of cards used and rank

    A 24-card pack is used. There are just six cards in each of the suits Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs and Spades and their ranking from high to low and their point values are as follows

    Card rank Ace Ten King Queen Jack Nine
    Points 11 10 4 3 2 0

    Melds (King and Queen)

    Suit Points
    Hearts 100
    Diamonds 80
    Clubs 60
    Spades 40
