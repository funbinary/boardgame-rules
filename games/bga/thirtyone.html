    Thirtyone

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    Score the most points to avoid losing lives.

    Game play

    Select 1 action each turn:

    1. Swap 1 card in hand for 1 face-up card.
    2. Swap all cards in hand for all the face-up cards.
    3. Pass for the remainder of the round and keep the cards you have.

    Scoring

    Same Suit

    • 2 or 3 cards.
    • Add the values:
    Rank A K Q J 10 9…2
    Value 11 10 9…2

    Same Value

    • Must be 3 cards.
    • Always scores 30.5.

    Turn end

    A player loses a life with:

    • < 21 points, or
    • ≥ 21 points & lowest or tied lowest score.

    Rule set
    Schwimmen
    • A commerce game using a piquet pack.
    Blitz
    • A draw and discard game using a standard deck.
    Custom
    • Table creator can customize variant options.
    Exchange mechanism
    Commerce
    • May exchange 1 or 3 cards with face-up cards.
    • If all players consecutively pass, all face-up cards are replaced.
    Draw and discard
    • Draw from the deck or discard pile, then discard.
    • Cannot discard the same card taken from the discard pile.
    Three of a kind
    Fire
    • Three of a kind: 30.5 points.
    • 3×A: 32 points.
    Three of a kind
    • Three of a kind: 30.5 points.
    Disabled
    Remove 2 to 6
    Dealer hand exchange
    • Dealer may exchange their hand with 3 cards from the deck before play begins.
      • Draw and discard variant: exchanged cards are shuffled into the deck.
      • Commerce variant: exchanged cards are used as the initial face-up cards.
    Hand tiebreaker
    • Suit (for regular hands) or the rank (for threes of a kind) breaks hand value ties.
    31 affects all other players
    • Round end by closing or 31: player with the lowest hand value loses a life.
    • Round end by Fire: all players except the winner lose a life.
    Closer rule
    • Tie with the round closer: closer wins the tiebreak.
    • Closer's score is the sole lowest: loses 1 more life.
    Points limit
    • Round end: players with ≤ 20 points or lowest score > 20 lose 1 life (except the winner).
    First turn close limit
    • Non-dealers cannot close the round during their 1st turn.
