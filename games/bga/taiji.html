    Taiji

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.4 · 排名 #14617 · 127人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Have the most pieces in the allotted quantity of groups


    Turn

    On your turn, place a piece on an empty pair of spaces, each piece has both your and your opponent's colour on it


    Game End

    The game ends when there are no more possible moves

    In the 7x7 grid, the single largest interconnected group of pieces determine the winner

    In the 8x8 grid, add together the TWO largest interconnected groups of pieces to determine the winner

    In the 9x9 grid, add together the THREE largest interconnected groups of pieces to determine the winner
