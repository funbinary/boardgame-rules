    Ekko

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Empty your hand the fastest, by playing cards higher than odds, lower than evens, and cards which are the reverse of what the active number is


    Turn

    On a turn, you must try to play a card on top of what came before

    If the current card is odd (+), then you must play a higher card

    If the current card is even (-), then you must play a lower card

    If you cannot meet this requirement, then you have to draw a card

    If you play a card in the 11s, 11, 22, 33, 44 and so on, you get another go straight away

    If your last played card is the next card you see, i.e. no one else could play, you can play any card ignoring odd or even rules

    You can interrupt out of turn by playing a mirror of what is displayed, this is the numbers in reverse e.g. 57 can be interrupted with 75, units and whole tens are mirrors of each other e.g. 02 vs 20

    If you successfully play a mirror, either discard a card of your choice (not to the play area, out of the game) or cause everyone else to draw a card

    If you play an invalid card, either normally or the wrong mirror, you take it back and draw a card as penalty


    Round End

    The round ends when a player empties their hand

    Every other player scores 1 point for cards remaining in hand, and 2 points for a card of the 11s in hand

    Then the cards are shuffled and a new round starts


    Game Ends

    Once a player meets or exceeds 25 points, the player with the lowest score wins!

    Note: At this time, the BGA implementation only plays to 12 points.
