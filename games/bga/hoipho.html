    Hoipho

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    👥 3–4 人 ⭐ BGG 6.1 · 排名 #20818 · 权重 1.80/5 · 100人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    During the game, players will try to gain Contracts by having the most money each round or by using Merchant’s skills. The first player to gain a required number of Contracts is the final winner. There are 15 merchants (12 merchants in the 2018 version of Hội Phố) with 15 unique skills and 5 Ship Cards. Players play a card simultaneously and compare reputation, the players who have the highest and second highest reputation merchant gain money while the lowest merchant triggers skill.

    Each round contains 4 turns (Player plays 4 out of 5 cards in hand). In a 5 player game, there are only 3 turns per round. Money and cards in hand are always hidden (unless the player is required to reveal it).

    Each Turn has the following steps:

    - Put a card facedown

    - Reveal a card

    - Resolve and reward

    After finishing the last turn (the 4th turn in a game of 3-4 players or the 3rd turn in a game of 5 players), the round ends.

    All players reveal their money and the player with the most money gains 1 Contract card (the top one of the contract deck)
