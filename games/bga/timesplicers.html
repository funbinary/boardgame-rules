    🎴 TIME SPLICERS — How to Play

    🕒 Objective

    Your goal is to collect 7 Time Crystals before your opponents.

    Time Crystals are earned by causing Timeline Collapses.

    🧩 Setup

    • Each player receives:
      • 1 Meeple
      • 2 Time Jump tokens
      • 2 Time Freeze markers
    • Each player draws 4 cards.
    • Reveal one card to create the first Timeline.
    • Place all meeples on this starting card.

    🔄 Turn Structure

    On your turn, you must perform ONE main action:

    1️⃣ Move to an Adjacent Card

    Move your meeple:

    • to an adjacent card in the same Timeline;
    • OR to a card with the same number in a different Timeline.

    ➡️ Then draw 1 card.

    2️⃣ Use the Time Portal

    The Time Portal allows you to teleport anywhere.

    First Turn:

    • Move to the Time Portal.
    • Draw 2 cards.

    Next Turn:

    • Move from the Portal to any card in play.
    • OR place a card and move onto it.

    3️⃣ Place a Card and Move Onto It

    You may place either:

    ➤ An Adjacent Card

    The card must:

    • be the same Timeline color;
    • follow the Timeline’s numerical order.

    Cards may be shifted to make room for an inserted value.

    ➤ A Parallel Card

    You may create a new Timeline using:

    • a different color;
    • but the same number (Era).

    ⏳ Fixed Points in Time

    Whenever there are:

    • 3 cards of the same Era → gain 1 Time Jump token.
    • 4 cards of the same Era → gain another Time Jump token.

    ✋ Hand Limits

    Minimum Hand Size: 4 Cards

    At the end of your turn:

    • if you have fewer than 4 cards, draw until you have 4.

    Maximum Hand Size: 7 Cards

    If you ever exceed 7 cards:

    1. Discard your entire hand.
    2. Lose 1 Time Jump token.
    3. Draw 4 new cards.

    ⚠️ If you cannot pay the penalty:

    you are eliminated from the game.

    ✨ Special Cards

    Special cards may be played at the beginning of your turn as a free action.

    🌀 Quantum Tunnel

    Move to any card in play before taking your normal action.

    ⚡ Time Jump

    Move to any other card within the same Timeline.

    A Time Jump token may also be used to:

    • discard your hand;
    • draw 4 new cards.

    ❄️ Time Freeze

    Place this card at the end of a Timeline before leaving it to prevent that Timeline from collapsing when empty.

    When you return to that Timeline:

    • discard your Time Freeze card;
    • recover your marker.

    ⚠️ Time Freeze does NOT prevent:

    • Partial Timeline Collapses;
    • Complete Timeline Collapses.

    💎 Earning Time Crystals

    🧨 1. Empty Timeline Collapse

    When you leave a Timeline:

    • with no other players remaining;
    • and no Time Freeze card protecting it,

    the Timeline collapses.

    ➡️ If it contained at least 3 cards:

    gain 1 Time Crystal.

    🔻 2. Partial Timeline Collapse

    When you create a sequence of 3 consecutive cards:

    • 1-2-3
    • 4-5-6
    • etc.

    ➡️ The sequence is discarded.

    Meeples on those cards move to the nearest remaining card on the left.

    ➡️ Gain 1 Time Crystal.

    ☢️ 3. Complete Timeline Collapse

    If a Timeline containing a Singularity (13) reaches 5 cards:

    ➡️ The entire Timeline is discarded.

    Meeples are moved to another Timeline.

    ➡️ Gain 2 Time Crystals.

    🏆 End of the Game

    The game ends immediately when a player collects their 7ᵗʰ Time Crystal.

    That player wins the game and successfully saves humanity. 🚀⏳
