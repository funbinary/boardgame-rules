    Miams

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.8 · 排名 #5486 · 权重 1.53/5 · 620人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Object

    Get the most points by rolling dice to mark off fruit skewers. The game ends when someone fills 50 hearts by marking skewers. Play until everyone has had the same number of turns, and then highest score wins.

    Flow of Play

    Each turn, go through these 3 phases:

    Roll

    Roll all 5 white dice to try and make the best possible fruit skewer (aka. dice combination) If your roll would allow more than one skewer, you get to choose which one to score.

    Possibilities are:

    Pair
    - 2 identical fruits
    Three of a kind
    - 3 identical fruits
    Full house
    - 3 identical fruits and 2 other identical fruits
    Four of a kind
    - 4 identical fruits
    Straight
    - 5 different fruits
    Yums
    - 5 identical fruits

    You may reroll, ONCE, as many dice as you wish.

    Score

    Circle the next free box for the skewer you made.

    Score Hearts (in other words points) and Bonus tokens depending on the skewer you made. Be aware that the bonuses are different on each score sheet. If the heart track is full, take the remainder in stars; the end of the game triggers.

    If no skewer scores, mark off a "catch-up" space - you'll get the associated bonus.

    For every cross rolled, the other players cross off a free scoring box of their choice. They get the bonuses but not the points.

    If you mark (either circle or cross out) all the scoring boxes in the first column, score 3 stars.

    Bonuses are:

    Reroll token
    - Spend a reroll token to reroll dice an extra time
    Stars
    - Stars are spent in the buy round, or are each a point at the end of the game
    Red Die
    - Spend before rolling to add the red die. The red die has a wild marshmallow face instead of a cross.

    Buy

    Buy a Yums card to obtain powerful effects

    Choose a card from the display, paying stars based on it's position. Cards slide down to fill.

    Types of cards:

    Permanent cards
    - ongoing abilities for the rest of the game
    One time use
    - discard to get a benefit
    End of turn
    - trigger after every scoring round
