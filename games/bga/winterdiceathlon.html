    Winterdiceathlon

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Ice Hockey:

    Attackers are players in red squares. Defenders are players in blue squares. The difference between your attackers and other players defenders is the number of points scored, if your rivals defender score is greater than your attacker score then you score 0. You play vs every other player one by one.

    Skeleton:

    Must be filled from top to bottom. Numbers must be equals to or lower than the number in the box. Lowest total sum wins.

    Slalom:

    Starting from the top, you may enter any number in the first square. Any subsequent number, depending on the symbol in the box, must be either greater than (>) or less than (<) the previous number. Lowest total sum wins. In case of needing to write a number >6 or <1 you may write any number in the box.

    Ski Jump:

    Must be filled from left to right. Subtract your launch and push off scores and your in air and landing scores then multiply them together. Highest score wins.

    Biathlon:

    Enter numbers from left to right. Subtract the target number from the dice used at every shooting station. If you enter the target number write a 3 instead. Highest total sum wins.
