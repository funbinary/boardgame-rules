    Pergola

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 7.3 · 排名 #1800 · 权重 2.03/5 · 2179人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    You are a gardener. Over 15 rounds you will grow and place plants, attract insects, and use special actions to develop your garden. At the end, you score on multiple categories, and the player with the highest total wins.

    Setup

    Each player gets: a garden board, a pergola board, a cascade (water) board, a scoring sheet, and starting tokens (frog, 2 water drops, birdhouse, honey pots). There is a shared action board with 4 action spaces, and a pool of tools (shovels, rakes, trowels, hoes) used in draft/action selection. The insect tokens, plants, lanterns, leaves, etc., are placed in a central supply.

    Turn Structure

    Each turn has 4 steps:

    1. Pick a Tool: Choose one of the tools from the action board. This also “locks in” the action associated with that spot.
    2. Enrich Your Garden: Take the plant/insect tokens indicated by the tool, and place them in your garden (or insects in free flying space) following placement rules.
    3. Perform the Action: The tool you picked tells you which of 4 actions to carry out:
      • Insect Flutter: move insects (ladybugs, butterflies, bees) in your garden
      • Bird Flight: draw a leaf from the leaf piles, then take the insect shown
      • Water Drop: move one water drop down the cascade; if it lands on an insect, you gain it
      • Frog Jump: move your frog to a waterlily in the pond, and gain the insect/dragonfly if conditions are met
    4. Cleanup / Refresh: Refill the gap on the action board, slide tools, and draw a new tool to replenish. The tool you used goes into your tool pot to mark your turn count.

    Repeat until each player has taken 15 turns.

    Placement & Special Rules

    Plants & insects must follow placement restrictions (e.g. magnolias branch, insects go on matching plants or flying space).

    Dragonflies require prerequisites before you can collect them.

    Lanterns give special one-time bonuses.

    Honey pots are filled by bees on buttercups and score points.

    Some actions chain (e.g. extra water drip).

    End game scoring

    At game end, players score from:

    • Plants
      • Each row of 3/2/1 different colored hollyhock flowers gives 6/2/1 points.
      • Each magnolia on level 1-3/4/5+ scores 1/2/3 points (whether it is single or double branch).
      • Each pair of pink+blue buttercups gives 3 points (whether there is a bee or not).
    • Insects
      • Each butterfly on level 1/2-3/4+ scores 1/2/3 points.
      • Each ladybug scores 1/2/3 points if there are 1/2/3+ ladybugs on its level.
      • Bees give points only for plants that are fully occupied. 2/4 points per buttercups with 1/2 bees, 8 points for lavenders with 3 bees.
      • Each small/medium/large dragonfly scores 1/3/5 points.
    • Filled honey pots
    • Leaves of different colors on the birdhouse
    • Cascade bonuses

    Highest total wins.
