    Fightfive

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.2 · 排名 #15494 · 169人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    Collect the most points by winning Clashes against your opponents. The game ends after 3 Bouts or when a player reaches 200 points.

    Card Reference

    • Number Cards (2–13): Used to meet or exceed the value of the active Clash Card.
    • Star Cards: Wildcards that automatically beat any number card. If two Stars are played, they result in a tie.

    Gameplay

    A Bout consists of 5 Clashes for a stack of cards. All players act simultaneously.

    1. Setup: Each player draws 5 cards from their own deck.
    2. The Clash:
      • The top card of a 5-card Clash stack is revealed.
      • Each player chooses one of their 5 hand cards and places it face-down.
      • All cards are revealed simultaneously.
    3. Determining the Winner:
      • Your card must be at least equal to the value of the revealed Clash Card to be eligible.
      • Among eligible players, the highest card wins.
      • The winner takes the Clash Card and all player cards played this round into their Point Pile.
    4. Ties & Misses:
      • If no one meets the Clash Card value or the highest cards played are tied, there is no winner.
      • All cards remain in the pot to be won in the next Clash.
    5. Next Clash: Reveal the next card from the stack until all 5 hand cards have been played.

    Scoring

    At the end of each Bout, players count their Point Piles:

    • 5 points: Each card with a value of 10 or more.
    • 10 points: Each Star Card.
    • 15 point bonus: Awarded to the player with the most cards in their Point Pile.

    Game End

    The game ends after 3 Bouts or once a player hits 200 points. The player with the highest score wins.
