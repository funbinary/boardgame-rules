    Fixtheteleporter

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    In Please Fix the Teleporter, you are a teleportation specialist competing to match a scrambled image. Flip and rotate your set of tiles to match the exact orientation and arrangement of the image on the card. Then be the first to slap the card to claim it. Claim 5 cards to win the game!

    Setup

    Shuffle the cards into a deck and place them face down in the middle of the table. Give each player a set of four tiles in their chosen color.

    Gameplay

    Flip one card face up on the table. You and your opponents must then race to assemble your tiles to match the pattern shown on the flipped card. When you believe you have matched the pattern with the correct orientation and arrangement, you must slap your hand onto the card to end the round immediately. If you were the first to slap the card, and all players verify that your tile arrangement matches the card exactly, then you may claim the card. If the pattern you create does NOT match the card, the card is placed face down under the deck, and you may not participate in the next round. Flip a new card from the deck to begin the next round.

    Winning the Game

    The first player to claim 5 cards wins!
