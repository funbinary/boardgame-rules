    Deluxecamping

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Set Up

    Players take turns first placing their bungalow then their 6 trailers.

    Bungalows may not be placed immediately next to the pool or next to the another player's bungalow.

    A fence will be placed automatically between the two pieces if a trailer is placed orthogonally adjacent to another player's piece.

    The three mystery tokens (2 ice creams and 1 pain in the neck guy) will be revealed.

    Game Play

    1. Movements - players have a total 2 movements divided between trailers or pain in the neck guy. Fences will be placed automatically when your trailer is moved immediately next to your opponent's bungalow or trailer.

    2. Add a fence - you can add a fence anywhere in the playground.

    Game End & Scoring

    Game ends when each section only contains one player's piece.

    Player score each square in their areas.

    Ice cream = +5 points if in your area Pain in the neck guy = -10 points, min 0 Pool = number of squares in your area x 2
