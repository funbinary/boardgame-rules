    Goblinhood

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Have the most gold twice


    Turn

    You always have 2 cards to choose from

    Place a card onto one end of the trail by overlapping one half with a half already present

    The half played and the half overlapped must match in colour

    If you cannot play a card, you are out for the round

    The new half which overlapped the old half has its effect triggered, NOT the half that hangs off

    Draw back to 2 cards at turn end


    Round End

    The round ends when the set number of draw deck depletions occur

    All treasure and remaining hand cards score, and the player with the most points wins the round

    If this was that player's second win, they win!
