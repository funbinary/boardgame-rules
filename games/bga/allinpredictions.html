    All In

    In this tactical bluffing game for 2-5 players, players use the powers of the Foretellers to manipulate their hands. The goal is to either hold the strongest hand at the end of the round or correctly predict the winner to collect the most runes.

    Gameplay

    The game consists of 3 rounds. In each round, players aim to build a set of 5 cards in front of them.

    • Setup: Each player is dealt 5 cards. 2 cards are placed face-up in the "Market" frame.
    • Player Turns: On your turn, you must play exactly 1 of 3 possible actions:
      • Play a card from your hand:
        • Choose any card from your hand place it face up in front of you.
        • Perform the effect(s) of that card.
        • Add the card you just played to the display.
      • Pass:
        • Draw 1 card from the deck and add it to the display.
      • Go All-In:
        • Put your 5 hand cards face down in front of you.
        • Draw 2 cards from the deck and add them to the display.
        • Only one player can Go All In each round.
        • If you went All In, you must pass on each of your following turns.
    • Prediction:
      • Once the display limit of two cards per row is reached, players secretly place their Prediction Token on the player (themselves or an opponent) they believe will hold the strongest hand.
    • Showdown:
      • Everyone reveals their final 5-card hand.
      • Scoring the Pot: The pot is split among all the people who predicted correctly.
      • Scoring Hands: Each player earns Runes according to their hand's rank (see below).
      • Rune Cards: For each Rune Card in your hand, you get 2 additional Runes.

    End of Game

    • Winner: After 3 rounds, the player with the most runes wins.

    Hand Rankings

    Hand strength follows standard Poker rankings (from highest to lowest):

    • 10 STRAIGHT FLUSH (5 cards of the same suit in sequence)
    • 7 FOUR-OF-A-KIND (4 cars of the same value)
    • 6 FULL HOUSE (Three-of-a-kind + Pair)
    • 5 FLUSH (5 cards of the same suit)
    • 4 STRAIGHT (5 card in numerical order)
    • 3 THREE-OF-A-KIND (3 cards of the same value)
    • 2 2 PAIRS
    • 1 1 PAIR
