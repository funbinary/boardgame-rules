    Streetcar

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.4 · 排名 #4013 · 权重 2.05/5 · 1882人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    The game is divided into two phases. In the first phase, you will connect terminals and buildings with track tiles. In the second phase, you control your trolley from terminal to terminal and make the necessary stops in between. The first one to do so wins the game.

    Gameplay

    In phase 1, players take turns to place 2 tiles on the board, until you decide to move on to phase 2. When placing, each tile must be placed on to an empty space of the board or replace a tile with brown background. Green tiles cannot be replaced. When replacing a brown tile, it must make the same connections as the existing one. When planning your route, keep in mind that your streetcar will only go forward and can only do turns less than 90 degrees.

    Tracks can never :

    - Lead off the board;

    - Lead into a building;

    - Have end(s) that cannot be continued.

    To fulfill a route, connect one terminal that matches your number to the other terminal that matches your number on the other side of the board, passing by each of the three stops. You can pass the stops in any order

    Once the route is fulfilled, you may select to start phase 2. From now on, you will act with phase 2 rules.

    In a deviation from the published rules, in the BGA version, once a player places their train, their tiles become available for folks who are still placing tiles.

    Phase 2

    Roll 3 dice and choose 1 to execute, then you can choose to pass or roll 2 dice and execute 1. Then you can choose to pass or roll 1 dice to execute. Then your turn ends.

    The dice have the following meaning:

    • Red Light - Trolley does not move.
    • One Green Lights - Trolley moves ahead one space.
    • Two Green Lights (2 faces) - Trolley moves ahead two spaces.
    • H - Trolley moves to next stop or terminal spot.
    • Tools - Trolley must go back to last station or terminal for repairs. This also ends the player's turn.

    In a deviation from the published rules, in the BGA version, there are 2x Two Green Light faces and 1x Tools face.

    Scoring

    The current BGA version allows all players to play until the very end. As soon as a player reaches the destination station with their tram, they receive 1 point for each milestone that their opponents have not yet reached.

    The key milestones are:

    • Placing the tram on the game board.
    • Each of the stops indicated on their task card (2 or 3, depending on player count).
    • Reaching the destination station.

    If someone presses the “Give Up” button, they lose the game with 0 points.
