    Forks

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.2 · 排名 #10658 · 权重 1.33/5 · 296人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Manage the market whilst making sure to keep hold of high value destinations

    Each destination has 11 cards, 10 numbered 3 to 12, then one extra depending on the color: grey 8, blue 7, yellow 6, green 5 and red 4, making a total of 55 cards. In two and three player games cards with value 10,11,12 are removed.

    Depending on number of players a set number of cards are removed (without revealing) at a set start of game and cards are drawn to Market.

    Game Setup
    Number of Players 2 3 4 5 6
    Cards removed at start of game 9 1 4 7 1
    Initial marketing cards 3 3 3 3 0

    Turn

    Draft 1 of 3 cards into your hand, then pass the remaining

    Draft 1 of the 2 cards into your hand, then the remaining goes into the central Market

    In two player games at the end of the round the top card of the deck is revealed and placed into the central Market.

    Game End

    The game ends when the deck runs out

    Out of all the 5 destinations, the three in the Market with the highest score will score positively

    Whilst the 2 in the Market with the lowest score will score negatively

    Reveal your hand cards and score each according to its value and whether it is a positive or negative scoring destination as depicted by the Market

    The player with the most points, wins!
