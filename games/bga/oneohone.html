    Oneohone

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    You will be placing cards in a 5x5 grid.

    Each row and column will be compared against your competitors. For each row and column, the player with the best hand will score 1 or 2 points.

    Method

    On your turn, either:

    • Place the face-up card on your grid, then flip another 'top' card, or
    • Select another 'top' card, causing it to flip, then place it on your grid

    Cards can only be placed if they are beside another card (up/down, left/right or touching a corner).

    The front face of the card will always be one of the colours and numbers shown on the back of the card. This will help you try to get a desired card.

    Scoring

    The better combination of cards on each row/column will beat competitors combinations.

    Cards can be IN ANY ORDER.

    Combinations from Best to Worst are:

    Combination Example Example Score
    Straight Flush  1   2   3   4   5  5 Consecutive numbers (in any order) all in the same colour 2
    5 of a Kind  4   4   4   4   4  5 of the same number (any colours) 2
    4 of a Kind  4   4   4   4  4 of the same number (any colours) 1
    Full House  4   4   4   7   7  3 of a Kind and a Pair (any order, any colours) 1
    Straight  1   2   3   4   5  5 Consecutive Numbers (in any order) 1
    Flush  4   7   9   2   3  All cards same colour (any numbers) 1
    3 of a Kind  4   4   4  3 of the same number (any colours) 1
    2 Pairs  4   4   7   7  2 pairs of cards, each with the same number (any colours) 1
    Cocktail  4   7   9   2   2  5 different colours (any numbers) 1
    1 Pair  4   4  2 cards with the same number (any colours) 1

    Strategy

    • Look at the back of cards to find ones that will help your combinations
    • Take cards that your opponent wants
    • If an opponent's row/column is unbeatable, use that row/column as a 'trash can' for your worthless cards
