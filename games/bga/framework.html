    Framework

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Be the first to place all your tokens on the frame tiles in your display by fulfilling the objectives on the tiles.


    Turn

    One player takes the bag and draws tiles equal to the number of players +1 (so 5 in a 4 player game).

    That player then selects one tile from those available and places it orthogonally adjacent to those in their display.

    In clockwise order, the other players do the same.

    When it comes to the final tile, the bag drawer takes this one too.

    Then the bag is passed on for a new bag drawer.


    Tasks

    Tiles are made up of objectives (number in circle) and frames (border).

    The objectives tell you how many frames of what colour need to be in a contiguous group touching that tile.

    Some tiles may have frames that help satisfy their own objective (e.g., a tile with objective "3 green with 1 green frame" next to a tile with "2 green frames and no objectives") which satisfies the objective of 3 green frames.


    Once an objective has been met, cover the objective with one of your tokens. Use all your tokens to win.

    There are four types of objectives:

    • One type requires a number of a single colour frame.
    • Another requires a combo of two colours in any amount equaling the total.
    • Another gives an OPTION of two colours, but only ONE can be used to complete.
    • The final type requires one objective to be completed before the other, shown by an arrow.


    Game End

    The game can end immediately if a player places their final token on an objective and wins!

    Alternatively, if the final tiles are drawn and no one has achieved this yet, after this round the player with the most completed objectives wins.
