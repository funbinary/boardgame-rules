    Gopher

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 4.2 · 排名 #30553 · 权重 1.50/5 · 79人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Red starts the game by placing a red checker anywhere on the board. Thereafter, Blue and Red take turns adding their own checkers to the board. You must place your checker next to only one enemy checker but no friendly checkers. The last player to place a checker wins.
