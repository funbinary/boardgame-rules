    Diceforgerebellion

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Rebellion is an Expansion for Dice Forge.

    You are recommended to play Dice Forge first to get the basic rules.

    Dice Forge basic Rules

    You control two dice. The sides can be changed by "forging" using gold. You collect Moon shards (Blue) and Sun Shards (Red) to complete Heroic Feats. Heroic Feats can grant victory points, one off or ongoing effects. Two Sun shards can also be used to take a second go.

    In the base game you can vary the cards available as Heroic Feats.

    Rebellion Rules

    This adds two "modules" which can be played individually (there is no option to combine them). There are also additional/alternative cards available.

    Goddess

    There is now a new area, a labryinth, where your golem (gifted to you by the Goddess) travels and unlocks special bonuses.

    New Die faces make your golem move forward, and with some Heroic Deeds (cards) backwards, allowing you to navigate through to access bonuses.

    Titans

    This module has not been developed for BoardGameArena.
