    Imperialminers

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Core Mechanisms

    • Simultaneous Play: All players act at the same time during each phase.
    • Chain Reactions: Every newly placed card activates itself and one card above it in a sequence leading up to the Surface board.
    • Faction Synergies: Six different factions offer unique strategic paths.

    Card Types & Factions

    Mine cards are divided into four levels (I to IV), built into the mine from top to bottom.

    • Scots: Powerful effects with costs that decrease for every other Scottish card in your mine.
    • Atlanteans: Utilize Machine tokens that grow in power over the course of the game.
    • Egyptians: Focus on rapid progression along the Progress boards.
    • Japanese: Benefit from having a wide variety of different factions within your mine.
    • Barbarians: Extremely strong effects but often come with the risk of Collapse tokens.
    • Romans: Reward precise placement and the collection of completed Carts.

    Gameplay

    The game lasts exactly **10 rounds**. Each round consists of two phases:

    1. Event Phase

    • Draw one card from the Event deck.
    • All players resolve its effect (Immediate, Ongoing, or End of Round).

    2. Mine Phase

    All players perform these steps simultaneously:

    1. Play a Card: Choose a card from your hand, pay its cost, and place it in the corresponding level of your mine.
      • Placement Rule: Every card must be adjacent to at least one card directly above it (or the Surface board).
    2. Trigger Chain Reaction:
      • Activate the newly placed card (choose one of its effects).
      • Then, choose one adjacent card directly above it and activate it.
      • Repeat this until you reach the Surface board, where you choose one of its three bonus effects.
      • Each card can be activated only once per round.

    Important In-Game Details

    • Machines: Can be placed on cards to enhance effects (max 3 per card).
    • Collapse: Blocks a card. When activated, only the Collapse token is removed instead of resolving the card's effect.
    • Carts: Can be completed between cards to score points at the end.
    • Progress: Move your marker on a Progress board to unlock powerful one-time bonuses.
    • Hand Limit: Maximum of 8 cards after resolving any effect.

    End of Game & Scoring

    After 10 rounds, the player with the most Victory Points wins.

    • Total sum of Victory Point tokens.
    • +1 point for every completed Cart in the mine.
    • Ties are broken by the most gold, then by the most cards in the mine.
