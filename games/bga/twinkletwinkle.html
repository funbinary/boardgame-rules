    Twinkle Twinkle

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Twinkle Twinkle is a tile placement game where your main goal is to score the most points as you fill out the night sky.

    On Your Turn

    Select a tile and place it anywhere in your sky. You may rotate and flip the tile.

    The tile you take will also be your turn order for the next round. The next round's tiles are below the current round tiles.

    In a Two Player game you will place twice.

    Your goal is to "connect the dots" to create 2 large clusters of stars. A constellation can have all sorts of turns but any 2 stars are only connected by a straight"ish" line. "V" shaped connections are not valid.

    Base Scoring

    2 Largest Constellations:

    Count up the number of stars in your largest and 2nd-largest constellations and score the points listed under your star chart

    Number of Constellations:

    Count how many constellations of at least 2 stars you have and score the points listed under your star chart

    NOVA STARS

    Nova stars are the same as regular stars, except that they are each worth 1 point at the end of the game. Nova stars can be included in constellations

    Variable Scoring

    Follow the directions on the scoring tiles. Any scoring tiles that say adjacenct only score for orthogonally adjacent tiles. Adjacent tiles never include diagonal tiles.
