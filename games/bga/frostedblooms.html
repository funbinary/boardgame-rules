    Frosted Blooms

    This game comprises of tile laying and building improvements, which you can include by leaving spaces within your garden.

    Game Play

    Form clumps of of white flowers, purple flowers, tulips and watercourse.
    Each round, you choose an objective card from your hand to score. For example, if you've just added to a clump of purple flowers, then you might choose to score the purple flower card, giving you two points for each purple flower adjacent to the new additions.
    In addition to the objectives in your hand, there are group objectives available for the first player to reach them.

    Buildings

    Building a (vacant) 1x1 square gives a worker, 3pts and a coin.
    Building a (vacant) 2x1 square gives a barn and 10 points.
    Building a (vacant) 2x2 square gives a windmill and 25 points.
    The green circle with a question mark in the corner of your screen will explain buildings in more detail.

    Each Round

    • Take the tile clockwise of the (pink) flower token. If you have coins, you may pay a coin for each tile you wish to step over. They will be collected by the player taking the tile
    • Place your chosen tile within your starting garden, forming collections of white flowers, purple flowers, tulips and water.
    • Choose which objective card you wish to score this round.
    • Check the joint objectives available. (note that taking a lower point card in a category means you can't also take a higher one in that category as well).

    Scoring

    Buildings score at the end of the game, in addition to points from the objectives.
