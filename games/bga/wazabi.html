    Wazabi

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Objective

    Be the first player to get rid of all of your dice.

    Gameplay

    • Each player starts with the same number of dice (default 4) and 3 cards.
    • Dice sides are Wazabi x3, Draw Card x1, and Give Die x1
    • On your turn, roll all your dice. Draw 1 card for each Draw Card symbol rolled and give all dice with the Give Die symbol to players of your choice.
    • Then, play a card with at most the number of wazabi (W) symbols rolled. Apply its effect. It is not compulsory to play a card each turn.

    End of the Game

    • The first player to have 0 dice left wins the game.

    Cards List (with Cost)

    • Miss a turn (0): Choose a player. When it is supposed to be their next turn, skip it and discard this card.
    • Reverse: (0): Gain another turn and reverse the game direction.
    • Draw (1): Draw 3 cards from the draw pile.
    • Hand limit 1 (1): Choose an opponent to discard their hand down to 1.
    • Lose a dice (1): Withdraw 1 of your dice from the game. This card cannot be played if 2 dice are given by you this round.
    • Steal a card (1): Steal a random card from an opponent's hand. If not possible, draw 1 from the deck.
    • Exchange (2): Exchange all your cards with an opponent of your choice.
    • Hand limit 2 (2): All opponents must discard their hand down to 2.
    • Pass cards (2): Choose a direction. Each player (including you) passes all their cards to the player on their left or right.
    • Pass dice (2): Choose a direction. Each player (including you) passes all their dice to the player on their left or right.
    • Exchange dice (3): Exchange all your dice with the opponent of your choice.
    • Lose 2 dice (3): Withdraw 2 of your dice from the game.
