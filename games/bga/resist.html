    Resist!

    Resist! is a solo card game where the player leads a group of Spanish resistance fighters (Maquis) against the Franco regime. The game ends after a certain number of rounds or if the resistance is crushed.

    Game Objective

    Collect as many Victory Points (VP) as possible by completing missions. The game ends immediately in defeat if 5 civilians are killed or the resistance runs out of cards to draw.

    Quick Setup

    • Create a deck of Maquis cards.
    • Set up a display of Mission cards.
    • Place enemies (Civil Guards) face-down on the missions.
    • Draw a starting hand of 5 Maquis cards.

    Game Flow

    The game is played over several rounds until the player chooses to end the resistance or loses. Each round has four phases:

    1. Planning Phase

    • Play Maquis cards from your hand.
    • Each card has two modes: Hidden and Revealed.
    • Hidden: Usually provides lower combat values but returns to your discard pile at the end of the round.
    • Revealed: The card is significantly stronger but is permanently removed from the game after the round (it goes to the "sacrifice" pile).
    • Use card abilities (e.g., reveal enemies, draw cards, boost attack values).

    2. Attack Phase

    • Choose one Mission from the display to attack.
    • Calculate your total Attack Value (sum of all played Maquis values).
    • Reveal all enemies defending the chosen mission.
    • Overcoming Defense: You must meet or exceed the mission's defense value to succeed.
    • Eliminating Enemies: You can also use your attack value to permanently defeat individual defenders (Civil Guards).

    3. Aftermath Phase

    • Check the effects of any remaining enemies on the mission (many will attack or kill civilians if not defeated).
    • If the mission was successful, take the card for VP. If not, it remains, but you suffer the consequences listed on the card.

    4. Recovery Phase

    • Place all "Hidden" cards into your discard pile.
    • Remove all "Revealed" cards from the game.
    • Draw a new hand of 5 cards. If the deck is empty, reshuffle the discard pile (unless specific defeat conditions apply).

    Card Categories & Symbols

    • Attack Value (Number): Your strength during the Attack Phase.
    • Mission Value: The strength required to win the mission.
    • Civilian Symbol: Found on missions or enemies; if 5 of these are lost, the game ends immediately.
    • Spies: These cards clog your deck. They have no combat value and must often be removed using special abilities.

    End of Game & Scoring

    The game ends when:

    1. The player decides to stop the resistance (after any round).
    2. No more missions are available.
    3. Defeat conditions are met (5 civilians dead, Maquis deck empty, or 2 missions failed).
    • Scoring: Total the VP of completed missions. Your rank is determined by a table in the rulebook (ranging from "Historical Defeat" to "Resistance Victory").
