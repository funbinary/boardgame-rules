    Coffee

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.4 · 排名 #16113 · 权重 2.56/5 · 115人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Place pieces to try to form a line whilst making sure there is always a spot for your opponent to place


    Setup

    If using the Pie rule, 2nd player may choose to swap pieces after 1st player's initial placement


    Turn

    On the first turn, Black places a piece on any space then adds the rod facing any direction

    After which, alternate turns by placing a piece on a space along the rod's direction and moving the rod to your piece and picking a direction for it to rest

    The rod must always point to at least 1 empty space


    Game End

    If a player makes a line of a pre-determined length, they win!

    If a player is unable to point the rod to an empty space, they lose!

    If a player makes a line but cannot place the rod, they win!
