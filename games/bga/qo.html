    Qo

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 3.4 · 排名 #0 · 5人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Place pieces whilst trying NOT to form a line from one end to the other, and having more pieces than your opponent with a difference of no more than 7


    Turn

    On your turn, either Place or Move

    If you Place, place a piece on any empty space

    If you Move, pay your opponent 2 pieces in order to move any piece already out (yours or your opponent's) one space, you can move this same piece any number of spaces paying the 2 piece cost for each space moved

    After taking your main action, remove any pieces which are now blocked in, these can either be blocked by opposing pieces or the wall


    Game End

    If a player forms a line of their pieces from one end to the other, they lose

    Otherwise, the game ends when a player runs out of pieces to place, in which case add together the number of both board AND supply pieces and the player with the most, wins!

    However, if the difference in score is 8 or greater, the player with the lower score wins!
