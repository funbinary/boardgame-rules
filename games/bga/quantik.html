    Quantik

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    ⭐ BGG 6.1 · 排名 #11966 · 权重 1.56/5 · 462人评分 📄 BGA 官方文档

    Game play

    • Each player gets 2 spheres, 2 cylinders, 2 cubes and 2 cones in their colour.
    • A random player starts.
    • When it is your turn, you can place a piece on the board, so long as there is not already an opponent's piece of the same shape in the same row, column or region.

    Game End

    • The first player to place the fourth different shape in a row, column, or region wins the game.
    • If a player cannot make a valid move, they lose.
