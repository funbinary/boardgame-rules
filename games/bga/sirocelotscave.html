    Sirocelotscave

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    In Sir Ocelot's Cave, Sir Ocelot and Professor Penguin explore a newly discovered cave system, competing for the precious gemstones found inside. To locate and collect gemstones, you will be using your tools — compass, lamp, and pickaxe — and your trusty companion’s good instincts. The deeper you get into the cave, the more valuable treasure you will find! In the end, the player with the most valuable collection of gemstones wins the game!

    Have fun exploring Sir Ocelot's Cave!

    How to play

    Players alternate taking turns until the game ends. You must go through the following steps in this order:

    1. Move your companion (optional) Starting with the first cave (blue level), you can assign your trusty companion to any empty space or one containing a (1-point) rock crystal. If placed on a (1-point) rock crystal, this is yours. Once inside a cave, you only can move your companion forward to the next cave level. You may move your companion only once per turn.

    NOTE: If you not feel like placing your companion in step 1., proceed with steps 2. to 3. until you want to start your turn by placing it.

    2. Place a token (mandatory) Regardless if you placed or moved your companion yet, you MUST place exactly one token: - either a tool on an empty circular space on the perimeter of a cave - or - if you have not already - the companion in side the (blue level) cave

    Your goal is to place the token such that you can collect gemstones. Be sure to check all visual axis after placing a token IRL. Here on boardgamerarena.com, the implementation will do this for you.

    You may place tools freely on the perimeter of any cave if the space is still vacant. you are not required to start at level 1. Note that tools are double-sided, showing different tools on both sides.


    3. Collect gemstones (optional) After placing a token, check if you can collect any gemstones: to collect a gemstone, it must be "seen" from three different sides by three different tools. One of the tools may be your trusty companion. It serves as a wild tool for collection gemstones, i.e. it can replace a missing tool for YOU.

    Note: Your companion does not block the view for your opponent, but it will only serve you to collect.

    The game ends when you both run out of tokens to place. The player with the most valuable collection wins.

    What to collect

    Gemstones: score points as shown on the token

    Geodes: are innocent looking rocks that need to be opened. When you collect a geode, immediately draw an amethyst from the corresponding bag (matching the color of the cave); the geode will be discarded. Amethysts are worth varying amounts of points. Their value is kept hidden until the end of the game.

    There are four special amethysts whose value is calculated at the end of the game:

    • The 1x1 amethyst, which scores 1 point for each 1-point gemstone you have collected.
    • The 1x3 amethyst, which scores 1 point for each 3-point gemstone you have collected.
    • The 1x blue amethyst, which scores 1 point for each remaining gemstone in the blue cave at the end of the game.
    • The 2x green/red amethyst, which scores 2 point for each remaining gemstone in the green and red caves at the end of the game.

    You can see these by looking in your gem bag; they remain secret from your opponent.

    Celestites: This mysterious type of gemstone is only available in level-1-cave and comes in three shapes: a sun, a crescent moon and a star. Whenever you collect a set of all three shapes, immediately draw an amethyst from the blue bag. Unlike geodes, you get to keep the celestites.
