    📄 BGA 官方文档

    In Above, you must be the first to move one of the Deities on Mount Olympus (the center of the board).

    • Players: 2 (duel) or 4 (teams of 2).

    It begins

    Each player gets the same 8 unique Deity cards (face up, "Powered").

    A player must activate one Deity cards at his turn, move the matching Deity pawn on the bard 1 space, then trigger its power.

    When you have no more Powered cards left, they all come back to Powered immediately!

    On Your Turn

    Activate a Deity Card: Choose one of your Powered deity cards

    Move the Deity Pawn: Move the corresponding deity pawn one space, on a free space, forward the center or sideways only. Never backward.

    Resolve and remove any token triggered (fertility, thunder or gate).

    Activate the Deity's Power: Use the Deity's special ability.


    4 Players Game

    Each team gets the same 8 unique Deity cards (face up, "Powered").

    Each player gets a random pick of 4 Deities.

    When you have no more Powered cards left, 4 new ones will be picked randomly from your team's discard!
