    Marcopolo

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Game ends after 5 rounds.

    Actions:

    • Once per turn, place some your dice on an action space, one die per square.
    • Typically, the lowest value die you place indicates how many times you may take the action, or how much you get.
    • You may place dice on any unoccupied action space.
    • You may place dice on an occupied blue action space by paying as many coins as the die value of the lowest die you place.
    • You may not place dice on an occupied blue space that already holds your colour of dice.
    • You may not place dice on a brown occupied space.

    Bonus actions:

    • Take 3 coins using a die (value does not matter)
    • Buy a black die (only once per turn) for 3 camels
    • Bump a die +1/-1 for 2 camels
    • Re-roll a die for 1 camel
    • Fulfill a contract by returning the required goods

    Extra end of game points:

    • 1 VP for every 10 coins left over
    • 7 VP to player with most contracts
    • Score VP from Beijing, depends on position in Beijing
    • Score 1 VP for every 2 resources if you have a trading post in Beijing

    Expert variant:

    • Distribution of City Bonuses: Randomized
    • Distribution of Characters: Randomly shuffle playercount +1, choose one, starting from last player.
    • Distribution of Goal Cards: Deal 4 choose 2 to keep.
