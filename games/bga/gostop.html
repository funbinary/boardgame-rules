    Go-Stop (Hanafuda) - Quick Rules

    Overview

    • Players: Ideally 3 players (2-player variants exist).
    • Deck: Hanafuda deck (48 cards, 12 suits/months).
    • Objective: Be the first to reach a minimum point threshold (usually 3 points) and decide whether to continue ("Go") or end the round ("Stop").

    Gameplay

    On your turn, perform the following steps:

    1. Play from Hand: Match a card from your hand with a card of the same suit (month) on the table. If no match exists, discard it face up.
    2. Draw from Deck: Flip the top card of the draw pile. If it matches a card on the table, you take both.
    3. Collect: Any matches made are placed face up in your scoring area, categorized by type.

    Card Types & Scoring

    • Brights (Gwang - 5 cards): 3 cards = 3 pts (if "Rain-Bright" is included: 2 pts), 4 cards = 4 pts, all 5 cards = 15 pts.
    • Animals (Yul - 9 cards): 5 cards = 1 pt, each additional = +1 pt.
      • Special (Godori): The 3 specific bird cards (Jan, Feb, Aug) together = 5 pts.
    • Ribbons (Ti - 10 cards): 5 ribbons = 1 pt, each additional = +1 pt.
      • Red Ribbons (Hong-dan): 3 red ribbons with writing (Jan, Feb, Mar) = 3 pts.
      • Blue Ribbons (Cheong-dan): 3 blue ribbons (Jun, Sep, Oct) = 3 pts.
      • Grass Ribbons (Cho-dan): 3 red ribbons without writing (Apr, May, Jul) = 3 pts.
    • Junk (Pi - 24+ cards): 10 cards = 1 pt, each additional = +1 pt. (Double-Pi count as 2 cards).

    Go or Stop?

    When you reach at least 3 points (7 points in 2-player games), you must announce:

    • Stop: The round ends immediately, and you win your score.
    • Go: Play continues. You gain bonuses (+1 pt for "1-Go" and "2-Go", score doubling starting at "3-Go"). If you call "Go" and an opponent scores before you "Stop," you lose everything ("Go-bak").

    Strategic Special Rules

    • Puck (Bomb): If you hold 3 cards of a suit in hand and the 4th is on the table, you can "bomb" the suit and take all four. You draw two cards from the deck afterwards.
    • Kiss (Chok): If the card you play from hand and the card you draw from the deck both match the same card on the table, you take all three.
