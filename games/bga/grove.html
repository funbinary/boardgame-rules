    Grove

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Have the most points after collectively discarding 10 cards


    Turn

    On your turn, draw 1 card either blind from the deck or from the faceup discard area

    After which, discard a card to the faceup discard area


    Game End

    The game ends when there are 10 cards in the faceup discard area

    Reveal your final hand

    The number at the top corner of the card is its starting points

    Each card has effects which may gain or lose you additional points

    The player with the most points wins!

    If tied, the tied players each draw 1 extra card blind from the deck and the new score should provide a clear winner
