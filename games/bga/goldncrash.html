    Goldncrash

    Board Game Arena 官方规则文档

    📄 BGA 官方文档

    Overview

    Earn more Gold than your opponent OR destroy all their Zeppelins


    Turn

    On your turn, take 2 actions which can be the same or different

    The actions are: Draw, Play, Discard

    If you draw, take a card into your hand from your personal deck (there is no hand limit)

    If you play, play a card from your hand to one of your zeppelins as long as that zeppelin does NOT have more cards than any other, then use the effect

    If you discard, discard the most recent card from one of your Zeppelins and carry out the discard effect


    Card Effects

    When you play a card, you carry out the effect based on the quantity of that card in that column

    If the only copy, do the effect once

    If second copy, do the effect twice

    If third copy, do the effect three times then discard all the cards attached to that Zeppelin, discard effects are NOT triggered in this way

    If you trigger the Maneuvre effect, this does NOT trigger the play effect


    Esteemed Guest=

    If you meet their criteria, you immediately take as a free action the corresponding Guest and place it on top of all other treasure collected so far

    Any treasure below an Esteemed Guest is protected and can't be messed with


    Game End

    If the final Zeppelin of a player is destroyed, the other player instantly wins!

    If a player empties their deck, resolve the rest of their turn, the opponent gets another full turn, then the player with the most accumulated gold wins!
