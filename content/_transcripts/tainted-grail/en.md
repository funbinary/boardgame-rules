## INTRODUCTION
Tainted Grail is a story-rich survival and exploration game for
Г-Ж players, set in a grim universe that blends Arthurian legends
and Celtic mythology with the uniÂue, dark vision of Awaken
Realms artists and writers.
Each player controls one of four unlikely heroes who must

failed. Chased by the encroaching, twisted power of the wyrd-
ness, fighting an uneven battle against depleting resources and
challenging Encounters, the Characters set out to achieve the

The different Tainted Grail Campaigns are set apart by

problems in different ways, ensuring no two games play alike.
